# The Unseen Crown (Umin)

## Overview

**The Unseen Crown** — called **Umin** in the old tongue — is the umbrella faction of all pathogen powers in the SoMe Universe. It is not a single species but a **coalition of diseases**: bacteria, viruses, fungi, parasites, and the叛变 tumor-cells, each retaining its own culture, command, and naming heritage while marching under one crown.

> **Design analogy**: Umin is to the SoMe Universe what **Halo's Covenant** is to its ring-worlds — a loose theocracy that gathers many "kinds" (here, disease families) beneath a single banner. Each member stays proudly distinct, yet owes allegiance to the whole.

**Seat of power**: Umin holds the **Disease Citadel** — spoken of in-fiction as **"the Unseen Crown's domain."** This is the place where diseases are born; its formal entrance is the **Dark Concourse**, a vaulted hall of black glass under slow dark water, off which the six faction-ways open.

**Enemy**: The **Athena Aegis Accord (AAA)** and the immune defenders of Planet Homo Sapiens.

## Naming Heritage

Umin's units follow the **Axis Powers naming convention** — German (armor/mechanized bacteria), Japanese (Shinto & kamikaze viruses), and Italian (Roman-legacy fungi) — fused with pathology. See [Umin Naming Convention](../Lore/Umin_Naming_Convention.md).

---

## The Disease Citadel

North of the Singularity in the prologue, the Disease Citadel is the player's first encounter with Umin: six banners crown the dark hall, each a recruiter who will trade a secret for an oath. Swear to one (e.g. `CHOOSE VIBRIO`, `CHOOSE DRACULA`, `CHOOSE FEAR`, `CHOOSE ARCHAEOLOGY`, `CHOOSE PALLIDUM`, or `CHOOSE STILLE`) and the concordance opens. See [Disease Citadel](../Locations/Disease_Citadel.md).
