## Overview

The **Rabies Subfaction** is Umin's most feared psychological warfare division. They embody humanity's primal fears — of darkness, of predation, of the corrupted undead. Their aesthetic draws from Gothic horror, their doctrine from the viral infection that inspired vampire legends.

**Core Philosophy**: *"Fear is not a weapon. Fear is the battlefield. We do not fight to win — we fight to make you afraid of winning."*

**Historical Connection**:
Scholars have long noted the striking parallels between traditional vampire folklore and rabies symptoms. The Rabies Subfaction deliberately embraces this connection, weaponizing the neurological disease that may have inspired the Dracula mythos.

## The Vampire-Rabies Connection

### Medical Basis

Rabies is a viral encephalitis transmitted through animal bites (typically dogs, bats, foxes). Once symptoms appear, the disease is **nearly 100% fatal**. The virus attacks the limbic system, causing profound behavioral changes.

**Classic Rabies Symptoms**:
- **Hydrophobia** (fear of water): Spasms of the throat when attempting to drink
- **Photophobia** (fear of light): Extreme sensitivity to light
- **Aggression**: Violent behavior, desire to bite
- **Hypersalivation**: Excessive drooling ("foaming at the mouth")
- **Insomnia**: Inability to sleep
- **Hypersexuality**: In some cases, increased sexual drive

### The Folklore Connection

**Dr. Juan Gómez-Alonso (1998)** proposed that vampire legends may have originated from rabies outbreaks:

| Vampire Trait | Rabies Symptom |
|---------------|----------------|
| Fear of sunlight | Photophobia |
| Fear of water (holy water) | Hydrophobia |
| Biting behavior | Aggression, desire to bite |
| Transmission by bite | Viral transmission via saliva |
| Nocturnal activity | Light sensitivity |
| "Undead" appearance | Wasting, pallor, sleep deprivation |
| Transformation of victim | Progression to symptomatic phase |
| Repulsion by garlic | Hypersensitivity to stimuli |

The Rabies Subfaction transforms this medical hypothesis into doctrine: **We are the original vampires. The legends were warnings.**

## Operational Doctrine

### The Threefold Terror

The Rabies Subfaction's combat doctrine revolves around three primal fears:

#### 1. Fear of Light (Photophobia Warfare)

**Tactical Application**:
- Operatives attack only at night or in enclosed dark spaces
- Use of blinding-flash grenades followed by darkness exploitation
- **"Eternal Night Protocol"**: Sabotage of lighting infrastructure before major operations
- Psychological conditioning of enemy troops to associate light with vulnerability

**Medical Reality**:
Rabies patients experience excruciating pain when exposed to bright light due to inflammation of the meninges and optic nerves.

**Umin Translation**:
> "Your eyes betray you. You cannot fight what you cannot see. But we can see in the dark."

#### 2. Fear of Water (Hydrophobia Warfare)

**Tactical Application**:
- Denial of water supplies to enemy troops
- Contamination of water sources with rabies virus
- **"Thirst Protocol"**: Psychological operations inducing hydrophobic responses in enemy commanders
- Exploitation of enemy's need for water (ambushes at wells, rivers, hydration points)

**Medical Reality**:
Rabies patients experience pharyngeal spasms when attempting to drink, eventually becoming unable to swallow even their own saliva.

**Umin Translation**:
> "Water is life. We are the death that makes you fear life."

#### 3. Fear of the Bite (Transmission Warfare)

**Tactical Application**:
- **Bite Units**: Specialized close-combat operatives trained to break skin and introduce viral payload
- **Saliva Missiles**: Projectile weapons delivering concentrated viral suspension
- **"Conversion Protocol"**: Deliberate infection of captured enemies, then release to spread panic
- **Psychological Impact**: Knowledge that any wound could mean inevitable death

**Medical Reality**:
Rabies transmission occurs when infected saliva enters broken skin, typically through bites. The virus travels along peripheral nerves to the brain at a rate of approximately 12-24mm per day.

**Umin Translation**:
> "One scratch. One drop of saliva. One moment of contact. That is all we need to make you one of us."

## Infection Protocol: The Turning

### Stages of Conversion

**Stage 1: The Bite (Day 0)**
- Virus introduced through wound
- Victim appears normal
- No detectable symptoms
- *Window for PEP (Post-Exposure Prophylaxis): 24-72 hours*

**Stage 2: The Incubation (Days 1-90)**
- Virus travels along nerves toward brain
- Variable duration (1 week to several years in rare cases)
- Victim remains asymptomatic
- *Window for medical intervention closes as virus reaches CNS*

**Stage 3: The Prodrome (Days 90-93)**
- Fever, headache, malaise
- Pain or paresthesia at bite site
- Personality changes
- Insomnia begins
- *Last chance for psychological conditioning before full conversion*

**Stage 4: The Awakening (Days 94-100)**
- Hydrophobia manifests
- Photophobia manifests
- Aggression emerges
- Hypersexuality in some cases
- *Full operative status achieved*

**Stage 5: The Death (Days 100-110)**
- Without intensive life support, death occurs
- Umin maintains "Lazarus Chambers" to extend operational life
- Average operative lifespan post-conversion: 6 months
- Elite operatives (Brides): Up to 5 years with advanced support

### Medical Reality Check

In the real world, once rabies symptoms appear, survival is virtually impossible. Only a handful of documented survivors exist, and most suffered severe permanent brain damage.

Umin's fictional "extension technology" allows operatives to remain functional longer than naturally possible, but even their best medicine cannot cure the infection — only delay death.

**The Tragic Truth**:
Every Rabies Subfaction operative is already dead. They are simply **fighting while dying**.

## Weaknesses & Countermeasures

### Tactical Vulnerabilities

**Light Sensitivity**:
- High-intensity lights can disorient or incapacitate operatives
- UV weapons cause additional damage due to photosensitivity

**Water Access**:
- Denial of processed hydration sources forces operatives into withdrawal
- Forced hydration can trigger disabling pharyngeal spasms

**Limited Lifespan**:
- Every operative has a clock ticking
- Extended operations risk losing experienced units to disease progression

**Bloodlust**:
- Combat behavior can be predicted by understanding viral compulsion
- "Decoy" targets can distract Berserker units

### Medical Countermeasures

**Post-Exposure Prophylaxis (PEP)**:
- Vaccine series + rabies immunoglobulin
- Must be administered within 24-72 hours of exposure
- **Tactical Application**: AAA medical units carry emergency PEP supplies

**Pre-Exposure Vaccination**:
- Standard for all frontline AAA personnel
- Does not prevent infection but buys time for PEP
- Reduces psychological impact of "conversion threat"

**Containment Protocols**:
- Any operative bitten by Rabies unit is isolated immediately
- PEP administered within 6 hours of potential exposure
- Psychological support for victims of "turning" threat

## The Pasteur Countermeasure

### Historical Background

**Louis Pasteur** (1822-1895), the "Father of Microbiology," conducted humanity's most crucial victory against rabies:

**Timeline**:
- **1882**: Pasteur isolated a rabies virus strain from an ox brain
- **1884**: Successful vaccination of dogs demonstrated
- **1885**: First human application — Joseph Meister, a 9-year-old boy bitten by a rabid dog, became the first person saved by the vaccine
- **1889**: Official announcement of rabies vaccine success

**The Breakthrough**:
Pasteur's genius lay in **attenuation** — weakening the virus through serial passage in rabbit spinal cords, then drying the cords to further reduce virulence. This created a safe, effective vaccine from an otherwise 100% fatal disease.

### AAA Countermeasure: The Pasteur Protocol

**Weapon Classification**: MECH (Viral Disease = Mech Class)  
**Designation**: PASTEUR-CLASS VACCINE DISPENSER  
**Codename**: "The Cure"

**The Pasteur Doctrine**:
> "One man found a way to turn death into life. We carry his legacy in every vial."

**Tactical Applications**:

1. **The Meister Protocol** — Emergency field vaccination
   - Deployable within minutes of bite exposure
   - Single-use auto-injector containing attenuated virus
   - 99% efficacy when administered within 24 hours
   - Named after Joseph Meister, the first life saved

2. **The Paris Protocol** — Pre-exposure vaccination
   - Standard inoculation for all AAA personnel
   - Three-dose series over 28 days
   - Named after Pasteur Institute in Paris

3. **The Spinal Cord Protocol** — Mass production
   - Continuous culture of attenuated virus
   - Named after Pasteur's original method using rabbit spinal cords

**Pasteur-Class Units**:

|-----------|-------------|------|

| **Field Hospital** | PASTEUR-H | Emergency PEP administration |

### Historical Echo: Joseph Meister

**The First Survivor**:
Joseph Meister (1876-1940) was the first human successfully vaccinated against rabies. He was bitten 14 times by a rabid dog, and Pasteur's experimental vaccine saved his life.

**Fate**:
Meister later became the caretaker of the Pasteur Institute in Paris. He reportedly committed suicide in 1940 rather than allow Nazi soldiers to desecrate Pasteur's tomb.

**In SoMe Universe**:
Meister is remembered as the **Patron Saint of Survivors**:
- His story is taught to every AAA medical recruit

- The Joseph Meister Medal is awarded to medical personnel who save lives under fire

**AAA Commemoration**:
