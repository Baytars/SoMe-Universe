## POLITICAL STRUCTURE

### Head of State

**Emperor Tiberson of Anstar**

**Title**: Emperor of Anstar, Supreme Commander of the Athena Aegis Accord

**Reign**: Since 2305 (47 years)

**Background**:
- Rose to power during the post-Crimson Winter reconstruction era
- Unified the fractured Lumbar territories through diplomatic and military means
- Principal architect of the Athena Aegis Accord

**Governing Philosophy**:
Tiberson believes in **"Strength Through Unity"** — that continental solidarity is the only viable defense against the Umin threat. He has dedicated his reign to building multinational institutions that outlast any single ruler.

## HISTORY

### Formation

The Empire of Anstar emerged from the chaos following the Crimson Winter, when the fragmented Lumbar territories were united under a single crown. Tiberson, then a regional military commander, proved instrumental in the unification wars and was crowned Emperor in 2305.

### The Aegis Initiative (2312)

Recognizing that no single nation could withstand the Umin threat alone, Tiberson convened the first Continental Summit. The resulting Anstar Declaration established the framework for the Athena Aegis Accord.

**Tiberson's Vision**:
> "I have seen what happens when nations stand alone against the darkness. They fall, one by one, until none remain. We must stand together, or we shall fall together."

### Modern Era

Under Tiberson's leadership, Anstar has become the military backbone of the Athena Aegis Accord, while progressively delegating more authority to multinational institutions — a deliberate strategy to ensure the alliance survives beyond his reign.

## CULTURE & SOCIETY

### National Character

Anstarians value **stability, discipline, and collective effort**. The empire's long history of defensive warfare has produced a culture that prizes fortification, preparation, and strategic patience.

### Capital City: Anstar City

The largest city on the continent, serving as both the imperial capital and the headquarters of the Athena Aegis Accord. The city is known for its monumental architecture, extensive defensive fortifications, and the Grand Council Hall where the Aegis Council convenes.
