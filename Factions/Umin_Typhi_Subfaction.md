## Overview

The **Typhi Subfaction** represents Umin's most sophisticated infiltration capability — not through brute force, but through **perfect mimicry**. They weaponize the asymptomatic carrier phenomenon, creating "Typhoid Mary" operatives who can impersonate high-value targets while the originals are secretly imprisoned.

**Core Philosophy**: *"The perfect spy is one who doesn't know they're a spy. The perfect impostor is one who was never suspected."*

## The Asymptomatic Carrier Doctrine

### Medical Basis

Typhoid fever (*Salmonella Typhi*) is transmitted through contaminated food and water. Approximately **1-6%** of infected individuals become chronic asymptomatic carriers, harboring the bacteria in their gallbladders without showing symptoms. These carriers can shed bacteria for decades.

**Historical Prototype**: Mary Mallon ("Typhoid Mary," 1869-1938)
- First identified asymptomatic carrier in the United States
- Infected approximately 50 people, 3 fatally
- Never showed symptoms herself
- Worked as a cook, inadvertently spreading the disease

### Weaponization

Umin's Typhi Division refined this natural phenomenon into **Project MARY** — a systematic program to:

1. **Identify** asymptomatic carriers with physical similarity to target individuals
2. **Train** them in mimicry, behavior, and memory implantation
3. **Replace** high-value targets (politicians, military leaders, scientists)
4. **Exploit** their access to gather intelligence, sabotage operations, or destabilize organizations

## Project MARY: The Substitution Protocol

### Phase 1: Target Selection

Priority targets for substitution include:
- **Political leaders** with access to classified information
- **Military commanders** controlling troop movements
- **Scientists** involved in weapons development
- **Public figures** with significant influence

**Selection Criteria**:
- High-value access or influence
- Predictable daily routines
- Limited close personal relationships (easier to impersonate)
- Physical compatibility with available carrier templates

### Phase 2: Carrier Preparation

**Template Acquisition**:
Typhi Division maintains a database of asymptomatic carriers sorted by physical characteristics. When a target is selected, they search for carriers with matching:
- Height and build
- Hair color and texture
- Skin tone
- Voice characteristics
- Approximate age

**Behavioral Training**:
- Intensive study of target's mannerisms, speech patterns, and habits
- Memory implantation through neural conditioning
- Cover story preparation (explanations for any behavioral changes)

**Medical Modification**:
- Surgical adjustments for closer physical match
- Hormonal treatments to alter voice, muscle mass, or other characteristics
- Gene therapy for superficial phenotypic changes

### Phase 3: Extraction & Substitution

**The "Cradle Protocol"**:

1. **Abduction**: Target is secretly captured during a routine activity (travel, sleep, medical visit)
2. **Imprisonment**: Original is held in a secure Typhi facility, kept alive for continued memory extraction and as leverage
3. **Insertion**: Carrier operative assumes target's identity
4. **Verification**: Operative undergoes routine interactions to confirm successful infiltration

**Timeline**:
- Average substitution operation: 72 hours
- Maximum acceptable absence before suspicion: 96 hours
- Memory extraction frequency: Weekly (to update operative with recent events)

### Phase 4: Activation

Once inserted, the "Mary" operative can:
- **Gather Intelligence**: Access classified materials, attend confidential meetings
- **Sow Discord**: Make controversial statements, issue contradictory orders
- **Sabotage Operations**: Redirect resources, delay critical decisions
- **Recruit Others**: Identify additional substitution targets

### The Sabotage Campaign

During her 68 days as "Yukio," MARY-07 executed a systematic destabilization campaign:

**Seduction & Manipulation**:
- **12 high-ranking officials** engaged in romantic/sexual relationships
- **4 rival factions** created within the bureaucracy
- **23 classified documents** leaked to Umin intelligence
- **3 military operations** delayed due to fabricated "medical concerns"

**Psychological Operations**:
- Played officials against each other using jealousy and rivalry
- Spread rumors about planned purges and reassignments
- Issued contradictory medical advisories to create confusion
- Used "Yukio's" perceived innocence to avoid suspicion

**Bureaucratic Sabotage**:
- Blocked promotions of competent officers (replaced with incompetent loyalists)
- Redirected medical supplies to "priority" (actually useless) projects
- Created friction between Foo (Medical Chancellor) and other ministers

**Estimated Damage Assessment**:
> The 68-day infiltration caused approximately **₳47 million** in direct economic losses and **irreparable damage** to 14 careers. More significantly, trust within the Alopes government was severely compromised. Officials who had vied for "Yukio's" favor were humiliated, and those who had trusted her judgment questioned their own competence. The psychological impact may take years to fully assess.

- Dietary preferences changed (favorite foods rejected)

**Raid & Rescue**:

3. Yukio found in suspended animation, physically healthy but traumatized

## Historical Context: The Real Typhoid Mary

Mary Mallon (1869-1938) was the first person in the United States identified as an asymptomatic carrier of typhoid fever. She worked as a cook and infected approximately 50 people over her career, 3 of whom died. She never showed symptoms and refused to believe she was contagious. She was forcibly quarantined twice, spending a total of 26 years in isolation.
