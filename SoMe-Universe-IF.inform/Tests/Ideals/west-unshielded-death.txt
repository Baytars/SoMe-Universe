Singularity
You are in the Singularity. The universe is beginning to explode.

WHO YOU ARE: You are the first traveler - a mind awake at the birth of the cosmos, before any side has claimed you. Ten powers want your oath; your task is to choose one and then walk every other.

HOW TO PLAY: Type a direction alone - N, S, E, W - to move. LOOK re-reads a room; EXAMINE <thing> or LOOK AT <thing> studies something; ASK <someone> ABOUT <topic> questions a character.

THE FIRST STEP: Two gates stand open - SOUTH to the Hall of Medicine, NORTH to the Disease Citadel - the Unseen Crown's domain. Walk to one (just type N or S). Inside, each faction keeps a recruiter; when one wins you over, swear with CHOOSE <patron> - e.g. CHOOSE CRADLE or CHOOSE VIBRIO.

Only after you have sworn can you cross the cosmos by concordance (GO TO <place>); until then GO TO is refused. Three roads leave the newborn light: south to Medicine, north to the Disease Citadel (the Unseen Crown's domain), and west - where the land glows wrong - belongs to the Old Metals. Type HELP any time to see this again.

>You have walked into the Unseen Crown's domain. Six banners crown the dark hall, and under each waits a recruiter who wants your oath:

   a plain-clothes veteran Archaea (the Archaeology Division), a containment supervisor with a drone on her shoulder (Fear Technology), a sunflower muse (the Pale Masquerade), a pale Bride of Count Dracula (the Undead Court), a personnel officer (the Department of Silence), and a gum-chewing girl in a sailor suit (the Abyssal Legion).

Court any of them before you swear - each hides a secret, if you ask. When one wins you over: CHOOSE VIBRIO, CHOOSE DRACULA, CHOOSE FEAR, CHOOSE ARCHAEOLOGY, CHOOSE PALLIDUM, or CHOOSE STILLE. For example: ASK THE GIRL ABOUT JOINING.

Pilgrim Turn
The road west forks at a shrine of white lead paint and surgical steel: a wayside chapel where the Radiology clergy keep their vigil. Beyond it the land turns strange - vitrified glass underfoot, the horizon glowing faintly, the light of the Black Cathedral catching wrong on the dust. A stone placard reads, in three languages: TURN BACK, OR COME ARMED.

A priest of the Radiology Order bars the west road - lead-lined vestments, a collimator staff, eyes that have watched tumors die and patients glow. His hands under the lead gloves are not quite hands anymore: the borrowed light has been settling into him for decades.

>The Radiologist's staff comes down across the road like a barrier dropping. "Stop. Hear the whole sentence before you spend your body.

"You want the gods. I understand - I serve their light, and I have watched it take tumors that no knife could reach. But radiology is a borrowed fire: we lead-line our priests, we dose our patients like artillery officers, and still the light takes its tax from us. You would walk into the Cathedral wearing nothing. That is not pilgrimage; that is donation.

"There is a road to worthiness. Aukin teaches it: the radiology curriculum, year on year - chelation weaves, lead habits, the grammar of shielding. Their Aegis gauntlets are the first lesson made wearable. Earn the gauntlets, wear them here, and I will not only step aside - I will walk you to the door.

"The gate to Aukin lies north of the port. CHOOSE CRADLE, if the Cradle will have you; otherwise buy its gauntlets at the Port Market and come back. The road west has no third option."

>The Radiologist does not lower his staff - but he steps aside, and the grief in his face is worse than the barrier was. "Stubbornness is also a kind of faith, and I have buried better stubbornness than yours. The Wastes will judge you in steps, not in words. Walk, then. Walk, and count."

Contaminated Wastes
Wind moves dust that never settles - every grain older than the first cell. Warning stones mark the old mills and the old mines; somewhere under the horizon, a radium afterglow pulses like a slow heart. A road of vitrified glass runs on west, toward a hall that catches the light and returns it wrong.

The afterglow finds you at once - a warmth in the teeth, a taste like a struck coin. The Old Metals" tally reads 1: a warning, not yet a sentence. The Cathedral is still west - walk, and count.

>At the Cathedral's threshold the borrowed light comes due all at once - the vitrified road behind you glows with your own afterimage, and the door keeps. The Old Metals take their tax at the step, not in the hall.
