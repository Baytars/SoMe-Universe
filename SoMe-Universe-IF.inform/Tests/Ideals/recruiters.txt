Singularity
You are in the Singularity. The universe is beginning to explode.

Two gates swirl in the newborn light: the Hall of Medicine to the south, the Disease Citadel to the north. Ten powers wait behind them for the first envoy to walk the war.

>You have walked into the Unseen Crown's domain. Six banners crown the dark hall, and under each waits a recruiter who wants your oath:

   a field archaeologist (the Archaeology Division), the Director with a drone on her shoulder (Fear Technology), a sunflower muse (the Pale Masquerade), a pale Bride of Count Dracula (the Undead Court), a personnel officer (the Department of Silence), and a gum-chewing girl in a sailor suit (the Abyssal Legion).

Court any of them before you swear - each hides a secret, if you ask. When one wins you over: CHOOSE VIBRIO, CHOOSE DRACULA, CHOOSE FEAR, CHOOSE ARCHAEOLOGY, CHOOSE PALLIDUM, or CHOOSE STILLE. For example: ASK THE BOSUN ABOUT JOINING.

>She pops her gum and throws you a lazy salute. "Welcome aboard, envoy - or not. Your call." She jerks a thumb at the tank. "That is an armored segment of the Cholera Octopus - trophy, museum piece, cannons cold. The ship herself is four thousand meters down: fifty meters of metal-biological armor, eight tentacles, eight hydro-cannons, one mind, and she is the polite part of our fleet. Four thousand meters below the waves our fortress runs drill-factories and geothermal stations; your maps spell the place Atlantis. People hear that and think, that is a lot of ocean. They are wrong. It is exactly enough ocean."

>She peers at your face for the flicker of a smile - and finds none. "...Okay. You want the real thing. Can't fire the trophy, envoy - it is the business end of a fifty-meter warship, and this hall has nice floors." Two fingers, a whistle - high, ultrasonic, felt in the teeth rather than heard. The water in the column goes still, then clears to impossible depth: a live window. Far below, lit by its own blue-green, the flagship's real tentacle swings a bore onto a hulk of Accord armor at four thousand meters and fires. No shell: a jet past its critical point; the hulk fails inward, cavitation collapsing at ten thousand degrees, micro-jets chewing armor like paper. The sound arrives seconds later as a soft knock on the glass. "Critical-pressure hydraulic cannon," she says, and for one second the cheerful is gone from her voice. "It fires water, envoy. Water. There is no counter except distance - and the distance is where we live." The smile returns like a mask going back on. "Gum?"

>She snaps a salute that suddenly has not one gram of laziness in it, and behind her the column clears to a live view of the abyss - the flagship's cannon-bores sealing like sleeping eyes. "Water is life, water is death," she says. "Take the water away and we are a museum exhibit - so we never let it be taken. Welcome to the water, envoy." You swear to the Abyssal Legion.


The war arranges itself around your oath. Somewhere on neutral waters, a Registrar adds a name to a ledger and begins cataloguing the conflict: the Ten Banners campaign.
An Accord manifest appears in your satchel.
(Type BANNERS at any time to review the campaign.)

The Singularity seals itself behind the Big Bang. The gates open. A launch skiff carries you to neutral waters.

The circuit of the Ten Banners begins.

Free Port Landing
The landing stage of the Pericardium Free Port, the one place the war may not touch: tariffs fund the Cradle, every flag may dock, and no act of war may be committed within the pericardial sac. The Registrar keeps the standards here, under Accord charter, where every faction's oath-binding is neutral ground. Go to any banner's hall from this deck - the port's manifests know them all.

On the chart table is a Rationality Protocol pamphlet.

The Registrar stands by the ten standards with a ledger under one arm, cataloguing the war before it happens, on neutral ground no faction dare violate.


HOW TO TRAVEL: no compass needed at this hub. List every destination:
      ASK REGISTRAR ABOUT DESTINATIONS
then jump straight there:
      GO TO UNDEAD COURT      or      GO TO MARKET      (GO TO LANDING brings you home.)
Carry every won seal home and REPORT to raise the standards.

HOW TO WALK: directions still work where roads exist - type N / S / and so on to walk a real road instead of using the concordance.

>The manifest takes your word, and the concordance carries you along the great vessels of the war.

Cradle Outer Court
The outer court of the Cradle, the Medulla: whitewashed cloisters where cadets from every nation sit cross-legged among signal-lattices, learning to see the body's own presentation before they are permitted to fight anything at all. Stairs rise to the Inner Court; a road runs east toward the sound of surf and money.
