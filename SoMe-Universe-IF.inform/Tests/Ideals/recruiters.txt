Singularity
You are in the Singularity. The universe is beginning to explode.

Two gates swirl in the newborn light: the Hall of Medicine to the south, the Disease Citadel to the north. Ten powers wait behind them for the first envoy to walk the war.

>You have walked into the Unseen Crown's domain. Six banners crown the dark hall, and under each waits a recruiter who wants your oath:

   a field archaeologist (the Archaeology Division), the Director with a drone on her shoulder (Fear Technology), a sunflower muse (the Pale Masquerade), a pale Bride of Count Dracula (the Undead Court), a personnel officer (the Department of Silence), and the Cholera Octopus heart-core floating in its tank (the Abyssal Legion).

Court any of them before you swear - each hides a secret, if you ask. When one wins you over: CHOOSE VIBRIO, CHOOSE DRACULA, CHOOSE FEAR, CHOOSE ARCHAEOLOGY, CHOOSE PALLIDUM, or CHOOSE STILLE. For example: ASK THE BOSUN ABOUT JOINING.

>The tank brightens; the limb drifts closer, and the glass fogs from the inside. "You stand before the heart-core of the Cholera Octopus, envoy - fifty meters of metal-biological armor, eight tentacles, eight hydro-cannons, one mind. Below the shallow scouts and the mid-depth berths our abyssal fortress descends four thousand meters: drill-factories, geothermal stations, a city your maps spell Atlantis. We do not board ships. We flood the field, boil it, and own the sea it stood on. Join the Legion, and the ocean becomes a weapon with your hand on the valve."

>A tentacle bore dilates against the glass - a mouth within the mouth. "A secret, freely shown. Our cannon does not fire shells. It fires water driven past its own critical point: the jet is no longer liquid, the pressure is four hundred atmospheres past steel, and where it lands the cavitation bubbles collapse at ten thousand degrees. Your Accord armor fails from the inside out. There is no counter except distance - and we own the distance."

>The bioluminescence dims, deliberately. "You are fishing for weaknesses. Honest, then: heat cooks us, chlorine unmakes our hide, drain the water and we are a museum exhibit. Now weigh that against the price of getting close enough to try. The Legion prints its vulnerabilities in its own doctrine, envoy - because by the time you reach them, you are already standing in our ocean."

>The heart-core dims its tank to abyssal black, and the eight cannon-bores seal like eyes going to sleep. "Water is life, water is death," it says. "Take the water away and we are a museum exhibit - so we never let it be taken. Welcome to the water, envoy." You swear to the Abyssal Legion.


The war arranges itself around your oath. Somewhere on neutral waters, a Registrar adds a name to a ledger and begins cataloguing the conflict: the Ten Banners campaign.
An Accord manifest appears in your satchel.
(Type BANNERS at any time to review the campaign.)

The Singularity seals itself behind the Big Bang. The gates open. A launch skiff carries you to neutral waters.

The circuit of the Ten Banners begins.

Free Port Landing
The landing stage of the Pericardium Free Port, the one place the war may not touch: tariffs fund the Cradle, every flag may dock, and no act of war may be committed within the pericardial sac. The Registrar keeps the standards here, under Accord charter, where every faction's oath-binding is neutral ground. Go to any banner's hall from this deck - the port's manifests know them all.

On the chart table is a Rationality Protocol pamphlet.

The Registrar stands by the ten standards with a ledger under one arm, cataloguing the war before it happens, on neutral ground no faction dare violate.


HOW TO TRAVEL: no compass needed at this hub. List every destination:
      ASK REGISTRAR ABOUT DESTINATIONS
then jump straight there:
      GO TO UNDEAD COURT      or      GO TO MARKET      (GO TO LANDING brings you home.)
Carry every won seal home and REPORT to raise the standards.

HOW TO WALK: directions still work where roads exist - type N / S / and so on to walk a real road instead of using the concordance.

>The manifest takes your word, and the concordance carries you along the great vessels of the war.

Stairs rise to the Inner Court; a road runs east toward the sound of surf and money.
