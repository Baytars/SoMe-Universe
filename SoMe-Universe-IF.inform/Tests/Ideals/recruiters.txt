Singularity
You are in the Singularity. The universe is beginning to explode.

Two gates swirl in the newborn light: the Hall of Medicine to the south, the Disease Citadel to the north. Ten powers wait behind them for the first envoy to walk the war.

>You have walked into the Unseen Crown's domain. Six banners crown the dark hall, and under each waits a recruiter who wants your oath:

   a resurrected Archaea riding a wheeled pressurized vat (the Archaeology Division), a containment supervisor with a drone on her shoulder (Fear Technology), a sunflower muse (the Pale Masquerade), a pale Bride of Count Dracula (the Undead Court), a personnel officer (the Department of Silence), and a gum-chewing girl in a sailor suit (the Abyssal Legion).

Court any of them before you swear - each hides a secret, if you ask. When one wins you over: CHOOSE VIBRIO, CHOOSE DRACULA, CHOOSE FEAR, CHOOSE ARCHAEOLOGY, CHOOSE PALLIDUM, or CHOOSE STILLE. For example: ASK THE BOSUN ABOUT JOINING.

>The mat-thing changes height without preamble - knee-high, then at your eye - and the air around you goes ammonia-bright and warm. "Before your oxygen, envoy, we were. Your kind files us under extremophile - a word meaning life that embarrasses your chemistry." A single filament separates from the mat and hangs in the air before your eyes, purple membrane patches blooming and re-rippling along it in real time. "Watch: reverse gyrase. Fever cannot unravel my letters. Acidity of pH one is my bath. One hundred thirteen degrees is my afternoon. Your Division digs for us the way the old fiction's covenant dug for its forerunners - everything you call technology, we call metabolism. Join, and dig us up. Wake more of us. We teach what oxygen forgot."

>The membrane flushes violet - the archaeal smile. One filament extends, slow, and touches the back of your hand: warm, mineral, faintly electric. "A secret, envoy, kept in your own body: you never stopped hosting us. Methanogens ride your gut to this day; every quiet bubble of methane you have ever produced was signed by my lineage. You did not discover the ancestors. You merely stopped reading the receipt." The filament withdraws. "My kin in the Division's vaults are viable and patient. When you join, you will help wake them - and they will surface from their vats the way I surface from mine."

>It presses one edge of itself flat against the chart-table, and where it touches, the wood hisses - the wood is being eaten, politely, at the pace of geology. "Your Accord levels our dig sites and calls it containment. We re-dig them twice as well in the dark. The catalogue of what waits: sulfur metabolism for the oxygen-poor war. Halophilic armor for the salt flats. Taq polymerase and reverse gyrase so fever cannot edit us. Methanogenesis to fight in the anaerobic gut of a continent. The Division does not worship the ancestors, envoy. It does the paperwork of waking them - and the paperwork is learning to walk."

>The Division Chief taps the tile motto. "In the beginning, there was sulfur. We are not inventing, envoy - we are remembering." You swear to the Archaeology Division.


The war arranges itself around your oath. Somewhere on neutral waters, a Registrar adds a name to a ledger and begins cataloguing the conflict: the Ten Banners campaign.
An Accord manifest appears in your satchel.
(Type BANNERS at any time to review the campaign.)

The Singularity seals itself behind the Big Bang. The gates open. A launch skiff carries you to neutral waters.

The circuit of the Ten Banners begins.

Free Port Landing
The landing stage of the Pericardium Free Port, the one place the war may not touch: tariffs fund the Cradle, every flag may dock, and no act of war may be committed within the pericardial sac. The Registrar keeps the standards here, under Accord charter, where every faction's oath-binding is neutral ground. Go to any banner's hall from this deck - the port's manifests know them all.

On the chart table is a Rationality Protocol pamphlet.

The Registrar stands by the ten standards with a ledger under one arm, cataloguing the war before it happens, on neutral ground no faction dare violate.


HOW TO TRAVEL: no compass needed at this hub. List every destination:
      ASK REGISTRAR ABOUT DESTINATIONS
then jump straight there:
      GO TO UNDEAD COURT      or      GO TO MARKET      (GO TO LANDING brings you home.)
Carry every won seal home and REPORT to raise the standards.

HOW TO WALK: directions still work where roads exist - type N / S / and so on to walk a real road instead of using the concordance.

>The manifest takes your word, and the concordance carries you along the great vessels of the war.
