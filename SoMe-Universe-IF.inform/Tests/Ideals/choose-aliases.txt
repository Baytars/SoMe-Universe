Singularity
You are in the Singularity. The universe is beginning to explode.

WHO YOU ARE: You are the first traveler - a mind awake at the birth of the cosmos, before any side has claimed you. Ten powers want your oath; your task is to choose one and then walk every other.

HOW TO PLAY: Type a direction alone - N, S, E, W - to move. LOOK re-reads a room; EXAMINE <thing> or LOOK AT <thing> studies something; ASK <someone> ABOUT <topic> questions a character.

THE FIRST STEP: Two gates stand open - SOUTH to the Hall of Medicine, NORTH to the Disease Citadel - the Unseen Crown's domain. Walk to one (just type N or S). Inside, each faction keeps a recruiter; when one wins you over, swear with CHOOSE <patron> - e.g. CHOOSE CRADLE or CHOOSE VIBRIO.

Only after you have sworn can you cross the cosmos by concordance (GO TO <place>); until then GO TO is refused. Three roads leave the newborn light: south to Medicine, north to the Disease Citadel (the Unseen Crown's domain), and west - where the land glows wrong - belongs to the Old Metals. Type HELP any time to see this again.

>You have walked into the Unseen Crown's domain. Six banners crown the dark hall, and under each waits a recruiter who wants your oath:

   a plain-clothes veteran Archaea (the Archaeology Division), a containment supervisor with a drone on her shoulder (Fear Technology), a sunflower muse (the Pale Masquerade), a pale Bride of Count Dracula (the Undead Court), a personnel officer (the Department of Silence), and a gum-chewing girl in a sailor suit (the Abyssal Legion).

Court any of them before you swear - each hides a secret, if you ask. When one wins you over: CHOOSE VIBRIO, CHOOSE DRACULA, CHOOSE FEAR, CHOOSE ARCHAEOLOGY, CHOOSE PALLIDUM, or CHOOSE STILLE. For example: ASK THE GIRL ABOUT JOINING.

>The Count regards you across the dry fountains. "Fear is not a weapon. Fear is the battlefield," he says. "Welcome to the field." You swear to the Undead Court.


The war arranges itself around your oath - and the Keeper of the Gate watches you like a woman who has just seen the weather change.
The Keeper's road-map appears in your satchel.
(Type BANNERS at any time to review the campaign.)

The Singularity seals itself behind the Big Bang. The gates open. A launch skiff carries you to neutral waters.

The circuit of the Ten Banners begins.

Free Port Landing
The landing stage of the Pericardium Free Port, the one place the war may not touch: tariffs fund the Cradle, every flag may dock, and no act of war may be committed within the pericardial sac. The Keeper keeps the standards here, on the one ground every faction still respects - where oaths bind and the war holds its breath. Go to any banner's hall from this deck - the port's manifests know them all.

On the chart table is a Rationality Protocol pamphlet.

A woman in travel-grey stands at the standards, and she is already speaking as you arrive - not at you, exactly, but near you, the way weather speaks. "Awake, are you? Good. The banners have been waiting for someone who could still choose."


HOW TO TRAVEL: no compass needed at this hub. List every destination:
      ASK KEEPER ABOUT DESTINATIONS
then jump straight there:
      GO TO UNDEAD COURT      (GO TO LANDING brings you home.)
Carry every won seal home and REPORT to raise the standards.

HOW TO WALK: directions still work where roads exist - type N / S / and so on to walk a real road instead of using the concordance.

YOUR ROAD: ten halls, ten seals, and every faction watching you choose. The Keeper raises a standard for each seal you bring home - type BANNERS to see how far you have come, REPORT to raise what you carry. No one has ever walked all ten halls and lived to be welcome in all of them. When the tenth standard flies, you will be the first - and you will finally see the whole war.
