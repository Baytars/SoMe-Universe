Singularity
You are in the Singularity. The universe is beginning to explode.

WHO YOU ARE: You are the first traveler - a mind awake at the birth of the cosmos, before any side has claimed you. Ten powers want your oath; your task is to choose one and then walk every other.

HOW TO PLAY: Type a direction alone - N, S, E, W - to move. LOOK re-reads a room; EXAMINE <thing> or LOOK AT <thing> studies something; ASK <someone> ABOUT <topic> questions a character.

THE FIRST STEP: Two gates stand open - SOUTH to the Hall of Medicine, NORTH to the Disease Citadel - the Unseen Crown's domain. Walk to one (just type N or S). Inside, each faction keeps a recruiter; when one wins you over, swear with CHOOSE <patron> - e.g. CHOOSE CRADLE or CHOOSE VIBRIO.

Only after you have sworn can you cross the cosmos by concordance (GO TO <place>); until then GO TO is refused. Three roads leave the newborn light: south to Medicine, north to the Disease Citadel (the Unseen Crown's domain), and west - where the land glows wrong - belongs to the Old Metals. Type HELP any time to see this again.

>You have walked into the Hall of Medicine, where the healing powers of the Accord keep their banners - and under each waits a recruiter who wants your oath:

   a medullary preceptor (the Cradle of Aukin - sworn agent of the Heavy Metal Pantheon), a fleet navigator (the Celestial Corps), and a quiet keeper (the Lab Martyrs).

Court any of them before you swear - each hides a secret, if you ask. When one wins you over: CHOOSE CRADLE, CHOOSE CELESTIAL, or CHOOSE MARTYRS. The Heavy Metal Pantheon keeps no banner in this hall - the Cradle keeps its interest as agent, and the Old Metals themselves answer only at their own door, west of the Wastes. For example: ASK THE PRECEPTOR ABOUT JOINING.

>The Generalizer lifts the honey-gold AIRE flame. "Then learn this first, traveler: the stricter the definition of self, the more of the self becomes unrecognizable." You swear to the Cradle.


The war arranges itself around your oath - and the Keeper of the Gate watches you like a woman who has just seen the weather change.
The Keeper's road-map appears in your satchel.
(Sworn is not won. GO TO each faction's hall, complete its trial, and earn all ten seals - then REPORT. Type BANNERS to track them; ASK a faction's representative ABOUT SEAL to learn its trial.)

The Singularity seals itself behind the Big Bang. The gates open. A launch skiff carries you to neutral waters.

The circuit of the Ten Banners begins.

Free Port Landing
The landing stage of the Pericardium Free Port, the one place the war may not touch: tariffs fund the Cradle, every flag may dock, and no act of war may be committed within the pericardial sac. The Keeper keeps the standards here, on the one ground every faction still respects - where oaths bind and the war holds its breath. Go to any banner's hall from this deck - the port's manifests know them all.

On the chart table is a Rationality Protocol pamphlet.

A woman in travel-grey stands at the standards, and she is already speaking as you arrive - not at you, exactly, but near you, the way weather speaks. "Awake, are you? Good. The banners have been waiting for someone who could still choose."


HOW TO TRAVEL: no compass needed at this hub. List every destination:
      ASK KEEPER ABOUT DESTINATIONS
then jump straight there:
      GO TO UNDEAD COURT      (GO TO LANDING brings you home.)
Carry every won seal home and REPORT to raise the standards.

HOW TO WALK: directions still work where roads exist - type N / S / and so on to walk a real road instead of using the concordance.

YOUR ROAD: ten halls, ten seals, and every faction watching you choose. The Keeper raises a standard for each seal you bring home - type BANNERS to see how far you have come, REPORT to raise what you carry. No one has ever walked all ten halls and lived to be welcome in all of them. When the tenth standard flies, you will be the first - and you will finally see the whole war.

>The Keeper keeps no trial to disclose.

>The manifest takes your word, and the concordance carries you along the great vessels of the war.

Shrine of the Knitting Mouse
A small bronze mouse in pince-nez glasses sits knitting a double helix - Z-DNA, left-handed, the still-unknown strand. Four lampposts ring the shrine, each worked with cell division in bronze. The altar before the monument carries offerings: strands, treats, wheels, slides. Laika's ghost-shape patrols the treeline. HeLa's soft luminosity sits by the altar like a candle that never gutters.

HeLa sits by the altar in a soft luminosity - the immortal navigator, first of the cell lines, still dividing, still serving.

Laika patrols the treeline in a slow, patient circle - the first of many to see the Earth from above, still on watch at the boundary between life and void.

Fabrica Bridge
The flagship's bridge: seven interconnecting chambers, one for each book of the Fabrica. Vesalius holds the chart-table at the centre. Harvey stands by a model of the Pulse, its loop closed with no dead ends. Bethune, in scarlet, checks a transfusion kit by the viewport. An anatomical star chart hangs on the after wall.

Vesalius, Admiral of the Celestial Fleet, stands with the posture of a man who corrected two hundred errors and expects to correct two hundred more.

Harvey, Commodore of the Blood Sea Fleet, stands by a model of the Pulse - a vessel shaped as a closed loop with no dead ends.

Bethune, in a scarlet uniform that is not regulation and does not intend to be, checks a mobile transfusion kit by the viewport.

Blood Court
The court of the Undead: a long dry hall where fountains stand as architecture only, running nothing. Dracula holds the far end like a fixed point the room is measured from. Three Brides flank him - Budapest, Bucharest, Belgrade - and the gallery behind is walled in glass, with what is preserved in the glass better left undescribed. Every surface is dark, dry, and exactly 0.1 lux.

Count Dracula stands at the far end of the dry hall. He does not so much hold the room as permit it to continue.

The Budapest Bride watches the stair, unhurried - black hair pinned with a single jade pin, the kind of stillness that makes you aware of your own pulse, which is, of course, the point.

The Bucharest Bride stands where the gallery light ends, and the dark approves of her: dark hair, darker eyes, the rose-and-ash coloring that launches a thousand warning poems.

The Belgrade Bride has a physician's hands - pale, precise, kept folded like an instrument tray - and the tired, luminous beauty of a portrait by someone who only painted grief.

>In the Blood Court: ASK Count Dracula about PASTEUR or MEISTER, then ASK each of his three brides - Budapest, Bucharest, Belgrade - about their PAST. Only then SHOW the Pasteur vial (bought from the Cradle's factor, one token) to the Count, and he will mark you a witness, not a courier.
>The manifest takes your word, and the concordance carries you along the great vessels of the war.

Abteilung Stille Office
A quiet office of quiet people: green-shaded lamps, gall-coloured wax, and a wall of dossiers on people who are not there any more. Two quartermasters work duplicate desks under the lamps - the young one and the stern one, and at this distance they are difficult to tell apart. The Stille Amanuensis presides from a raised desk.

The Stille Amanuensis runs the intelligence analysis of Abteilung Stille from a raised desk, stamping gall-coloured wax with metronomic calm.

The young quartermaster catalogs requisitions with a slight smile, as if the requisitions were jokes only he got.

The stern quartermaster catalogs the same requisitions with no smile at all, in the same rhythm, at the same desk-style, in the same uniform.

>In the Stille office: READ THE DOSSIERS WALL to take the MARY-07 folder, read its three enclosures, then NAME two flaws to the Amanuensis - DIET, HAND, WARMTH, SLEEP or TIMING. Only then will she hear your ACCUSATION of the stern quartermaster.

Abyssal Deck
A floodable landing deck under sodium lights: the navy of the Unseen Crown, keel-anchored in shallow artificial sea. The flagship rides at the centre - the Cholera Octopus, fifty meters of metal and arm, each of eight tentacles cradling a cannon-bore. The Octopus Admiral walks the wet deck as if salinity were a rank.

The Octopus Admiral walks the wet deck as if salinity were a rank, eight grease-marks of hydro-cannon oil on her sleeve.
