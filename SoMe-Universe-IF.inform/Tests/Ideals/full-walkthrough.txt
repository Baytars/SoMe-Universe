Singularity

WHO YOU ARE: You are the first traveler - a mind awake at the birth of the cosmos, before any side has claimed you. Ten powers want your oath; your task is to choose one and then walk every other.

HOW TO PLAY: Type a direction alone - N, S, E, W - to move. LOOK re-reads a room; EXAMINE <thing> or LOOK AT <thing> studies something; ASK <someone> ABOUT <topic> questions a character.

Three roads leave the newborn light: south to Medicine, north to the Disease Citadel (the Unseen Crown's domain), and west - where the land glows wrong - belongs to the Old Metals.
