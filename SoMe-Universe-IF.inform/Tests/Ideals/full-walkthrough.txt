>The preceptors walk the outer doctrine first. CORTICAL SELECTION: cadets prove they can see the body's signal at all. Those who cannot read the presenting lattice are released back into circulation - unharmed, ungraded, and dangerous to no one but themselves.

>Then the inner doctrine. MEDULLARY SELECTION: the AIRE-lit library of every tissue the body owns. Strong binders of self are deleted; agonistic binders go to the Treg Cloister, to return as suppressors of friendly fire rather than as fighters.
