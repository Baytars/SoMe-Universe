# SoMe Universe

**Saga of Medicine** — A medical sci-fi game universe where human anatomy becomes a cosmic battlefield.

## Overview

In the world of SoMe, the human body is reimagined as a vast universe. **Planet Homo Sapiens** serves as the primary stage, where organs become nations, blood vessels transform into highways, and the immune system stands as the defender against an endless tide of pathogenic invaders.

Players take on the roles of anthropomorphized cells—Red Blood Cells, White Blood Cells, T-Cells, Stem Cells, and more—each with unique abilities and career paths. The enemy? **The Unseen Crown (Umin)**: a coalition of pathogens—bacteria, viruses, fungi, parasites, and cancer cells—each faction drawing inspiration from real-world history and military doctrine.

## Key Concepts

### Medical History as Weapon System
Characters are named after pioneers of medicine, with their "weapon class" determined by the scale of disease they fought:
- **John Snow** → Tank (cholera, a bacterial disease)
- **Ignaz Semmelweis** → Tank (puerperal fever, streptococcal)
- **Edward Jenner** → Mech (smallpox, a viral disease)
- **Fritz Lickint** → Carrier (lung cancer, tumor)

### Real Science, Fictional Form
- Antibiotic ammunition follows real pharmacological mechanisms (β-lactams inhibit cell wall synthesis)
- Pathogen evolution includes horizontal gene transfer mechanisms (conjugation, transduction, transformation)
- Environmental hazards reflect actual disease pathology

### Fear as a Weapon System
Umin's Fear Technology Division creates supernatural constructs (ghosts, zombies, Slender Man) to exploit human superstition and distrust of science.
