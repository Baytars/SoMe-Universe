- **Official Name:** Empire of Anstar
- **Chinese Name:** 安星帝国

## 3. GOVERNMENT & POLITICS

- **Regime type:** Hereditary empire
- **Founder/Sovereign:** Emperor **Tiberson** convened the first Continental Summit (2312) and signed the Anstar Declaration
- **Supreme Commander (AAA):** **Tiberson of Anstar** — strategic command authority *(see Open Item: tenure/dynasty)*
- **Diplomatic service:** supplies officers to external postings; historically the largest source of seconded **Foreign Selves** instructors to the Cradle (e.g. Vasud Stuart)

**Political role:** Anstar is the AAA's **military and political hegemon** — largest military contributor, host of headquarters, holder of the Supreme Commander post, strongest influence in Council votes. Its position in the internal debate leans to **military deterrence**, in tension with Alopes's prevention-first stance.

## 5. MEDICAL-DOCTRINAL POSTURE

- Favours credible deterrence and forward basing; sponsor of permanent forward operating bases after 2340.
- Nonetheless fully integrated into AAA medical standards (built largely on Alopesian doctrine).
- Anstarian intelligence is implicated in the **DOPPELGÄNGER** scandal through former Chair Vasud Stuart; the foreign ministry pensioned him into Lower Intestinal exile rather than expose the case — an institutional sore point.

## 7. HISTORY

- **2312 — The Anstar Initiative:** in response to escalating Umin incursions on the Lumbar–Sacrum border, Emperor Tiberson convenes the first Continental Summit; six nations sign the Anstar Declaration.
- **2315 — Operation *Shield of Anstar*:** with Cervia, contains an early Umin probe.
- **2318–2340 — Expansion:** Anstar oversees the cervical and thoracic accessions as the alliance grows from six to fourteen.
- **2337 — *Lumbar Defense*:** eight member states repel a coordinated Umin offensive.
- **2343 — DOPPELGÄNGER aftermath:** the scandal (Anstar-linked Chair compromised) strains but does not break the Cradle arrangement and cools ties with Alopes for ~18 months.
- **2340–present — Umin escalation:** permanent forward bases and an expanded Rapid Response Corps.

---

## 8. RELATIONS

- **Duchy of Alopes:** central axis of the AAA — Anstar the sword, Alopes the medicine. Ongoing good-natured rivalry over deterrence vs. prevention; DOPPELGÄNGER caused a temporary rift.
- **Empire of Aukin:** closest institutional partner to the Cradle; supplies most seconded Foreign Selves instructors.
- **Other member states:** smaller states periodically complain that resources flow disproportionately to Anstar; Anstar remains the indispensable guarantor.
- **Umin:** primary adversary; no diplomatic relations; active conflict.
