# Duchy of Alopes (阿珞佩斯公国)
## The Medical Vanguard of the Athena Aegis Accord

> **Status: DRAFT.** Roster facts (continent Sacrum, accession 2325) are canonical. Sections beyond the roster draw on existing character/faction files and are marked **[PROPOSED]** where inferred.

## 1. DESIGNATION

- **Official Name:** Duchy of Alopes
- **Chinese Name:** 阿珞佩斯公国
- **Demonym:** Alopesian
- **Continent:** Sacrum (骶骨大陆), southern hemisphere, eastern flank
- **Date of Accession to AAA:** 2325
- **Capital:** Anshington (安星堂) **[PROPOSED: seat of ducal government; distinct from the AAA headquarters in Anstar City]**

**Name meaning:** *Alopes* is the ancestral **mother-name** of the healing peoples of the old respiratory capital Phoenix. In the naming-war of the Schism it was carried south by the branch that founded the Duchy, while the branch that held the old ground later took the name Aukin (see [The Alopes Schism](../Lore/The_Alopes_Schism.md)). The name is therefore a creed of identity and medical continuity, not a geographic label.

Alopes occupies the Sacrum land-mass — the keystone at the base of the vertebral chain, wedged among the great pelvic structures and the lower circulation conduits.

## 3. GOVERNMENT & POLITICS

- **Regime type:** Hereditary duchy
- **Head of state:** the Grand Duke / Grand Duchess **[TBD: name]**
- **Medical Chancellor:** Dr. **Yuan Foo (袁甫)** — head of the medical establishment and senior privy counsellor
- **Chief Medical Advisor:** **Yuan Yukio (袁雪炤)** — counsel to the Medical Chancellor; the narrator-avatar's post
- **Military command:** Kadd (Marshal) **Yuan Akio (袁秋雄)** — commander of the Jackalope Guard, twin brother of Yukio

**Characteristic posture:** Alopes guards an **independent medical-military doctrine**. Its forces are deliberately not wholly dependent on the Cradle's (Aukin's) definition of "self," and the Jackalope Guard keeps an independent command chain within Alopesian territory. This produces recurring, manageable friction with AAA joint command ("Alopesian autonomy" controversy).

- Maintains the gateway screening systems (Snow / Semmelweis) that model entry–exit epidemic control.

## 5. MEDICAL-DOCTRINAL POSTURE

Alopes is the standard-bearer of the **prevention-first / medicine-over-deterrence** position in the AAA's internal debate (opposed to Anstar's deterrence emphasis):

- Quarantine and early detection over force deployment.
- Every soldier has basic medical training; every medic has combat-survival training.
- Historical memory of the **Hunt** during the Crimson Winter — a people turning on its own healers — makes Alopesian doctrine insist on trust, transparency and protection of medical staff.
- Female healers of the lineage are venerated as the **White-Clad Angels (白衣天使)**.

The **Cytokine Alert System** is named as the Alopesian node feeding the continental early-warning network.

## 7. HISTORY

- **Crimson Winter (2295):** Umin pneumonic plague and the Hunt shatter the old healing people; the Schism divides them into the southern Alopes line, the Awakened (later Aukin), and the Umin-serving Alpsox line.
- **Founding of the Duchy:** the southern branch carries the mother-name to Sacrum and establishes Alopes. **[Date TBD]**
- **2325 — Accession:** joins the Athena Aegis Accord in the "Great Integration," bringing medical expertise and the Jackalope Guard.
- **2343 — Operation DOPPELGÄNGER:** a Typhi MARY operative (MARY-07) replaces Chief Medical Advisor Yukio for 68 days; rescued by the Jackalope Guard. Leads to identity-verification protocols and a temporary 18-month chill with Anstar.
- **2352 — Operation Wounded Crane:** first combat Drift crisis; an Alopesian Jaeger team is extracted after neural disconnection; Drift stability protocols revised.

---

## 8. RELATIONS

- **Empire of Anstar:** alliance partner — Anstar leads on military/political power, Alopes on medical doctrine. Strained by DOPPELGÄNGER, otherwise the alliance's central axis.
- **Empire of Aukin:** shared ancestry; mutual suspicion over **curriculum bias** at the Cradle. Alopes keeps independent doctrine precisely to avoid total reliance on Aukin education. Alopesian cadets (including Yukio) still train at the Cradle.
- **Other member states:** generally cooperative; occasional disputes over resource flow toward founder/major states.
- **Umin:** primary adversary; no relations.
