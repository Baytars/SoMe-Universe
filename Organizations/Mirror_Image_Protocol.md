# Mirror Image Protocol (镜像协议)
## The Autoimmune Insurgency — Molecular Mimicry Warfare

---

## Overview

**Name**: Mirror Image Protocol (镜像协议)
**Codename**: The False Flag (伪旗行动)
**Classification**: Independent Insurgency / Autoimmune Organization
**Symbol**: Broken mirror reflecting identical faces
**Motto**: *"When friend and foe wear the same face, who do you trust?"*

---

## Concept

The Mirror Image Protocol is neither Umin invader nor AAA defender. They are **the enemy within**—a rogue faction that exploits the body's own immune system to attack itself.

Their weapon is **molecular mimicry**: the ability to present antigens that look identical to self-proteins, tricking immune cells into attacking their own citizens.

**Strategic Goal**: Create distrust between AAA forces (police/immune cells) and civilian populations, fragmenting the body's defenses from within.

---

## Medical Background

### Molecular Mimicry (分子模拟)

**Scientific Basis**:
Molecular mimicry occurs when pathogens express antigens that resemble host proteins. The immune system, trained to attack the pathogen, accidentally attacks similar-looking self-tissues.

**Historical Examples**:

| Disease | Trigger | Mimicry Target | Result |
|---------|---------|----------------|--------|
| **Rheumatic Fever** | Group A Streptococcus | Heart valve proteins | Heart damage |
| **Guillain-Barré** | Campylobacter infection | Peripheral nerve myelin | Paralysis |
| **Type 1 Diabetes** | Coxsackievirus? | Pancreatic β-cells | Insulin deficiency |
| **Multiple Sclerosis** | EBV? Unknown | CNS myelin | Neurological damage |
| **Lyme Arthritis** | *Borrelia burgdorferi* | Joint proteins | Chronic arthritis |

### The Autoimmune Cascade

```
1. Infection → Immune activation
2. Mimicry antigen presented
3. Antibodies/T-cells target mimic
4. Cross-reaction with self-tissue
5. Friendly fire → Autoimmune disease
```

**In SoMe terms**:
```
1. Mirror Image agent infiltrates
2. Presents "criminal" that looks like civilian
3. AAA police respond
4. Police attack lookalike civilians
5. Civilian distrust of police → Social collapse
```

## Organization Structure

### Leadership: The Mirror Council

**The Council of Reflections** (镜像议会)
- Seven members, each representing a major autoimmune pathway
- Identity concealed—they appear as ordinary citizens until activated
- Decisions made through "consensus of chaos"

### Divisions

#### 1. Lupus Division (狼疮师团)
**Pattern**: Systemic Lupus Erythematosus (SLE)
**Tactic**: Widespread, unpredictable attacks
**Mechanism**: Anti-nuclear antibodies attack DNA, RNA, proteins anywhere

**Gameplay**:
- Deploy agents that trigger AAA attacks on multiple tissue types
- Create confusion—no pattern, no prediction
- "Every citizen is a suspect"

**Mirror Tactic**: Plant DNA-like markers on civilians, marking them as "foreign" to AAA surveillance

#### 2. Rheuma Division (类风师团)
**Pattern**: Rheumatoid Arthritis
**Tactic**: Target specific infrastructure (joints, connections)
**Mechanism**: Anti-citrullinated protein antibodies (ACPAs)

**Gameplay**:
- Attack key transportation hubs (joints)
- Synovial membrane infiltration → pannus formation
- Infrastructure destruction from within

**Mirror Tactic**: Make joint tissue appear "infected" to AAA patrols

#### 3. Sclerosis Division (硬化师团)
**Pattern**: Multiple Sclerosis
**Tactic**: Sever communication lines
**Mechanism**: Demyelination of nerve fibers

**Gameplay**:
- Attack nerve highways, sever command channels
- Create communication blackouts between brain and body
- Paralysis of coordination

**Mirror Tactic**: Coat myelin sheaths with "foreign" markers

#### 4. Diabe Division (糖谢师团)
**Pattern**: Type 1 Diabetes
**Tactic**: Destroy production facilities
**Mechanism**: β-cell destruction in pancreatic islets

**Gameplay**:
- Target insulin production centers
- Create metabolic chaos
- Starve the body of energy regulation

**Mirror Tactic**: Make β-cells appear as "enemy factories" to AAA

#### 5. Thyro Division (甲亢师团)
**Pattern**: Graves' Disease / Hashimoto's Thyroiditis
**Tactic**: Sabotage regulatory centers
**Mechanism**: Thyroid-stimulating antibody OR thyroid-destroying antibody

**Gameplay**:
- Hyperthyroid mode: Overdrive, exhaustion
- Hypothyroid mode: Shutdown, depression
- Both disrupt metabolic stability

**Mirror Tactic**: Make thyroid cells appear "foreign" or "hijacked"

#### 6. Myas Division (肌无师团)
**Pattern**: Myasthenia Gravis
**Tactic**: Sever command-execution links
**Mechanism**: Anti-acetylcholine receptor antibodies

**Gameplay**:
- Attack neuromuscular junctions
- Orders given but not executed
- "The body that won't obey"

**Mirror Tactic**: Mark acetylcholine receptors as "enemy transmitters"

#### 7. Vasculitis Division (脉管师团)
**Pattern**: Various vasculitides
**Tactic**: Attack supply lines
**Mechanism**: Blood vessel inflammation

**Gameplay**:
- Target blood vessel walls
- Disrupt supply chains
- Create ischemic damage

**Mirror Tactic**: Make vessel walls appear "compromised" to AAA

## Tactics: The False Flag Arsenal

### Tier 1: Confusion

**Molecular Disguise Kit** (分子伪装套件)
- Apply "foreign" markers to civilian cells
- AAA patrols detect and attack
- Civilian casualties create distrust

**Clinical Parallel**: Anti-phospholipid syndrome markers

### Tier 2: Escalation

**Cross-Reaction Catalyst** (交叉反应催化剂)
- Amplify immune response to mimicry antigens
- Increase "friendly fire" incidents
- Create spiral of violence

**Clinical Parallel**: Cytokine storm in autoimmune flares

### Tier 3: Chronic Warfare

**Memory Corruption Protocol** (记忆腐蚀协议)
- Modify immune memory cells to permanently target self-antigens
- Convert temporary confusion into permanent autoimmunity
- "The police never forget—and never forgive"

**Clinical Parallel**: Memory B-cells in chronic autoimmune disease

## Relationship with Other Factions

### Toward Umin

**Strategic Alliance of Convenience**:
- Mirror Image benefits from Umin invasions (more chaos, more immune activation)
- Umin benefits from autoimmune confusion (weakened defenses)

**But**: Mirror Image is not Umin. They have their own agenda—**permanent destabilization**, not conquest.

**Umin's View**: "Useful idiots. They weaken the host, then we take over."

### Toward AAA (Athena Aegis Accord)

**Primary Target**:
- Mirror Image exists to corrupt and destroy AAA effectiveness
- Make AAA the villain in civilian eyes
- "The police are attacking us!"

**AAA's Dilemma**: How to distinguish real threats from Mirror Image decoys?

### Toward Alopes

**Threat to Yukio**:
- As Chief Medical Advisor, Yukio is responsible for immune system coordination
- Mirror Image's goal is to make her own immune cells turn against her citizens
- Personal nightmare: watching her own forces attack innocents

## Historical Parallels in Lore

### The Great Betrayal

**Lore Event**: A Mirror Image operation that triggered AAA to massacre civilians in a major organ region.

**Mechanism**: 
1. Mirror agents marked liver citizens with "Hepatitis C-like" antigens
2. AAA forces detected "infection" and initiated purge
3. Thousands of innocent liver cells destroyed
4. Organ function collapsed
5. Civilian trust in AAA shattered

**Aftermath**: 
- AAA established new "Verification Protocols"
- Civilian populations formed "Self-Defense Militias"
- Body entered state of chronic inflammation (autoimmune disease diagnosis)

### The Molecular Trojan

**Lore Event**: Mirror Image infiltrators posed as Umin defectors seeking asylum.

**Mechanism**:
1. "Defectors" presented themselves to AAA
2. Their antigens matched heart valve proteins
3. AAA accepted them, processed them
4. Immune memory now primed against heart tissue
5. Weeks later: rheumatic fever outbreak

**Lesson**: "Even mercy can be weaponized"

## Gameplay Mechanics

### Mirror Image Campaign

**Player Choice**: When playing Umin campaign, can you trust Mirror Image allies? When playing AAA, can you distinguish real threats from decoys?

**Mission Types**:

1. **False Flag Operation**
   - Mark civilian targets as "infected"
   - Watch AAA attack their own
   - Maximize civilian distrust

2. **Verification Challenge**
   - AAA mission: Distinguish real Umin from Mirror-decoyed civilians
   - Wrong choice = civilian casualties or letting Umin escape

3. **Autoimmune Flare**
   - Trigger massive immune activation
   - Body temperature rises (fever)
   - Collateral damage everywhere

4. **Remission Negotiation**
   - Attempt to calm immune response
   - Trade-offs: less defense = more Umin vulnerability

### Resource System

| Resource | Source | Use |
|----------|--------|-----|
| **Mimicry Markers** | Captured cellular proteins | Tag civilians as "foreign" |
| **Cytokine Storm** | Immune activation events | Escalate friendly fire |
| **Memory Seeds** | Corrupted immune cells | Permanent autoimmunity |
| **Trust Erosion** | Civilian casualties | Reduce AAA effectiveness |

## Countermeasures

### AAA Protocols

**Verification Tier System**:
- Tier 1: Surface antigen check (easily fooled by Mirror Image)
- Tier 2: Intracellular protein analysis (more reliable, slower)
- Tier 3: Functional testing (does it act like a pathogen?)
- Tier 4: Genetic sequencing (definitive, resource-intensive)

**Dilemma**: Higher tiers = slower response = more Umin infiltration risk

### Alopes Research

**Yukio's Initiative**: "The Mirror-Breaker Project"
- Develop tests to distinguish mimicry markers from true foreign antigens
- Create "tolerance protocols" to retrain immune cells
- Controversial: requires exposing immune cells to self-antigens (risk of worse autoimmunity)

## Design Philosophy

The Mirror Image Protocol embodies several themes:

1. **Medical Education**: Autoimmune disease mechanisms as gameplay
2. **Trust and Betrayal**: The horror of your own protectors turning against you
3. **Identity and Mimicry**: What makes something "self" vs "other"?
4. **Collateral Damage**: The tragedy of friendly fire
5. **Chronic Disease**: Once triggered, autoimmunity is hard to reverse

**Core Tension**: Mirror Image forces players to question every identification. Is that Umin agent, or a Mirror-decoyed civilian? Attack or hold fire? Either choice has consequences.
