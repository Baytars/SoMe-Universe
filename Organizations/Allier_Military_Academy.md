# ALLIER MILITARY ACADEMY (阿利尔军事学院)
## Anstar Imperial Armed Forces — Officer Training Command

---

## INSTITUTIONAL OVERVIEW

### Name Origin

**Allier** derives from the name of **Allierna** — the woman after whom the Anstar Empire was founded.

**The Founding Legend**:
Before Alopes existed, before the continent was charted, there lived an old noble family on the lands that would become Continent Sacrum. Their daughter — **Allierna** — was renowned for her beauty, her lineage, and her unwavering spirit.

A young man named **Tiberson** came to these lands as an adventurer. Upon meeting Allierna, he was overcome with love. But she was of old nobility; he was a wanderer. The gap between them was impassable.

So Tiberson did what no man had done before: he united the warring tribes of the continent under his banner, built cities from nothing, and forged alliances where there had only been conflict. When he finally stood before Allierna's family, he was no longer a wanderer. He was an emperor.

He founded **Anstar Empire** — its very name rooted in **Allierna** — and returned to claim her hand. She accepted.

**The Academy's Name**:
The academy bears her name as a reminder: **alliance is forged through devotion**. Not convenience. Not treaties. Devotion.

> *"Tiberson did not create an empire to rule. He created an empire to be worthy of love."*

## ARCHITECTURAL DESIGN

### The Three Towers

Like Corbulo Academy's iconic observatory, Allier features three signature structures:

| Tower | Name | Purpose |
|-------|------|---------|
| **West Tower** | *Turris Fidei* (Tower of Trust) | Academic instruction, strategic studies, briefing rooms |
| **East Tower** | *Turris Armorum* (Tower of Arms) | Combat simulation, weapons training, physical conditioning |
| **North Tower** | *Turris Allier* (Tower of Alliance) | Cross-factional integration, diplomatic simulation, cultural exchange |

The three towers converge at the **Forium** — the central gathering space where students from different nations, factions, and backgrounds are forced into proximity. There are no separate dining halls by nationality. There are no assigned seating by political allegiance. The Forium is where alliances begin.

### The Witness

At the courtyard's center grows a single oak tree, said to be descended from a sapling that Allierna herself planted when Tiberson departed to build his empire. It is called **"The Witness"** — it has seen every class graduate, every scandal unfold, every alliance formed and broken.

It represents her patience: she waited. He returned.

## ACADEMIC STRUCTURE

### Class Tiers

Allier admits cadets in three tiers, reflecting the academy's philosophy that different soldiers need different training:

| Class | Designation | Background | Curriculum Focus |
|-------|-------------|------------|-----------------|
| **Class I** | *"Ignis"* (Fire) | Direct imperial commissions, noble-born | Leadership ethics, strategic command |
| **Class II** | *"Ferrum"* (Iron) | Allied nation nominations, professional soldiers | Tactical execution, unit cohesion |
| **Class III** | *"Silicium"* (Stone) | Civilian选拔, technical specialists | Innovation, unconventional thinking |

### Core Curriculum

**Class I - Ignis (Leadership Track)**:
- Advanced Strategic Doctrine
- Imperial Political Philosophy
- Ethical Command Seminars
- Cross-Factional Negotiation
- Historical Analysis of Alliance Failures

**Class II - Ferrum (Tactical Track)**:
- Combat Simulation (Full Immersion)
- Small Unit Tactics
- Allied Force Integration
- Field Medicine
- Psychological Resilience Training

**Class III - Silicium (Innovation Track)**:
- Unconventional Warfare Theory
- Technology Integration
- Propaganda and Information Warfare
- Civilian-Military Relations
- Emergency Response Protocols

### The Integration Exercise (Term-End Assessment)

All three classes converge for the **Integration Exercise** — a month-long simulation requiring Ignis strategists to command Ferrum tacticians using Silicium innovations. No class can succeed without the others.

> *"The academy does not ask which class is superior. The academy asks: what happens when fire, iron, and stone are forged together?"*

## DISTINCTIVE TRADITIONS

### The Anshington Hour

Each evening at sunset, the entire academy observes **The Anshington Hour** — five minutes of absolute silence. No one speaks. No one moves. Students stand at attention in the Forium, facing the oak tree.

The tradition commemorates the civilians who sheltered imperial soldiers during the Siege of Anshington, and the soldiers who died protecting those civilians in return. It is a reminder that **alliance is not one-way**.

> *"We do not protect civilians because they are weak. We protect civilians because without them, we are not an army. We are only armed men."* — Empress Allierna

### The Alliance Knot

Upon graduation, each student receives a **knot of braided cord** — three strands representing Ignis, Ferrum, and Silicium. The knot is worn on the left wrist during active service. When a graduate dies in service, their knot is returned to the academy and hung in the Forium's Hall of Remembrance.

There are now over **12,000 knots** hanging in the Hall.

### Cross-Factional Pairing

Unlike institutions that train soldiers for specific national service, Allier's **Cross-Factional Pairing** program places graduates into **allied nations' armed forces** for their first three years of service. An Anstar noble may serve in Alopes' Jackalope Guard. An Alopesian cadet may be assigned to Anstar's Imperial Navy.

The program is controversial. Critics call it naive. Proponents call it the only reason the continent has remained at peace for forty years.

### Structural Parallels

| Corbulo Academy | Allier Military Academy |
|-----------------|------------------------|
| Founded by UNSC | Founded by Imperial Decree |
| Located on Reach (Fortress world) | Located on Allierna Plateau (Fortress installation) |
| Mission: Train Spartans for war | Mission: Train officers for alliance |
| Three Training Wings | Three Towers |
| Emphasis on physical conditioning | Emphasis on trust-building |
| Spartan-II (genetically enhanced) | Ignis (noble-born, politically enhanced) |

### Key Differences

**Corbulo trains warriors. Allier trains partners.**

Corbulo's Spartans are designed to be **superior** — stronger, faster, more capable than any enemy. Allier's graduates are designed to be **allied** — capable of working with anyone, from any background, toward a shared goal.

Corbulo's curriculum assumes the enemy is **other**. Allier's curriculum assumes the challenge is **division**.

### The Corbulo Correspondence

There is an unofficial exchange program between Allier and Corbulo — technically unofficial because neither institution officially acknowledges the other's existence. Students who discover it (through careful historical research or well-placed inquiries) call it **"The Hidden Curriculum"**.

The correspondence consists of a single shared course: **"Why We Fight"** — a joint seminar comparing UNSC strategic philosophy with Anstar Imperial doctrine, taught by rotating faculty from both institutions.

## CRITICISMS & CONTROVERSIES

### "Alliance or Assimilation?"

Critics from allied nations argue that Allier does not forge alliances — it **assimilates**. Students who attend return to their home nations thinking, speaking, and strategizing in Anstar terms. The "cross-factional integration" becomes Anstar-ification in disguise.

**Academy Response**: *The Ignis program specifically trains students to preserve their native identity. The goal is not to create Anstar officers. The goal is to create officers who can work WITH Anstar.*

### "The 34% Who Fail"

The Class I graduation rate (34%) is a source of constant controversy. Critics argue that the rate is artificially low — designed to intimidate rather than educate. The academy counters that the rate reflects the genuine difficulty of learning to **trust before commanding**.

> *"The cadets who fail are not weak. They are those who could not learn to need others. Those who cannot need others will not lead. They will only dominate."* — Allier Commandant, 2347

### "The Anshington Hour is Performative"

Some students consider the Anshington Hour a hollow ritual — five minutes of standing while the empire's civilian-military relations remain fractured in practice.

The academy maintains that **ritual is infrastructure**. The silence is not meant to solve the problem. It is meant to **remind** students that the problem exists.
