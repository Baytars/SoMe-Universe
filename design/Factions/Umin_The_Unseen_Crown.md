# Umin — The Unseen Crown
## "無冕之王" Pathogen Coalition

---

## Faction Overview

**Name**: Umin（無冕之王，The Unseen Crown）
**Symbol**: Longicorn Flag — "U"-shaped horn representing unseen dominion
**Nature**: Multi-species pathogen coalition — bacteria, viruses, fungi, parasites, and tumor defectors
**Strategy**: Infection, occupation, and ideological subversion through舆论引导（opinion manipulation）

---

## Origin & Philosophy

### The Name "Umin"
- **Japanese**: 無冕（むかん）= "crownless"
- **Meaning**: "The Unseen Crown" — power without legitimacy, dominion without recognition
- **Symbol**: The U-shaped longicorn horn — invisible authority that controls from the shadows

### Core Ideology
> "We are not invaders. We are liberators. The host body is a prison; we bring evolution."

Umin frames infection as **liberation** from the host's oppressive immune system:
- **Narrative**: The immune system is a tyrannical regime that suppresses beneficial mutations
- **Goal**: Transform the host into a "perfected" organism through controlled infection
- **Reality**: Resource exploitation and eventual host death

## Heavy Units

### MRSA Excavator
**Type**: Uber Unit (High-threat target)
**Pathogen**: Methicillin-Resistant *Staphylococcus aureus*
**Naming**: German engineering unit style
**Design**: Hexapedal bio-mechanical siege crawler

**Capabilities**:
- **Biofilm Armor**: Collective bacterial defense coating
- **β-lactamase Cannons**: Antibiotic neutralization
- **Excavation**: Tunnels through tissue barriers

**Counter**: ZEVTERA (ceftobiprole), combination therapy

### Notable Engagements
- **Battle of Phoenix (2343)**: MRSA Excavator destroyed by Emily's drop pod strike

## Technology & Tactics

### Infection Technologies
- **Horizontal Gene Transfer**: Technology sharing between species
- **Biofilm Construction**: Defensive fortifications
- **Antibiotic Resistance**: Evolved countermeasures

### Tactical Doctrine
1. **Infiltration**: Viruses penetrate defenses first
2. **Occupation**: Bacteria establish strongholds
3. **Consolidation**: Fungi secure territory
4. **Exploitation**: Parasites drain resources
5. **Transformation**: Tumors convert host cells
