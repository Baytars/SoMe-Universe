## STRATEGIC BACKGROUND

### The Preceptor Problem

By 2347, Umin Strategic Command had identified the Preceptor Order as the **single greatest threat** to Umin operations on Continent Sacrum.

Unlike combat units—which Umin could engage and destroy through superior numbers—the Preceptors operated on a different battlefield entirely. They did not fight Umin. They **anticipated** Umin. They sealed vulnerabilities before Umin could exploit them. They neutralized nascent threats before those threats could mature.

Three years of Umin operations on Continent Sacrum had produced a troubling pattern:

| Operation | Umin Objective | Outcome |
|-----------|---------------|---------|
| Operation Hemorrhage | Pathogen spread in Coastal Zone | **Failed** — Preceptors detected and neutralized within 48 hours |
| Operation Deeproot | Sabotage of medical facilities | **Failed** — Preceptors identified infiltration and sealed vulnerabilities |
| Operation Silent Spring | Psychological warfare campaign | **Failed** — Preceptors detected messaging patterns and preemptively inoculated populations |
| Operation Harvest Moon | Mass casualty event | **Failed** — Preceptors evacuated civilians before attack |

Umin analysts reached a devastating conclusion:

> *The Preceptor Order has made us visible. Every operation we launch, they see. Every plan we make, they anticipate. We are not fighting the Jackalope Guard. We are fighting prophecy.*

### The Discovery: Ves弗尼尔

The breakthrough came from an unexpected source.

During Umin's archaeological excavation of ancient consciousness networks—specifically, data fragments from the **Genshin (原神) Continuum**—Umin Intelligence discovered a case study in **information warfare**:

**The Prophet Ves弗尼尔 (Vedrfolnir)**

In the Genshin narrative, Ves弗尼尔 was a seer—an entity with the ability to perceive future events. When he prophesied truths that **the Black King (黑王)** found inconvenient, the Black King responded with surgical precision:

**The Prophecy That Triggered Retaliation**:
> *"The throne of the divine will fall. The god of the sky will weep. The chosen one will be unmade."*

**The Black King's Response**:
Rather than kill the prophet—which would create a martyr and draw attention—Ves弗尼尔 was **blinded**. Deprived of his sight, the prophet could no longer verify his visions. He could no longer speak with certainty. His truth became a whisper, then a murmur, then silence.

The prophecy, rendered uncertain, lost its power to change the future.

**Umin Strategic Assessment**:
> *The enemy does not fear the sword. The enemy fears the eye. If we cannot kill the Preceptors—without revealing our hand—then we must blind them.*

## OPERATION PLANNING

### Phase 1: Understanding the Enemy

Umin spent two years studying the Preceptor Order's sensory apparatus:

**Target Identification**:

| Component | Function | Vulnerability |
|----------|----------|---------------|
| Aesculapian Sensor Array | Real-time pathogen monitoring | Requires continuous calibration; vulnerable to interference |
| Early Warning Network | Threat pattern analysis | Depends on human interpretation; vulnerable to cognitive attack |
| Inter-Unit Communication | Intelligence sharing | Vulnerable to signal disruption |
| Population Trust Network | Social resilience against manipulation | Vulnerable to erosion |

**Key Insight**:
> The Preceptors are not strong. They are **correct**. Their power lies not in weapons, but in **certainty**—the certainty that comes from seeing clearly. Take away that certainty, and they become like any other soldier: blind, uncertain, afraid.

### Phase 2: The Blinding Protocol

Umin developed a three-pronged approach to **blind** the Preceptor Order:

#### Prong 1: Sensory Degradation (感知退化)

**Objective**: Degrade the Aesculapian Sensor Arrays' accuracy without triggering alarm

**Method**: 
- Deploy **Noise Pathogens**—engineered microorganisms that produce false positives in biosensor arrays
- Create "sensor fatigue" through constant low-level false alarms
- Train Preceptor operators to ignore alerts as routine static

**Result**: Within 6 months, Preceptor sensor arrays were generating 400% more alerts—95% of which were false. The signal-to-noise ratio collapsed.

#### Prong 2: Cognitive Corruption (认知腐化)

**Objective**: Corrupt the interpretation layer—the human analysts who make sense of sensor data

**Method**:
- Through the **Despair Cult (绝望教派)**, spread psychological narratives suggesting that Preceptor interventions were "causing" the crises they were meant to prevent
- Introduce **"Prophet's Doubt"**—a manufactured philosophical crisis within the Order: *"What if our interventions create the very threats we detect?"*
- Isolate senior Preceptors through misinformation, creating contradictory operational guidance

**Result**: By 2349, the Preceptor Order was fractured. Senior Preceptors issued conflicting orders. Junior Preceptors stopped trusting their sensors.

#### Prong 3: Communication Severance (通讯切断)

**Objective**: Isolate Preceptor teams from each other and from command

**Method**:
- Disrupt inter-unit communication through **electromagnetic interference pulses**
- Deploy **Trust Erosion Protocol** to convince Preceptors that their colleagues had been compromised
- Introduce false intelligence suggesting that certain Preceptor teams were Umin infiltrators

**Result**: The Preceptor Order fractured into isolated cells, each unable to verify the other's observations.

## OPERATION EXECUTION: 2350-03-15

### The Day of Blinding

At 0347 hours, Umin Strategic Command initiated **Operation Blinding Prophet** in three simultaneous strikes:

#### Strike 1: The Sensor Storm (传感器风暴)

**Target**: All Aesculapian Sensor Arrays across Eastern Continental Sacrum

**Action**: Umin deployed a synchronized release of **Noise Pathogens** across 12 monitoring zones. Within 4 hours, sensor arrays were generating so many false positives that Preceptor operators began dismissing genuine alerts as noise.

**Recorded Preceptor Communication** (intercepted):
> *"All zones showing red. This can't be right. Check calibration on Array 7-Alpha."  
> "Already checked. It's showing the same readings. I think it's a system glitch."  
> "Just mark it as noise and continue. We can't investigate every false alarm."*

#### Strike 2: The Doubt Broadcast (怀疑广播)

**Target**: Preceptor Order command structure

**Action**: Umin Despair Cult broadcast a pre-recorded message on all Preceptor communication channels:

> *"You think you see. But what you see, do you truly understand? Every intervention you make—does it prevent, or does it create? The pathogen you sealed yesterday—was it there because of Umin, or because of you? Look into your own heart, Preceptor. The enemy you fight... might be your own certainty."*

**Effect**: The philosophical crisis within the Order crystallized into paralysis. Senior Preceptors halted operations to "reassess methodology." Junior Preceptors stopped making decisions without approval.

#### Strike 3: The Coordination Kill (协同击杀)

**Target**: Preceptor inter-unit communication networks

**Action**: Umin deployed targeted electromagnetic pulses against Preceptor relay stations. Communication between field teams collapsed.

**Consequence**: Preceptors who would have coordinated to investigate the sensor anomalies were now isolated, each operating on corrupted data with no way to verify their observations.

The Preceptor Order—the most effective预警系统 in Alopes—was **blinded**.

### The Unseen Massacre

With the Preceptor Order blind, Umin launched a series of operations that had been planned but previously impossible:

**Operation Hollow Victory** (2350-03-18):
- Target: Phoenix Eastern Residential Zone
- Objective: Mass casualty event
- Pre-Blinding Assessment: **Impossible** — Preceptors would have detected and evacuated

**The Irony**:
> *The Preceptors could not see the attack because they had been blinded. And when they were blamed for the failure, they could not defend themselves. How do you prove you would have prevented something that, by your blindness, you demonstrably did not?*

## THE REFORMATION

### The New Doctrine

The Blinding forced a fundamental rethinking of Preceptor methodology:

**Old Doctrine**: *"See everything. Interpret everything. Prevent everything."*

**New Doctrine**: *"See uncertainly. Act anyway. Trust the pattern, not the detail."*

The reformed Preceptor Order developed protocols for operating under **cognitive warfare conditions**:

| Challenge | Old Response | New Response |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| False positives flooding sensors | Investigate each alert | Pattern analysis over individual detection |
| Communication disrupted | Wait for reconnection | Distributed autonomous response |
| Cognitive corruption spreading | Suppress dissent | Embrace productive doubt |
| Isolated operation | Request coordination | Operate on probabilistic frameworks |

### The Prophetic Reformation

**Void Speaker Bian Que III** issued a new doctrinal statement:

> *"We have learned a terrible truth: the eye can be blinded. The sensor can be corrupted. The communicator can be silenced. But the pattern cannot be unseen—not by blinding one eye, but by blinding all eyes.*
>
> *We were wrong to trust our instruments. We were wrong to trust our certainty. What remains when certainty is gone?*
>
> *Only this: the willingness to act in the absence of proof. The courage to prevent when prevention cannot be guaranteed. The faith—yes, faith—that the pattern is real even when we cannot see it.*
>
> *The enemy blinded us with noise. We will learn to see through the noise. The enemy blinded us with doubt. We will learn to act despite doubt.*
>
> *We will not be silenced. We will not be blinded. We will be wounded, diminished, ridiculed—and we will still see.*
>
> *This is the Reformation. This is our second sight."*

### The Unanswered Question

The Blinding left an unanswered question that haunts the Preceptor Order:

> *If we had not been blinded, would we have prevented the massacre?*

The mathematical models say yes. The philosophical crisis says: *we cannot know. We can never know. And that unknowing is our burden.*

This question becomes a central narrative thread in SoMe:

- **Can prophecy be trusted if the prophet can be corrupted?**
- **What is the value of warning that cannot be verified?**
- **Is the Preceptor who fails to prevent a disaster, despite their warning, a hero or a failure?**

## MECHANICAL INTEGRATION

### Gameplay Effect: The Blinding Condition

In SoMe gameplay, **Preceptor units** can be affected by the **Blinding** debuff:

**Blinding (预警失灵)**:
- Sensor accuracy reduced by 70%
- Cannot detect threats until they are within close range
- Probability-based detection replaces deterministic detection
- Cannot share intelligence with allied units

**Recovery**:
- Requires 3 turns of "Sensor Recalibration" action
- Can be accelerated by dedicated Scanner units
- Partial recovery (50% effectiveness) available after 1 turn

### Tactical Implication

The Blinding creates asymmetric gameplay:

- **Umin** can unlock **Blinding Protocol** as a special operation
- **Preceptor Order** must develop **Counter-Blinding** tactics
- Players must balance between trusting Preceptor intelligence and operating independently
