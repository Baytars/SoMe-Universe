## Purpose

Pacific Rim provides reference methodology for **mecha vs parasite combat** in SoMe. When human cells (at cellular scale) must combat macroscopic parasites, Jaeger-Kaiju dynamics offer a template for:
- Multi-pilot neural synchronization systems
- Mech classification by technological generation
- Threat classification for giant enemies
- Tactical evolution against adaptive adversaries

| Mark I | 1st | Nuclear Reactor | Prototype, analog systems, high radiation risk |
| Mark II | 1st | Nuclear Reactor | Improved shielding, limited production |
| Mark III | 1st | Nuclear Reactor | Refined design, Gipsy Danger exemplar |
| Mark IV | 2nd | Digital Technology | Transition to digital, improved efficiency |
| Mark V | 2nd | Digital Technology | Advanced neural interface |
| Mark VI | 3rd | Digital Technology | Peak pre-closure design |
| Mark VII | 4th | Digital Technology | Post-closure, Striker Eureka |

### Neural Handshake & Drift System

**Core Mechanism**: Two pilots must synchronize neural activity to operate a single Jaeger.

**Drift Compatibility Factors**:
- Shared experiences/trauma
- Compatible personality profiles
- Trust and rapport
- Physical/neural conditioning

**The Drift**:
- Shared consciousness stream
- Access to partner's memories (including "RABIT" - Random Access Brain Impulse Triggers)
- Requires mental discipline to maintain
- Pilot compatibility determines drift stability

**SoMe Translation**:
- **Dual-cell pilot system**: Two differentiated cells synchronize to operate combat chassis
- **Signal molecule drift**: Cytokine/exosome exchange creates shared operational awareness
- **Differentiation memory**: Stem cell origins create compatibility bonds
- **RABIT analogue**: Traumatic cellular memory from past infections/injuries

| Category | Classification Criteria | Example |
|----------|------------------------|---------|

| Category II | Medium threat, emerging capabilities | Knifehead |

| Category V | Extreme threat, unprecedented scale | Slattern |

**Measurement Parameters**:
- Water displacement tonnage
- Toxicity level
- Radiation signature
- Collective threat assessment

### Kaiju Biology

- **Composition**: Silicon-based lifeforms
- **Blood**: Ammonia-based liquid (highly toxic to carbon life)
- **Origin**: Breach portal to Anteverse
- **Creation**: Genetically engineered bioweapons by Precursors
- **Adaptation**: Each engagement yields data for next generation
- **Hive Intelligence**: Category V specimens show emergent autonomy

**SoMe Translation**:
- **Parasite Categories**: Ascending scale for helminths, protozoa, ectoparasites
- **Displacement analogue**: Host tissue damage potential
- **Toxicity**: Immunomodulatory/toxin secretion capacity
- **Radiation → Inflammation signature**: Cytokine storm potential
- **Adaptation**: Immune evasion mechanisms evolve per encounter

## Combat Doctrine Evolution

### Jaeger-Kaiju Arms Race

**Phase 1 - Initial Deployments**:
- Jaegers dominate, Kaiju unprepared
- Easy victories, public confidence high
- Individual heroism emphasized

**Phase 2 - Kaiju Adaptation**:
- Larger, faster, more specialized Kaiju
- Jaeger losses mount
- Military doctrine shifts to attrition

**Phase 3 - Desperate Measures**:
- Jaeger program defunded
- Coastal wall strategy (failed)
- Underground resistance operations

**Phase 4 - Counter-Offensive**:
- Nuclear strike on breach
- Final defense at portal
- Strategic breakthrough

**SoMe Translation**:
- **Immunotherapy → Jaeger Programme**: Initial promise, eventual adaptation
- **Pathogen evolution → Kaiju generations**: Rapid adaptation to treatments
- **Combination therapy → Multi-Jaeger tactics**: Coordinated assaults
- **Precision medicine → Mark VII tech**: Targeted interventions

## SoMe Integration: Mecha-Parasite Combat

### The Scale Paradox

In SoMe, humans are cell-sized operating within host organisms. Parasites range from:
- **Microscopic**: Plasmodium, Giardia (individual combat)
- **Macroscopic**: Schistosomes, Nematodes, Cestodes (Jaeger-scale engagement)

### Mecha Chassis Types

| Chassis Class | Anti-Parasite Role | Pilot Configuration |
|---------------|-------------------|---------------------|
| Scout Frame | Detection, early warning | Single pilot |
| Combat Frame | Direct engagement | Dual pilot (Drift) |
| Heavy Frame | Siege warfare | Quad pilot (Cluster Drift) |
| Command Frame | Tactical coordination | Dual pilot + AI support |

### Neural Drift → Cytokine Synchronization

**Mechanism**: Two cells exchange signal molecules to achieve operational unity.

**Compatible Pairings**:
- Same lineage (e.g., two T cells)
- Complementary function (e.g., T cell + B cell)
- Stem-origin bond (cells from same progenitor)

**Drift Breakdown Consequences**:
- Signal dissonance
- Chassis feedback damage
- Cognitive desynchronization
- Combat vulnerability

### Threat Categories for Parasites

| Category | Parasite Type | Chassis Requirement |
|----------|--------------|---------------------|
| I | Protozoa (Giardia) | Scout/Single pilot |
| II | Small helminths (Hookworm) | Combat frame |
| III | Medium helminths (Ascaris) | Heavy combat |
| IV | Large helminths (Schistosoma) | Heavy frame + support |
| V | Massive parasites (Tapeworm segments) | Multi-frame coordination |
