### Appearance

**Civilian Form**:
- Bipedal humanoid android resembling a teenage human girl
- Brown eyes
- Molded green hair with drill-shaped tendrils (tight hair curls)
- Pink armoured dress (default outfit)
- Tiara-like headband lined with sensors
- Matching row of sensors on chest (necklace-like)
- Pointed silver antennae on either side of head

**Combat Form** (after Shadow rebuilds her):
- Sharper features
- Built-in armour
- Equipped with a sword

---

### Personality

Emily is a **happy young girl** who loves her family very much. Key traits:

- **Homeschooled**: Earth equivalent of 8th grade
- **Space Colony Project**: Her family is part of a study on how human families can live in space
- **Monitored Life**: Family monitored by Ministry of Science; life is part of a simulation
- **Real Emotions**: Despite simulation context, Emily's emotions and dreams are genuine
- **Blue Knight Fan**: Enjoys hearing her father's stories about the Blue Knight

---

### Story Arc

**The Kidnapping**:
1. **Katari** kidnaps Emily's parents
2. Emily immediately volunteers to join the Blue Knight's army
3. She agrees to become a super soldier through modifications
4. **Critical Decision**: She knows her memories could be deleted forever, but takes the chance — she cares more about saving her parents from slavery

**The Resolution**:
- Emily is fully restored
- She decides she doesn't want to be a warrior anymore

**Potential SoMe Backstory**:
Emily was a civilian in a frontier colony when Umin forces captured her parents. Despite being a non-combatant (student), she volunteered for the Jackalope Guard's emergency augmentation program. The procedure granted her combat capabilities but risked permanent neural damage. She fought alongside Lt.
