## P0 Critical Visual Elements

### 1. Drop Pod Design
**Reference**: Halo ODST SOEIV (M9407/M8823)

**Key Frames**:
- `Key_Frames/01_drop_pod_control.jpg` - Interior control panel
- `Key_Frames/02_hologram_panel.jpg` - Hologram communication
- `Key_Frames/03_cockpit_descent.jpg` - Full cockpit during descent

**SoMe Adaptation**:
- Keep ergonomic metal frame with worn industrial aesthetic
- Replace holographic panels with **Exosome** communication system
- Add medical vital signs display (heart rate, O2 saturation)
- Color scheme: Gunmetal gray + Blue LED accents (Alopes signature)
- HUD elements: Target designation, altitude countdown, squad status

**External Reference**:
- Halopedia: https://www.halopedia.org/Category:Images_of_M9407_drop_pod
- Artstation: https://www.artstation.com/search?query=odst%20drop%20pod

### 2. MRSA Excavator Design
**Reference**: Halo Blisterback (Gydrozka Workshop Siege Crawler)

**Key Frames from Halo Wars 2**:
- Location: `用户上传/halo_frames/` (frames 50-80 contain Blisterback)
- External Reference: `https://halo.wiki.gallery/images/1/1b/HW2_Blisterback_render.png`

**SoMe Adaptation**:
- **Original**: Hexapedal (6 legs) siege crawler
- **SoMe Design**: Maintain hexapedal structure but add bio-mechanical fusion
  - Legs: Mechanical joints + organic muscle fiber cables
  - Hull: MRSA colony biofilm coating (translucent green-yellow)
  - Weapons: Antibiotic resistance mechanisms visualized
  - Size: ~15m height (comparable to Halo Blisterback)

**Medical Accuracy**:
- MRSA = Methicillin-Resistant Staphylococcus Aureus
- Visual elements should reflect:
  - Biofilm protective coating
  - β-lactamase enzyme release (green mist)
  - Efflux pump mechanisms (visible on hull)

### 3. Jaunty Jackalope Flag
**File**: `Key_Frames/07_jaunty_jackalope_flag.jpg`

**Design Elements**:
- Shape: Hexagonal top + Curved shield bottom
- Core Icon: Mechanical stag with neon blue glow
  - Silver-gray metal body
  - Blue LED circuit patterns
  - Antlers framing the center
- Medical Symbol: Caduceus (double serpent staff) + Longsword
- Background: Navy blue + Hexagonal honeycomb texture
- Glow Effects: Neon blue edge lighting

**Placement in Video**:
- Shoulder patch on Emily's armor
- Flag on Nightingale-class command vessel
- Tactical display during briefing

## P1 Secondary Visual Elements

### 4. Emily Character Design
**Armor**: Standard soldier loadout (no special armor design)
**Reference**: Halo ODST trooper aesthetic

**Key Features**:
- Helmet: Enclosed tactical with blue-tinted visor
- Body: Standard-issue combat armor (dark gray/navy)
- Rank insignia: Corporal chevrons
- Unit patch: Jaunty Jackalope flag on left shoulder
- Equipment: Standard assault rifle

**Personality Visual Cues**:
- Young, athletic build
- Eyes visible through visor (optional)
- Confident posture despite rookie status

### 5. Nightingale-class Medical Command Vessel
**Naming Origin**: Florence Nightingale (1820-1910), "Lady with the Lamp"
**First Deployment**: Battle of Phoenix (2343)

**Design Principles**:
- Hybrid command + medical facility
- Aerial platform (not space vessel)
- Multiple landing bays for Drop Pods
- Prominent medical cross insignia
- Color: White hull + Red cross markings + Blue accent lights

**Visual Reference Needed**:
- Hospital ship aesthetics (USNS Mercy class)
- Flying command center (Helicarrier from MCU)
- Medical bay interior layouts

### 6. Phoenix City Appearance
**Reference Frames**:
- `Key_Frames/04_city_aerial.jpg` - Aerial view during descent
- `Key_Frames/05_descent_anomaly.jpg` - Urban environment
- `Key_Frames/06_city_street.jpg` - Street level (New Mombasa style)

**SoMe Adaptation**:
- **Pre-war**: Modern city with aviation infrastructure
  - Air Force Academy
  - Multiple airports (regional hub)
- **War-torn**: Heavy damage from Umin occupation (2341-2343)
  - Destroyed buildings
  - Bio-weapon contamination zones
  - Emergency medical stations

**Atmosphere**:
- Dawn lighting (Operation Jaunty Jackalope timing)
- Smoke and debris
- Emergency lighting
- Contrast: Cold tactical blue (Alopes forces) vs Warm orange (explosions/fires)
