# The Seekers of Anstar
## Anstar Imperial Elite Reconnaissance Unit

---

## Unit Overview

### Designation
**"The Seekers"** — *Anstar Imperial 7th Deep Reconnaissance Corps*

### Motto
> *"Videmus omnia. Portamus veritatem."*
> *"We see all. We carry truth."*

### Affiliation
**Anstar Empire** — The sovereign power of the immune sphere, guardian of the Sacrum Continent's central nervous system.

### Role
Long-range deep reconnaissance, intelligence gathering behind Umin lines, target designation for surgical strikes, and the extraction of biological specimens from hostile territories.

### Status
**ACTIVE** — Currently operating in contested zones along the Cervical Plateau perimeter.

## Core Personnel

### I. COMMAND ELEMENT

#### GAMOR STUART (宋佳木)
**Rank:** Lieutenant Commander
**Title:** Commanding Officer, The Seekers
**Family:** Second son of the House of Stuart, Anstar Empire

**Profile:**
The second son of the Stuart family—born into privilege but shaped by the realities of a generation that grew up under Umin shadow. Gamor's tactical acumen is legendary within Anstar's officer corps: he processes battlefield data faster than any analyst, anticipates enemy movement with unsettling precision, and has an almost unnatural ability to read terrain as if the tissue itself were speaking to him.

But what sets Gamor apart is not his pedigree. It is his **conviction**. He believes—genuinely, without performance—that every soldier under his command must return alive. Not because protocol demands it, but because he cannot stomach the alternative.

**Combat Specialty:** Tactical Command, Terrain Analysis, Multi-Unit Coordination

**Personality Notes:**
- Speaks rarely, but when he does, everyone listens
- Carries a worn field journal—filled not with orders, but with the names of every soldier he has ever lost
- Treats his men as family; treats enemies as puzzles to be solved
- His relationship with his family is complicated—Stuart prestige opened doors, but Gamor has spent his career proving he earned his rank

**Famous Line:**
> *"We're not here to win a firefight. We're here to make sure there ISN'T a firefight. That's what makes us dangerous."*

---

#### KIMI JIMI (季芈)
**Rank:** Sergeant First Class
**Title:** Deputy Commander, Intelligence & Signals Specialist

**Profile:**
Kimi Jimi—known to the squad simply as **Kimi**—is the Seekers' deadliest weapon and its most unpredictable asset. A world-class marksman with a suppressed anti-material rifle that she calls *"Long Whisper,"* she can eliminate a Voice Knight at 800 meters before the target even registers the sound. But her true power lies in the digital realm: she speaks the Umin's encrypted communications fluently, has cracked their pattern-based cipher systems twice in the field, and once—during the Siege of the Lymphatic Fork—hacked a Umin relay node to broadcast false orders that caused a thirty-minute friendly-fire incident behind enemy lines.

Kimi is not officially Anstar military. She was recruited from a civilian cyber-operations program at age nineteen after a series of unauthorized incursions into Umin communication networks. Her methods are... unorthodox. Her commanding officers either love her or want her court-martialed. Gamor is firmly in the former camp.

**Combat Specialty:** Sniper, Signals Intelligence, Cyber Warfare, Electronic Countermeasures

**Equipment:**
- *Long Whisper* — Suppressed anti-material rifle, custom-modified for stealth engagement
- Portable signal interception array (carried in two backpacks)
- Neural uplink interface for real-time communication jamming

**Personality Notes:**
- Uses humor as a shield; jokes constantly, especially during firefights
- Genuinely likes people, which is rare in her profession
- Secretly terrified of losing squad members; covers this by being the first one into danger
- Has a rivalry with Echolot that is 40% professional respect and 60% mutual annoyance

**Famous Line:**
> *"The Umin think their whispers are secure. I've been reading their bedtime stories for three years."*

### II. RECONNAISSANCE ELEMENT

#### SPECTER
**Designation:** Echo-Specter (callsign only; true identity classified)
**Role:** Infiltration Specialist

**Profile:**
The Seekers' ghost. Specter has entered Umin-held territories fourteen times and returned fourteen times—though "returned" is generous: on three occasions, the extraction team found only a trail of bodies and a note in Umin command cipher. No one knows Specter's real face, real name, or real history. This is by design.

Specter's specialty is **perspective**: they see what others overlook, remember what others forget, and move through hostile territory like a memory the body tries to forget.

**Combat Specialty:** Stealth movement, disguise, interrogation resistance, psychological infiltration

**Personality Notes:**
- Communicates primarily through hand signals; speaks only when necessary
- May or may not be a single individual; squad lore suggests "Specter" is a codename passed between multiple operatives
- Has a strange rapport with Wraith—the two move in silence together in ways that unsettle other squad members

#### WRAITH
**Designation:** [REDACTED]
**Role:** Survival Specialist, Long-Range Tracker

**Profile:**
If Specter enters Umin territory to *see*, Wraith enters to *endure*. The longest recorded solo reconnaissance mission by an Anstar operative lasted forty-seven days—conducted entirely behind Umin lines, without extraction support, with minimal equipment. The operative returned with intelligence that prevented the hemorrhagic catastrophe at the Fourth Molar. That operative was Wraith.

Wraith does not speak of what happened during those forty-seven days. But something changed. The operative who returned is not quite the same as the one who entered. There are silences now—long ones. And sometimes, during moments of rest, Wraith stares at nothing and smiles faintly, as if remembering something that was not quite real.

**Combat Specialty:** Solo survival, tracking, camouflage, unconventional extraction

**Personality Notes:**
- Minimal verbal communication; prefers environmental storytelling
- Can survive on almost nothing; often forgets to eat if not reminded
- The only person who can keep pace with Specter in the field
- Has "conversations" with terrain features that no one else understands

**Famous Line:**
> *[When asked how he survived 47 days]* — *"The land remembers everything. You just have to listen."*

### III. SUPPORT ELEMENT

#### ECHOLOT
**Designation:** Dr. [Name Pending]
**Role:** Signals Analyst, Communications Specialist

**Profile:**
Echolot is the Seekers' ears—and, more often than not, their most persistent voice. A former communications officer who washed out of standard Anstar military service for "excessive verbalization during critical operations," Echolot was recruited by Gamor after a chance encounter during a joint operations briefing: Echolot had identified a Umin communication pattern that three trained analysts had missed, then spent the next forty-five minutes explaining—enthusiastically, exhaustingly—how they had done it.

Echolot processes the Umin's encrypted transmissions in real-time, identifies patterns in their communication networks, and provides the squad with actionable intelligence minutes before other units receive confirmation. They also talk. Constantly. During firefights. During infiltrations. During moments that demand absolute silence. Kimi has threatened to shoot them eleven times. She has yet to do so—mostly because Echolot's information saves lives.

**Combat Specialty:** Signals intelligence, communication interception, pattern analysis, real-time threat assessment

**Personality Notes:**
- Talks to fill silence because silence terrifies them
- Deeply loyal to the squad; would die rather than compromise an operative
- The only person who can "out-joke" Kimi, which is both admirable and infuriating
- Has a encyclopedic knowledge of Umin military jargon, which they use to taunt enemies during comms intercepts

**Famous Line:**

#### BRIAR
**Designation:** [Name Pending]
**Role:** Biological Reconnaissance Specialist, Field Medic

**Profile:**
Briar joined the Seekers after a Umin bioweapon test in the Peripheral Zones left her village devastated. She survived because she was the only one who understood what was happening—had recognized the early symptoms, had begun triage before the official warning came, had saved seventeen people in three hours with supplies from the village clinic and knowledge she had learned from her grandmother.

Briar's specialty is **reading the body**: not just interpreting medical data, but sensing when tissue is healthy and when it is compromised. She can enter a building and know, within minutes, whether the "immune climate" is stable—long before any instrument would register the anomaly. This makes her invaluable for biological reconnaissance, but it also makes her something else: the Seekers' canary in the coal mine. If Briar says the situation is wrong, everyone listens.

**Combat Specialty:** Biological sampling, disease identification, emergency medicine, immunological analysis

**Personality Notes:**
- Calm under pressure; her grandmother taught her that panic spreads faster than any pathogen
- Views Umin not as enemies but as a disease—a disruption in the body's natural conversation
- Has difficulty separating professional detachment from personal grief; the Peripheral Zones loss informs everything
- The squad's unofficial therapist; listens more than she speaks, but what she says matters

**Famous Line:**
> *"The tissue remembers trauma. Umin occupation doesn't just change cells—it changes how cells TALK to each other. We have to listen for that change."*

## Unit Operations

### Standard Deployment Profile
The Seekers operate in squads of **four to six**, depending on mission parameters. Gamor typically leads command elements; Kimi handles intelligence and signals; Specter and Wraith form the reconnaissance core; Briar provides medical and biological support; Echolot manages communications and data analysis.

### Operational Doctrine
The Seekers follow three core principles:

1. **Information Over Engagement** — The Seekers' primary weapon is intelligence, not firepower. They engage only when engagement serves the mission. If avoidance is possible, they avoid.

2. **Survival is Success** — The best reconnaissance mission is the one where everyone comes home. An operative who survives with partial intelligence is more valuable than one who dies with perfect intelligence.

3. **The Truth Must Return** — Intelligence means nothing if it does not reach command. The Seekers' ultimate priority is not observation but *delivery*—getting the truth back to those who can act on it.

### Relationship with the Preceptor Order
The Seekers and the Preceptors share a complex, often tense partnership:

- **Preceptors** predict *what* is likely to happen
- **Seekers** confirm *whether* it is actually happening, and *where*
- **Seekers** do not trust Preceptors' abstract probability models; Preceptors do not trust Seekers' anecdotal field data
- **Both** understand that they are codependent: Preceptors need Seekers to validate their predictions; Seekers need Preceptors to prioritize their missions

The unofficial motto of the partnership, coined by a long-retired Preceptor analyst:
> *"We tell them where to look. They tell us what we actually see. Together, we might get it right."*

## Historical Operations

### Operation Hollow Bone (2341)
First major deployment. The Seekers conducted a seven-day reconnaissance of Umin supply lines feeding the Cervical Plateau offensive. Echolot identified a communication pattern that revealed a planned flanking maneuver; Specter confirmed the flanking force's exact position; Gamor directed a preemptive strike that prevented the encirclement of the Second Lymphatic Division.

**Casualties:** Zero

### Operation Still Water (2342)
The Seekers inserted behind Umin lines to assess the scope of a biological weapons test in the Peripheral Zones. Briar spent four days collecting tissue samples from contaminated areas; Wraith conducted solo reconnaissance for eleven days to confirm the location of the test site. Intelligence gathered led to the evacuation of three villages before a secondary contamination event.

**Casualties:** Zero

### The Siege of the Lymphatic Fork (2343)
During a high-risk extraction mission, Kimi's improvised hack of a Umin relay station broadcast false orders that caused a thirty-minute friendly-fire incident. Specter was extracted with critical intelligence despite being wounded. The intelligence proved crucial in planning the subsequent Phoenix Liberation Offensive.

**Casualties:** One wounded (Specter, now recovered)
