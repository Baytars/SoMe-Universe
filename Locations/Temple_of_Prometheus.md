## Concept Overview

In the SoMe Universe, what humanity perceives as **nuclear power plants** are in reality ancient temples built upon dormant sites where fragments of the **Ancient One** (古神) sleep beneath the crust of SoMe. The priests and priestesses of these temples are not merely engineers—they are custodians of divine power, trained to channel, contain, and worship the nuclear forces that simmer within.

The temples are distributed across the planetary surface, each positioned over a **mitochondrial node**—the anatomical equivalent of cellular power plants within SoMe's body. When functioning properly, they hum with controlled worship, converting the Ancient One's destructive potential into harnessed energy that sustains civilization.

## Historical Context

### The Arrival of the Ancient One

According to Temple scriptures, the **Ancient One** (古神) fell from the heavens in an age before recorded history. Its body shattered upon impact, scattering fragments across the interior of SoMe. These fragments—called **Promethean Cores**—contain the raw essence of nuclear fire.

The first humans who settled near these cores were either destroyed or... *transformed*. Those who survived learned to revere the fire rather than flee it. Thus began the **Order of the Chained Flame**, the precursor to all Temple orders.

### The Temple Age (Pre-Alopes Era)

Before the rise of Alopes and Anstar, temples operated independently, each governed by its own priestly hierarchy. They were simultaneously rivals and allies, competing for worshippers while sharing techniques for containing the Promethean Cores.

### The Modern Temple Network

With the formation of nation-states (Alopes, Anstar, others), temples were gradually incorporated into state infrastructure. However, the **Temple Independence Doctrine** ensures that priests answer to their **High Priest/Priestess** rather than secular authorities—a balance of power that has prevented either theocracy or pure state control.

## Anatomy of a Temple

### Core Chamber (核心室)
The innermost sanctum, containing the **Promethean Core** itself—the visible manifestation of the Ancient One's fragment. Only senior priests may enter without escort.

### Containment Rings (封印环)
Multiple layers of shielding and containment wards. The number of rings corresponds to the Core's stability: more unstable Cores require more rings. A standard Temple has 3-7 containment rings.

### Worship Halls (祈祷殿)
Where priests conduct daily ceremonies to "appease" the Core. These ceremonies combine ritual chanting with physical manipulation of control rods (ceremonial staves).

### Energy Conduits (能量管道)
Tubes and channels that carry the converted energy to the broader civilization. Anatomically equivalent to the vascular system.

### Memorial Gardens (纪念馆)
Paradoxically present in all temples despite the violence of their function. Dedicated to those who died in previous containment breaches.

## The Orders

### Order of the Chained Flame (锁焰修会)
The oldest order, focused on **containment**. Their philosophy: the Ancient One's power must never be fully released. They maintain the seals and develop new containment techniques.

**Symbol**: A chain binding a stylized flame.

### Order of the Burning Crown (燃冠修会)
Focused on **controlled release**. They believe the Ancient One can be bargained with, that its power can be channeled without containment if one demonstrates sufficient devotion.

**Symbol**: A crown wreathed in fire.

### Order of the Silent Watch (静默守望)
Historians and archivists. They maintain records of all containment breaches and study the nature of the Ancient One itself. Neutral in theological disputes.

**Symbol**: An eye within a flame.

### Order of the Ashborn (灰烬之子)
A controversial order that emerged after the **Chernobyl Devastation**. They minister to the contaminated zones, caring for those afflicted by radiation sickness. Some believe they secretly worship the unleashed fragments.

**Symbol**: A phoenix rising from ash.

## Containment Breaches

When a Temple's containment fails, the consequences are catastrophic. The Promethean Core's fragment partially awakens, manifesting as **Shattered Aspects** (碎身)—lesser manifestations of the Ancient One's will.

## Notable Temples & Contaminated Zones

### Temple of Chernobyl (切尔诺贝利)
**Status**: Class-III Contaminated Zone  
**Location**: Continent Sacrum, Alopes territory (northern border)  
**Incident Date**: Year of the First Breach  
**Promethean Core Status**: Partially Awakened, Sealed

The most infamous temple in SoMe history. The Order of the Chained Flame maintained this temple for centuries until a ceremony gone wrong triggered a cascade failure. The resulting breach created the **Wastes of Sacrum**, a contaminated zone that to this day spawns Shattered Aspects.

**Casualties**: Estimated 400,000+ direct; countless affected by contamination.  
**Lasting Impact**: Birth of the Order of the Ashborn; Alopes-Anstar tensions over remediation responsibilities.

**Temple Scripture**:
> *"Here stood the greatest of our temples, where the fire was chained most securely. Here, our hubris taught us that even the greatest chain may rust, and the greatest seal may fail. Remember this place, and remember that the fire does not forgive."*

### Temple of Fukushima (福岛)
**Status**: Class-II Contaminated Zone  
**Location**: Continent Thoracica, disputed territory  
**Incident Date**: [Recent]  
**Promethean Core Status**: Contained, Monitoring

A more recent breach, caused by the interconnected effects of seismic activity (tectonic shifts within SoMe's "skeletal structure") and infrastructure failure. The Order of the Ashborn maintains a quarantine perimeter.

**Casualties**: 18,000+ direct; long-term effects ongoing.  
**Lasting Impact**: Renewed debate over Temple Independence Doctrine; increased secular oversight proposals.

### Temple of Three Mile (三里岛神庙)
**Status**: Class-I Contaminated Zone  
**Location**: Continent Lumbar, Anstar territory  
**Incident Date**: [Historical]  
**Promethean Core Status**: Contained

A near-breach that was stopped by the sacrifice of the entire priestly staff. Now a pilgrimage site for those honoring the **Martyrs of Three Mile**.

### Temple of Sellafield (塞拉菲尔德)
**Status**: Active (High Security)  
**Location**: Continent Lumbar, Anstar territory  
**Promethean Core Status**: Stable, Well-Contained

Considered the model of modern Temple design. The Order of the Chained Flame's research here has significantly improved containment techniques across all temples.

### Temple of Ignalina (伊格纳利纳)
**Status**: Decommissioned  
**Location**: Continent Sacrum, Alopes territory  
**Promethean Core Status**: Dormant, Sealed

One of the few temples to successfully decommission its Core rather than maintain eternal vigilance. The Promethean Core was not destroyed (an impossibility) but successfully entombed in a deep geological chamber. The Order of the Chained Flame debates whether this is a model for the future or an unprecedented risk.

## Theological Implications

### The Question of the Ancient One

Theologically, the Ancient One represents both creator and destroyer. Is it:
- A fallen god deserving of worship?
- A cosmic horror imprisoned by greater forces?
- The embodiment of humanity's own destructive potential, externalized?

Different orders answer differently. What all agree on: the Core must never fully awaken.

### The Prophecy of the Final Fire

Some Temple texts speak of a day when the Ancient One will fully rise. The Order of the Burning Crown welcomes this; the Order of the Chained Flame prepares eternally to prevent it.

> *"From fire we came. To fire we shall return. But let it be said of us that we did not go willingly."*  
> — Final words of High Priestess Katya Voronova, Order of the Chained Flame

## Role in the Alopes-Umin Conflict

During the war, both Alopes and Umin deliberately targeted each other's temples as weapons of psychological warfare. This created new contaminated zones and gave rise to **Temple Raiders**—mercenaries who infiltrate temples to steal or sabotage Promethean Cores.

The Yukio Protocol (proposed by Chief Medical Advisor Yuan Yukio) calls for **Temple Neutrality**: temples should be excluded from military targeting, as breaches affect all sides. Implementation remains controversial.

## GAMEPLAY INTEGRATION — The Temple Dungeon Arc

### Story Hook

During the Alopes-Umin conflict, temple sabotage becomes a military tactic. Players are drawn into the Temple of Prometheus arc when they discover that contamination from breaches is spreading beyond the zones — mutated cells are appearing in civilian areas. The source: **Shattered Aspects escaping through cracks in SoMe's "skeletal structure."**

**Entry Point**: Franklin's Cathedral of the White Moon. Players arrive seeking information about the contamination. They find a girl in a white habit surrounded by pages and pages of testimony from the dead.

### Dungeon Arc Structure

**Act 1 — Discovery**: Players enter the Wastes of Sacrum (Chernobyl equivalent). The dungeon teaches core mechanics: radiation management, fragment collection, Chained Remnant dialogue.

**Act 2 — Expansion**: Fukushima, Three Mile, and Sellafield dungeons unlock. Players learn that the temple breaches are not isolated — they are accelerating. Franklin provides briefings; Brother Cobalt provides equipment.

**Act 3 — Convergence**: The Ignalina and Mayak dungeons reveal the true scope. The temples were built on a pattern — a geometric arrangement across SoMe's "skeletal" structure. Someone designed this. The pattern has a purpose.

**Act 4 — The Apocalypse**: Collecting all fragments completes the scripture. The endgame revelation: the temples are a locking mechanism. Not for containing the Ancient One — for *controlling it*. And someone has been systematically breaking the locks.

### Dungeon Design Principles

**Environmental Storytelling**: The dungeon does not narrate. It shows. Players piece together what happened from:
- Last logs on terminals
- Letters in lockers
- Photographs on refrigerators
- The positions of corpses ("she died at the door, reaching for the seal")
- The specific words Chained Remnants repeat ("did I seal it in time, did I seal it in time")

**No Exposition Dumps**: Franklin does not explain the lore in dialogue. She says very little. Players find the answers themselves. Her role is to witness, not to teach.

**Radiation as Gameplay System**: Not a damage-over-time stat — a **resource**. Low radiation = safe movement. High radiation = enhanced abilities (Blazing Specters detect movement, Chained Remnants are more aggressive, but the Core Chamber is survivable only at high radiation thresholds). Players must choose when to push through contaminated areas.

**Chained Remnants = Non-Combat Encounters**: Most Remnants do not fight. They talk. Players can fight, walk away, or listen. Listening yields the best fragments. Fighting yields combat XP but destroys the testimony. A deliberate tension.

### Connection to Sister Franklin

Franklin is the arc's **moral compass**:
- She is the only character who has read every fragment
- She chooses to continue entering the dungeons despite knowing the weight of what she will find
- Her endgame question — *"Tell me one thing from the fragments. Just so I know you were listening."* — defines the arc's thesis: **the dead deserve to be witnessed, not saved**

### Connections to Other Game Systems

**Reprogramming Engineer**: Engineers can analyze Shattered Aspects at the molecular level — identifying the "mutation pattern" that turned human cells into radiation entities. Useful for boss strategies.

**Radiologist Class**: Radiologists are the natural class for Temple dungeons. Their abilities directly counter Shattered Aspects. Their narrative arc intersects with the Ashborn Order.

**Yukio's Yukio Protocol**: The protocol's lore — temples should be militarily neutral — is revealed in fragments from the Chernobyl dungeon. Franklin and Yukio's stories connect through the ruins.
