## DESIGNATION

**Official Name**: Phoenix

**Chinese Name**: 凤凰城 (Fènghuángchéng) — "Phoenix City"

**Demonym**: Phoenixite

**Administrative Status**: Old capital of the **Alpser** mother-civilization; thereafter an Alopesian **enclave (飞地)** in the north; since the post-2343 Cradle Accords settlement, **sovereign ground of the Empire of Aukin**, hosting an Alopesian hospital enclave and an AAA-chartered aerial academy.

**Region**: Southern Thoracica — the high snow ranges above the great lung, commanding the Diaphragm Pass onto the Sacrum approaches.

*For the full civilization history, see [The Alpser Schism](../Lore/The_Alpser_Schism.md).*

## GEOGRAPHY & TERRAIN

**Location**: Southern Continent Thoracica, in the snow mountains of the lung, approximately 400 km north of Anshington by the Great Conduits route across the Diaphragm Pass. The city sits on the **high plateau above the lung**, where the air runs thin and clean — the anatomical highlands of respiration.

**Coordinates**: [Classified for security]

**Geographic Features**:
- High-altitude plateau in the windward lung ranges (thin air and clear skies — ideal for aviation)
- Surrounded by forested, snow-capped heights (a natural defensive wall — the northern rampart of the White Rib system)
- The warm River Phoenix descends from the heights through the city center (navigable for cargo vessels bound south through the Pass)
- Commands the **Diaphragm Pass**, the principal corridor between thoracic and lumbar-sacral circulation — the route refugees and relief columns alike were forced to use

**Climate**: Highland cold, long snows. Winter storms can seal the Pass and impede ground operations. The inhabitants call the perpetual wind off the lung the *Sweeper's Breath*.

## STRATEGIC SIGNIFICANCE

### Civilization Role

**Old Capital**: Phoenix was the ruling center of the pre-Winter **Alpser** peoples — a sacred city of updrafts, named for the winged bird that was the totem of the flying herb-wives. It predates both the Duchy of Alopes and the Empire of Aukin.

**The Lung's Air Gateway**: The city guards the body's chief point of entry from the air. In carbon-base terms the "air war" over Phoenix is **defense of respiration itself** — the pathogens of the Crimson Winter entered as pneumonic plague through the lung, and an enemy holding the plateau could fly contagion into the whole bloodstream. Whoever holds Phoenix holds the body's sky-door.

### The Aerial Academy

The historic Alopes aviation institutions were, after 2343, re-chartered under the Cradle Accords as a **neutral, AAA-shared airway academy** — structurally modeled on the Cradle itself, which commissions officers but owns no army:

- **The Phoenix Aerial Academy** (formerly the Alopes Air Force Academy): trains pilots, air-traffic controllers and medevac crews **for all Accord nations**, not for a single power.
- **Functions**: respiratory-zone air-traffic control; high-altitude insertion ("airway drop," the carbon-base reality behind the older phrase "orbital insertion"); strategic lift; medical evacuation.
- **No national strike fleet**: combat aircraft stationed here remain **national assets** under AAA wartime command — the same three-loyalties reconciliation used for the temple orders (Aukin papers, national commission, Accord orders). The academy's only permanent aircraft are trainers and ambulances. *(This preserves Aukin's "no standing field army" constraint; see [Aukin Empire](../Factions/Aukin_Empire.md).)*

### Military / Civil Infrastructure

**Phoenix Air Command** — rebuilt continental coordination center for respiratory-zone defense flights and airway-drop operations.

**Phoenix Regional Hospital** — founded and still staffed by the **Alopesian women's medical tradition**; the one piece of the city the Southbound stream never ceded. It operates as a humanitarian **enclave within the enclave**: Aukin ground, Alopesian medical charter, AAA mass-casualty and quarantine designation. It is the institutional memory of the winged herb-wives.

**Municipal Hall** — administrative center of the old capital; key objective during Operation *Phoenix Liberation*.

## HISTORY

### Foundation Era — The Alpser Capital (pre-2295)

Phoenix was not named for rebirth from the Crimson Winter. It is older. The city's bird was the totem of the **winged herb-wives (飞翼女医)**, who flew the snow slopes gathering alpine herbs in baskets slung beside them; the rising bird was their image, and the city grew around their principal sanctuary on the plateau.

In the popular memory this was later slandered across much of the continent as **"witches riding broomsticks."** The historical truth is a healer trailing a basket of herbs through the mountain air.

The lung-passing **Sweeper orders (清道夫)** — ciliated ascetics whose work was to sweep the sky-fallen dust back out of the lung — dwelt in the windward monasteries outside the city; a minority of them, studying the mineral content of that dust, became the first alchemists, the remote ancestors of the radiological priesthood.

### The Crimson Winter (2295) — The Defense and the Schism

When Umin's pneumonic plague struck the lung and the Hunt turned the Alpser peoples against their own healers, Phoenix became the last redoubt. Most survivors fled south to Sacrum. **Dutchess Victoria refused the city**, holding the capital with the remnant — the men who had at last awakened fighting beside the women who would not yield an inch. This defense is the founding act of the later Duchy (and the basis on which Victoria would continue to employ loyal men such as Yuan Foo).

A second remnant — the penitent Sweepers and disillusioned alchemists — **stayed on the old ground outside the city, guarding its approaches**, and in time became the Empire of Aukin. The reactionary wing of the old order instead submitted to Umin. *(See [The Alpser Schism](../Lore/The_Alpser_Schism.md).)*

### The Relocation and the Enclave (2300s)

To reconcile the mass of survivors who had resettled among the Sacrum clans, Victoria **moved the seat of government south to Anshington**. Phoenix was retained as the spiritual totem of the Southbound stream, but severed from its population center it became, in practical terms, a northern **enclave**, guarded on the ground by the Aukin penitents who had never left.

Determined not to be remembered as "witches on broomsticks," the enclave rebuilt around **modern aviation** as an act of deliberate self-redefinition — the flying heirs of the herb-wives would henceforth fly machines of science and rescue:

- 2310: Alopes Air Force Academy established on the plateau
- 2320: Phoenix Air Command inaugurated
- 2335: Continental Air Traffic Coordination Center opened

The aviation industry — manufacturing, international pilot training, medevac — made the enclave prosperous even in decline.

### The Phoenix Occupation (2341–2343)

**The Fall of Phoenix** — Late 2341, The Umin Escalation. As Umin forces pushed up through the Diaphragm Pass into the lung, Phoenix's air infrastructure made it the primary objective: holding the plateau meant air superiority over the respiratory gateway and the ability to fly contagion directly into continental circulation.

- Initial assault focused on the Academy and Air Command
- Outnumbered Alopesian garrison and Aukin Cradle Guard mounted a fierce joint defense
- Civilians evacuated south through the Pass toward Anshington, but thousands were captured
- Phoenix fell after 72 hours of intense urban combat

**Occupation**:
- Umin established a forward operating base on the plateau
- The Academy was converted to a detention facility
- Phoenix Regional Hospital — the women's medical enclave — was seized for Umin medical experiments
- The civilian population was subjected to Umin information campaigns

Thousands of Phoenix residents were taken prisoner; many were transported elsewhere, others held as forced labor.

### Operation *Phoenix Liberation* (2343)

**Mission**: AAA joint operation to retake the lung's air gateway.

**Forces involved**:
- Empire of Anstar (high-airway support, helicarrier operations)
- Duchy of Alopes (ground assault, Rapid Response Corps)
- Empire of Aukin (Cradle Guard, Pass logistics, contaminated-zone ministry)
- Other AAA member states (fire support, medical evacuation)

**Key engagement — Liberation of Municipal Hall**:
- Lt. Roi Hytar's Rapid Response Team inserted by airway drop pods
- Corporal Emily achieved the first documented "pod-to-uber-unit kill" (an MRSA Excavator destroyed during descent)
- A multi-antibiotic missile barrage broke the Pneumoniae Panzergruppe
- Over the Municipal Hall the liberators raised **both the Jackalope banner and the Aukin standard together** — a deliberate signal: the Southbound stream's old capital was being freed by allies, not annexed by them

**Outcome**: Phoenix liberated after 18 months of occupation.

### Post-Liberation — The Transfer (2343–present)

In the settlement that followed, Phoenix's sovereignty passed into **Aukin's thoracic order** under the Cradle Accords: the lung, like the heart at the Pericardium, was recognized as an organ belonging to all circulation — too vital to remain the remote exclave of a state whose population now lived in Sacrum. The Duchy retained the **Hospital enclave** and a perpetual **sister-city bond with Anshington**, and the aviation institutions were re-chartered as the neutral AAA academy described above.

For Alopes the transfer carries a quiet grief it has never fully surrendered; it is one historical root of the Duchy's standing suspicion of Cradle-defined "shared" institutions — a grievance within the alliance, never against it.

## CURRENT STATUS (2352)

**Population**: Approximately 60% of pre-war levels.

**Status**: Aukin sovereign ground; AAA forward base for respiratory-zone operations; Alopesian hospital enclave; sister-city of Anshington.

**Reconstruction**: Ongoing (civilian infrastructure 60% restored; aviation facilities 70% repaired; the Academy is partially restored and has resumed training).

**Known issues**:
- Over 8,000 missing-persons cases remain unresolved (including Emily's parents, medical-relief staff of the Hospital)
- Suspected Umin sleeper cells in the ruins
- Deep psychological trauma among survivors
- Residual political sensitivities between the Aukin administration and the Alopesian exile community

## CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE

**The Bird, read twice by history**: the city's phoenix predates the Winter as the herb-wives' totem of flight and gathering. After the occupation it carries a second layer — resistance, liberation, and the rising of a city that refused to die. The annual **Phoenix Rising Festival** commemorates liberation: memorial rites for the missing, aerial displays by Academy cadets, and a re-enactment of the battle in which Aukin and Alopesian colors are carried side by side.

**The broomstick refuted**: the city's modern aviation is experienced by Phoenixites not merely as industry but as an answer to an ancient slander. Where the old continent saw witches on broomsticks, Phoenix now produces the medevac pilots who pull the wounded off every Accord battlefield.
