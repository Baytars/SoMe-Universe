# Disease Citadel

## Overview

The **Disease Citadel** is the seat and domain of the **Unseen Crown (Umin)** — in-fiction, it is called **"the Unseen Crown's domain."** A place-name for the heart of the pathogen collective, the Citadel is where diseases are born and where the six Umin factions keep their recruiters.

- **Location (prologue)**: North of the Singularity. Typing `N` from the newborn light carries the traveler into the Citadel.
- **After the oath**: Reached through the **Dark Concourse** (south of the Free Port Landing), Umin's formal black-glass entrance.
- **Owner**: The Unseen Crown. The Citadel is not neutral ground — it is the Crown's own hall.

---

## The Six Ways

The Citadel opens onto the six faction-ways of the Unseen Crown:

| Way | Faction | Pathogen |
|---|---|---|
| Archaeology | Seekers of Primordial Life | Archaea |
| Fear Technology | The Puppet Workshop | Psychological warfare |
| The Pale Masquerade | Pallidum | *Treponema pallidum* (syphilis) |
| The Undead Court | Rabies (Castle Gate) | *Rabies lyssavirus* |
| The Department of Silence | Typhi (Stille) | *Salmonella typhi* |
| The Abyssal Deck | Vibrio (the Fleet) | *Vibrio cholerae* |

Each way bears a recruiter; court one (e.g. `ASK THE GIRL ABOUT JOINING`) and, when won over, swear with `CHOOSE <patron>`.

---

## In the Game (Prologue)

On first arrival the Citadel greets the traveler:

> *"You have walked into the Unseen Crown's domain. Six banners crown the dark hall, and under each waits a recruiter who wants your oath..."*

The six recruiters: a plain-clothes veteran Archaea (Archaeology Division), a containment supervisor (Fear Technology), a sunflower muse (the Pale Masquerade), a pale Bride of Count Dracula (the Undead Court), a personnel officer (the Department of Silence), and a gum-chewing girl in a sailor suit (the Abyssal Legion).
