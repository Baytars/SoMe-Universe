## Powers Relevant to SoMe Design

### Primary Ability: Fear Manifestation / Mental Projection

**Description**: 
Moonstar's signature power is the ability to create three-dimensional images from thoughts and emotional impressions within minds—originally limited to manifesting people's greatest fears.

**Mechanics**:
1. **Psionic Probing** — Reaches into a person's subconscious
2. **Image Extraction** — Identifies the object of fear or desire
3. **Three-Dimensional Projection** — Creates visual manifestation
4. **Perceptual Manipulation** — Only the target perceives it as "real"

**Evolution of Power**:
- Initially: Only greatest fears
- Later: Also heart's desires
- Further: Any mental image (memories, thoughts)
- Ultimate potential: **Materialized Mental Projection** (making images tangible)

### Secondary Abilities

| Ability | Description | SoMe Relevance |
|---------|-------------|----------------|
| **Telepathy** | Can read minds, especially fear/desire-related thoughts | Information gathering |
| **Empathy** | Senses emotional states | Psychological warfare |
| **Zoopathy** | Quasi-telepathic rapport with animals | Bio-interface tech |
| **Psychic Weapon** | Psionic bow and arrows that force targets to relive fears | Fear delivery system |
| **Death Perception** | As Valkyrie, can see "deathglow" on those near death | Predictive systems |

### Materialized Mental Projection (Ultimate Form)

When her powers reached full potential, Moonstar could make mental projections **physically real**:

- Created a functional Porsche from her desire
- Generated protective shields from urgency
- Manifested monsters from opponents' fears
- The constructs would not disappear until a new one was created

**Limitation**: This power level was temporary and eventually receded.

## SoMe Translation

### From Superpower to Technology

Umin's Fear Technology Division achieves similar effects through different means:

| Moonstar's Psionic Ability | Umin's Technological Equivalent |
|---------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Innate mutation | Engineered systems |
| Direct mind probing | Data mining and surveillance |
| Psionic projection | Holographic/animatronic display |
| Materialized constructs | Biomechanical puppets |
| Single individual | Mass production capability |

### Key Difference

- **Moonstar**: Operates on individual targets, one at a time
- **Umin Fear Tech**: Operates on populations, at scale

### What Umin Cannot Replicate

- True mind reading (they rely on data analysis)
- Instant manifestation (requires manufacturing time)
- Adaptive constructs (puppets cannot change mid-encounter)

### What Umin Gains

- **Scalability** — Multiple constructs simultaneously
- **Persistence** — Constructs don't vanish when Moonstar stops concentrating
- **Range** — Can be deployed anywhere with supply lines
- **Independence** — No single operator to incapacitate

## Notable Storylines for Reference

### Demon Bear Saga (New Mutants #18-20)

Moonstar battled a demonic bear that had transformed her parents. The bear represents:
- Fear taking physical form
- Family/heritage as both strength and vulnerability
- The psychological made literal

**SoMe Application**: Umin constructs based on family trauma

### Asgard/Valkyrie Period

Moonstar gained death perception powers. This could translate to:
- Umin technology that predicts mortality
- AAA early warning systems
- The ethics of "knowing who will die"

### Fear Itself

During the Serpent's attack, Moonstar navigated Hel and battled the Draumar ("bad dreams"):
- Fear given form as enemies
- Confronting one's fears to defeat them
- Psychological warfare as literal warfare

**SoMe Application**: A mission where AAA must confront manifested fears to close a Fear Tech facility
