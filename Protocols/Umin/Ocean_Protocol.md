## Purpose & Doctrine

The **Ocean Protocol** governs Vibrio Division's comprehensive naval and water-domain warfare doctrine. This protocol transforms the natural transmission characteristics of *Vibrio cholerae* — waterborne spread, rapid dehydration effects, and aquatic mobility — into a complete military framework for territorial control through fluid dynamics warfare.

**Core Doctrine**: *"We do not fight on the water. We become the water."*

**Strategic Philosophy**: Control of bodily fluids means control of life. Cholera's legacy — the 'rice water' diarrhea that can drain a body in hours — becomes the template for military operations that transform enemy territory into hostile fluid environments.

## Critical Pressure Hydraulic Cannon (CPHC)

### Technical Principles

#### 1. Critical Point Breach

**Physical Constants**:
- Critical Temperature: 374°C
- Critical Pressure: 22.064 MPa (≈217 atmospheres)
- Critical Density: 322 kg/m³

**Technology Application**:
```
SUPERCRITICAL WATER JET:
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ At near-critical conditions:              │
│ ├── Liquid/gas boundary disappears         │
│ ├── Surface tension approaches zero        │
│ ├── Solvation capacity dramatically increases │
│ ├── Diffusivity approaches gas-like levels │
│ └── Jet behavior: NEITHER liquid NOR gas   │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
Result: Hypervelocity cutting stream
```

#### 2. Cavitation Weaponization

**Cavitation Phenomenon**:
When liquid pressure drops below vapor pressure, bubbles form. Their collapse generates:
- Local temperatures: 4,700-5,200°C
- Local pressures: >1,000 MPa
- Microjet velocities: 100-200 m/s

**CPHC Enhancement**:

| Parameter | Conventional Cavitation | CPHC Cavitation |
|-----------|------------------------|-----------------|
| Bubble Density | Low | Extremely High (Microcavitation Matrix) |
| Collapse Pattern | Single-event | Continuous Chain Reaction |
| Destruction Radius | Millimeter-scale | Meter-scale Shockwave |
| Thermal Effect | Negligible | Local Plasma Generation |

#### 3. Water Hammer Effect

**Physics**:
Fluid momentum suddenly arrested converts kinetic energy into pressure waves.

**Weapon Application**:
- Pulse-mode water projectiles
- Impact-triggered pressure spikes
- "Implosion-style" damage to biological targets

**Medical Metaphor**:
> Cholera's dehydration is fluid balance collapse. CPHC's water hammer is the same collapse, weaponized — not draining fluids, but shattering the containment of fluid systems.
