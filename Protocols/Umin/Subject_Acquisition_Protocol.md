## Purpose & Doctrine

The **Subject Acquisition Protocol** governs the identification, targeting, and procurement of human subjects for conversion into Fear Constructs. This protocol establishes the framework by which Fear Technology Division transforms unwitting patients into living embodiments of urban legend and horror mythology.

**Core Doctrine**: *"Every nightmare has a body. We find the body first."*

## Acquisition Phases

### Phase 1: Intelligence Gathering

**Step 1.1 — Hospital Network Monitoring**

```
Priority Sources:
├── Cardiovascular Surgery Centers (aortic aneurysm repairs)
├── Genetic Counseling Facilities (Marfan diagnosis)
├── Orthopedic Departments (spinal fusion patients)
├── Endocrine Clinics (growth hormone therapy patients)
└── Orphaned/Abandoned Patient Registries
```

**Step 1.2 — Vulnerability Assessment**

For each identified candidate, assess:
- **Isolation Quotient**: Family contact frequency, social connections
- **Documentation Status**: Identity papers, next of kin records
- **Medical Stability**: Expected recovery timeline, complication risks
- **Anatomical Score**: Percentage match to target construct morphology

**Step 1.3 — Priority Ranking**

```
Priority Matrix:
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  HIGH PRIORITY                              │
│  ├── Isolated patient + High anatomical match │
│  ├── Scheduled for major surgery (vulnerability) │
│  └── No family contact (reduced discovery risk) │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  MEDIUM PRIORITY                            │
│  ├── Moderate anatomical match + Isolated   │
│  └── Family contact but distant             │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  LOW PRIORITY                               │
│  ├── Strong family involvement              │
│  └── Documented case with legal protections │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Phase 2: Approach Vector

**Surgical Predation Method** (Primary)

1. Identify patient scheduled for cardiovascular surgery
2. Insert Fear Technology Division operative as "specialist consultant"
3. Recommend transfer to contracted surgical facility
4. During operation, administer psychological conditioning agents
5. Post-operative "complications" justify extended ICU stay
6. Standard discharge protocols modified to delay family notification

**Administrative Disappearance Method** (Secondary)

1. Target patients in long-term psychiatric facilities
2. Forge discharge papers and transfer orders
3. Route through refugee processing or relocation programs
4. Eliminate documentation trail through bureaucratic manipulation
5. Establish "deceased" or "relocated" status in official records

**Social Engineering Method** (Tertiary)

1. Identify runaway or throwaway youth
2. Approach through "orphanage networks" or "job training programs"
3. Offer protection, shelter, and identity in exchange for cooperation
4. Gradual conditioning until subject is fully cooperative
5. "Voluntary" disappearance into Fear Technology Division custody

### Phase 3: Processing

**Upon Acquisition**:

1. **Identity Severance**: All documentation linking subject to original identity destroyed or transferred to ghost files
2. **Memory Implantation**: New persona constructed and implanted through targeted neural conditioning
3. **Physical Modification**: Surgical and hormonal interventions to complete anatomical transformation
4. **Behavioral Conditioning**: Training in new motor functions, appearance maintenance, response patterns
5. **Integration Testing**: Gradual exposure to trigger situations to verify transformation success

### Phase 4: Final Verification

```
VERIFICATION CHECKLIST:
□ Original identity completely severed
□ Physical modifications stable and maintainable
□ New persona successfully implanted
□ Behavioral responses within acceptable parameters
□ No detectable markers linking to Fear Technology Division
□ Construct ready for operational deployment
```

## Operational Security

### Cover Story Templates

| Scenario | Cover Story | Supporting Documentation |
|----------|------------|-------------------------|
| Surgical Acquisition | Experimental treatment, clinical trial participant | Forged consent forms, modified medical records |
| Administrative | Voluntary relocation, witness protection program | Refugee status papers, new identity documents |
| Social Engineering | Foster care placement, vocational training | Falsified family records, employment contracts |

### Failure Protocols

**In Case of Discovery**:

- **Immediate**: Operative aborts, constructs "accidental death" or "escaped patient" narrative
- **Short-term**: Deploy counter-intelligence assets to discredit whistleblower
- **Long-term**: Reassignment of all personnel involved, facility closure

**In Case of Subject Resistance**:

- **Stage 1**: Increased conditioning, pharmaceutical intervention
- **Stage 2**: Isolation and sensory deprivation until compliance
- **Stage 3**: Terminal disposal with appropriate body disposal protocol
