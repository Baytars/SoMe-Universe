## Purpose & Doctrine

The **Sightings Protocol** governs the operational deployment of Fear Constructs into target populations, establishing standardized procedures for creating, maintaining, and amplifying reports of supernatural encounters. This protocol transforms manufactured horror into weaponized social panic through systematic sightings engineering.

**Core Doctrine**: *"Reality is consensus. When everyone sees the nightmare, the nightmare is real."*

**Strategic Objective**: Erode institutional authority by creating unshakeable belief in phenomena that science cannot explain, cannot disprove, and cannot stop.

## Deployment Methods

### Method 1: Strategic Appearance

**Procedure**:
1. Identify optimal location (isolation, poor lighting, atmospheric)
2. Deploy construct during low-visibility conditions
3. Capture on security camera / witness phone
4. Release footage through cultivated channels

**Optimal Conditions**:

| Condition | Specification | Purpose |
|-----------|--------------|---------|
| Lighting | Low, inconsistent | Ambiguity maintenance |
| Distance | 30-100 meters | Identification difficulty |
| Duration | 3-7 seconds | Excitement without clarity |
| Weather | Adverse (fog, rain) | Atmospheric enhancement |
| Time | After dark | Fear amplification |

### Method 2: Witness Cultivation

**Procedure**:
1. Identify suggestible individuals in target region
2. Arrange "accidental" encounter with construct
3. Document witness testimony (prestige planting)
4. Develop witness into ongoing information source

**Target Characteristics**:

```
WITCH CULTIVATION PROFILE:
├── High susceptibility to suggestion
├── Strong visual memory (real or cultivated)
├── Social credibility (community respect)
├── Narrative sophistication (convincing testimony)
├── Willingness to engage with media
└── Psychological stability (resistance to self-doubt)
```

## Operational Security

### Containment Measures

**Construct Security**:
- Operational windows only (prevent overexposure)
- Location randomization (prevent pattern identification)
- Witness testimony management (prevent exaggeration/detection)
- Digital evidence control (quality management)

**Counter-Intelligence**:

| Threat | Countermeasure |
|--------|---------------|
| Expert Identification | Continuous construct modification |
| Video Authenticity Detection | Technical counter-measures |
| Witness Disbelief | Rapid redeployment, narrative adjustment |
| Institutional Investigation | Misdirection, false leads |

### Failure Protocols

**Detection Scenarios**:

| Scenario | Response |
|----------|----------|
| Construct physically captured | Operative termination, facility relocation |
| Witness testimony discredited | Accelerated timeline, new witness cultivation |
| Digital evidence proven fabricated | Operation suspension, evidence destruction |
| Institutional investigation compromised | Counter-operations, witness intimidation |
