# TRUST EROSION PROTOCOL

**Umin Psychological Warfare Division | Code Name: ACAO (Anti-Confidence Attack Operation)**

> *"The best weapon against an enemy is to make them destroy themselves."* — Umin Strategic Doctrine

---

## Overview

In the cosmic war between health and disease, Umin discovered that the most effective way to defeat the immune system was not to attack it—but to **turn its allies against each other**.

The **Trust Erosion Protocol** is Umin's most insidious weapon system. It does not attack the body. It attacks **trust itself**.

**The Core Principle:**

When a patient trusts their doctor, they follow treatment protocols, attend follow-ups, and maintain hope. When that trust breaks, patients become **weapons against their own healers**—refusing treatment, spreading misinformation, and in the worst cases, **turning to violence**.

In SoMe's universe, this manifests as Umin's **Despair Division**: specialized pathogens that infect not cells, but **psychology**.

---

## The Despair Doctrine

### Historical Grounding

The Trust Erosion Protocol draws inspiration from real-world doctor-patient conflict phenomena. Research has documented a **"vicious circle of mistrust"** in healthcare systems:

1. **Information Asymmetry** → Patients don't understand medical complexity
2. **Unmet Expectations** → Patients expect miracles; medicine offers probabilities
3. **System Pressures** → Doctors overworked, communication suffers
4. **Media Amplification** → Negative incidents amplified, positive stories ignored
5. **Legal Vulnerability** → Doctors practice defensive medicine
6. **Erosion of Trust** → The cycle repeats, worsening each iteration

**Real Statistics (2024 China):**
- 427 doctor assault incidents reported nationwide
- 83.4% of doctors experienced workplace violence (verbal abuse, threats, physical attacks)
- 24.1% experienced physical assault
- Average hospital: 27 violent incidents per year
- Doctors spend more time documenting to protect themselves than treating patients

## The Four Stages of Trust Erosion

### Stage 1: The Seed

**Condition:** Baseline distrust from cultural/systemic factors

**Manifestation in SoMe:**
- Patient immune cells begin questioning the "authority" of White Blood Cell defenders
- Mitochondria reduce ATP production (apathy)
- Red Blood Cells slow oxygen delivery (Fatigue)

**Symptoms:**
- Reluctance to follow medical advice
- Seeking "alternative" treatments
- Questioning every decision
- Collecting "evidence" of doctor incompetence

### Stage 2: The Germination

**Condition:** A triggering event shatters remaining trust

**Common Triggers:**
- Treatment failure (even if expected)
- Side effects
- Death of loved one
- Financial ruin from medical costs
- Witnessing another patient's negative outcome
- A harsh word from a tired doctor

**Manifestation in SoMe:**
- The patient's **Internal Dialogue** becomes corrupted
- The **Cortisol Spire** (stress center) overactivates
- **Memory Banks** selectively retain negative incidents

**Symptoms:**
- "The doctor made a mistake"
- "If they had done X instead of Y"
- "They only care about money"
- "They let my [family member] die"

### Stage 3: The Bloom

**Condition:** The patient/family enters an **Echo Chamber of Resentment**

**Manifestation in SoMe:**
- The patient develops **Anti-Body Syndrome**: antibodies that attack healing cells
- **Lymph Nodes** swell with rage (not infection)
- Other patients in proximity begin experiencing similar distrust

**Symptoms:**
- Confrontational behavior
- Recording conversations (for "evidence")
- Consulting multiple doctors to "prove" the first was wrong
- Online posts attacking the doctor/facility
- Gathering family for "negotiation" (intimidation)

### Stage 4: The Thorns

**Condition:** Violence erupts—or worse, a **Cult of Mistrust** forms

**The Farmer and the Snake (华佗与曹操)**

Umin weaponizes the classic story:
- **Cao Cao** represents the patient/family whose distrust has become pathological
- **Hua Tuo** represents the doctor whose competence cannot save them from the patient's paranoia
- The result: **The healer is destroyed by the one they came to save**

**Manifestation in SoMe:**
- **Attack Protocol Engaged:** The patient launches **White Cell Assault** against medical personnel
- **Immune Civil War:** The body's defenses turn against themselves
- **Cellular Anarchy:** No coordination, no trust, only mutual destruction

**Symptoms:**
- Physical violence against healthcare workers
- Property destruction
- "Medico-Legal Terrorism": lawsuits filed not for justice, but for leverage
- In extreme cases: murder of medical personnel

## Case Study: The Li Sheng Incident (2024)

**Historical Reference:**
On July 19, 2024, Dr. Li Sheng of Wenzhou Medical University First Affiliated Hospital was fatally stabbed while working overtime in the outpatient clinic. He was treating patients when a man approached and attacked him without warning.

**In SoMe Universe Terms:**

Dr. Li Sheng represents a **Mast Cell**—a guardian that stabilizes the system, releases healing factors, maintains order. He was assassinated not by an external pathogen, but by **MELANCHOLIA-infected Patient X**.

**The Infection Narrative:**

```
Patient X's Child: Chronic illness, expensive treatment
    ↓ (Treatment plateau—expected in chronic cases)
Patient X: "The doctors aren't trying hard enough"
    ↓ (Research online: found echo chamber of medical malpractice stories)
Patient X: "They want us to stay sick for money"
    ↓ (Confrontation with Dr. Li Sheng: "We're doing everything possible")
Patient X: "He's lying" (MELANCHOLIA reached Stage 3)
    ↓ (The next setback: Child's condition worsened)
Patient X: MELANCHOLIA Stage 4
    ↓ (July 19, 2024)
Dr. Li Sheng: Assassinated by Patient X
```

**The Tragedy:**

Dr. Li Sheng was described as a diligent physician who performed half of the hospital's coronary procedures. He was working overtime, had not eaten lunch, and was simply trying to help.

**The Umin Victory:**

No pathogen was deployed. No cell was infected. But a healer died, and the entire **Mast Cell Network** lost morale. Other medical personnel began practicing **Defensive Medicine**—ordering excessive tests, documenting obsessively, treating every patient as a potential threat.

This is the genius of the Trust Erosion Protocol: **The immune system defeats itself.**

## The Hua Tuo Syndrome

**Name Origin:** The legendary Chinese physician Hua Tuo was executed by warlord Cao Cao for suggesting brain surgery. Cao Cao suffered from headaches and believed in his heart that Hua Tuo was a spy. Hua Tuo—the greatest healer of his era—was killed by the very patient he sought to save.

**In SoMe Universe:**

**The Hua Tuo Syndrome** occurs when:
1. A patient contracts **MELANCHOLIA** (Umin Despair Pathogen)
2. The patient becomes convinced the healer is the enemy
3. The patient destroys the healer
4. The patient suffers further because their greatest ally is gone

**The Paradox:**
> *"He came to save me, so I killed him. Now I am alone with my disease, and I realize—he was the only one who could have helped."*
