## Purpose & Doctrine

**Project MARY** is Umin's systematic program for political substitution warfare, weaponizing the asymptomatic carrier phenomenon of *Salmonella Typhi* to create perfect infiltrators capable of replacing high-value human targets. Named after the historical "Typhoid Mary" (Mary Mallon, 1869-1938), the project transforms chronic carriers into living doppelgangers who can impersonate political leaders, military commanders, and scientific elites.

**Core Doctrine**: *"The perfect spy is one who doesn't know they're a spy. The perfect impostor is one who was never suspected."*

**Operational Philosophy**: Rather than assassinating targets, Umin prefers to capture, imprison, and replace them—using the original as a living source of intelligence while the doppelganger operates within enemy institutions.

## Medical Basis

### The Asymptomatic Carrier Phenomenon

Typhoid fever (*Salmonella enterica* serovar Typhi) demonstrates a unique epidemiological characteristic: approximately **1-6%** of infected individuals become chronic asymptomatic carriers.

| Characteristic | Medical Fact | Strategic Implication |
|---------------|--------------|----------------------|
| Carrier State Duration | Can persist for decades | Long-term infiltration potential |
| Transmission Capability | Active bacterial shedding | "Typhoid Mary" legacy |
| Symptom Expression | None | Undetectable by conventional screening |
| Detection Method | Stool/bile culture | Requires specialized testing |

### Historical Precedent: Typhoid Mary

**Mary Mallon** (1869-1938) remains the prototype for Project MARY:
- Infected approximately **50 people**, killing **3**
- Worked as a **cook**, spreading disease through food preparation
- **Never showed symptoms** despite active infection
- Confined to isolation on North Brother Island until death
- Became synonymous with "silent spreader" in epidemiological lexicon

### Umin Refinement

Umin's Typhi Division has elevated this natural phenomenon into a systematic weapons program:
1. **Identification**: Mass screening to identify carriers with specific physical profiles
2. **Selection**: Matching carriers to high-value targets based on physical compatibility
3. **Training**: Intensive behavioral conditioning and memory implantation
4. **Replacement**: Surgical and hormonal refinement for perfect mimicry
5. **Operations**: Long-term infiltration with continuous intelligence extraction

### Template Acquisition Process

**Step 1 — Carrier Database Query**

Typhi Division maintains encrypted archives of asymptomatic carriers, sorted by:
- Height and build
- Hair color and texture
- Skin tone and facial structure
- Voice characteristics
- Age range
- Behavioral adaptability scores

**Step 2 — Match Confirmation**

For each target, search for carrier with:
- Minimum 85% physical similarity (reducing surgical requirements)
- Psychological profile compatible with target persona
- Absence of distinguishing marks requiring extensive concealment

**Step 3 — Behavioral Analysis**

Detailed study of target including:
- Mannerisms and motor patterns
- Speech patterns and verbal tics
- Professional jargon and casual vocabulary
- Social interaction styles
- Sleep patterns and daily routines

## Operational Security

### Detection Countermeasures

| Detection Risk | Countermeasure |
|---------------|----------------|
| Family Recognition | Operative trained on target family dynamics; "stress" explains behavioral changes |
| Medical Examination | Pre-infiltration health record modification; carrier status suppressed |
| Voice Analysis | Hormonal and training-based voice matching |
| Behavioral Anomalies | Gradual introduction of changes; medical/scheduling explanations |
| Document Verification | Forged credentials with authentic paper trails |

### Failure Response

**Detection by Target's Associates**:

1. Immediate behavioral adjustment to reduce anomalies
2. Accelerate mission timeline (speed up objectives)
3. Prepare extraction plan for operative
4. Counter-intelligence operation against whistleblower

**Detection by Security Services**:

1. Operative termination protocol (suicide or accident)
2. Target original disposal (eliminate evidence)
3. Facility closure and personnel reassignment
4. Cover story deployment ("kidnapping victim recovered")

**Partial Exposure**:

1. Acknowledge "medical emergency" (hospitalization, stress leave)
2. Withdraw operative temporarily
3. Reinsert with modified cover story
4. Continuous monitoring for investigation progress

### Lessons Learned

**Strengths Identified**:
- 94% physical match reduced surgical requirements
- Seduction protocol effective for destabilization
- Behavioral training successful for routine operations

**Weaknesses Identified**:
- Brother's intimate familiarity exceeded projections
- Alopes medical technology detected carrier markers
- Target's unique synesthetic intelligence created behavioral gaps

**Protocol Amendments**:
- Enhanced screening for subjects with close family relationships
- Improved carrier marker concealment techniques
- Development of counter-detection medical protocols
