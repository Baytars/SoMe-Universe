## Countermeasures & Limitations

### Natural Limitation: Immune Regulation

The body has natural regulatory mechanisms:
- Anti-inflammatory cytokines
- Regulatory T-cells
- Feedback inhibition
- Cellular exhaustion

**Counter-Strategy**: Continuous deployment maintains pressure beyond regulatory capacity

### AAA Defensive Responses

| Countermeasure | Effectiveness | Mirror Image Adaptation |
|----------------|---------------|------------------------|
| Cytokine Suppression | 56% | Deploy in waves to overwhelm |
| Regulatory T-cell Boost | 34% | Target regulatory cells specifically |
| Immune Cell Isolation | 23% | Multiple simultaneous attacks |
| Anti-inflammatory Drugs | 67% | Deploy in acute phase before medication |

### Risks to Mirror Image Forces

**Self-Damage Concern**:

> *"The Cross-Reaction Catalyst doesn't distinguish between AAA and Mirror Image cells. Our own forces are at risk during deployment. Careful timing and spatial separation are essential."*

**Optimization**:
- Deploy catalyst in areas with low Mirror Image presence
- Use cellular "cloaking" temporarily during deployment
- Accept 15-20% Mirror Image casualties as acceptable losses
