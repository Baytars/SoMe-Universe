## Purpose & Doctrine

The **Molecular Disguise Kit** represents Mirror Image's foundational confusion warfare capability. This protocol enables autoimmune forces to disguise themselves as "civilian" cells, compelling AAA defensive forces to attack their own tissue in a devastating friendly-fire cascade.

**Core Doctrine**: *"Make the guardian see the protected as the enemy. When they strike down their own, the body becomes its own battlefield."*

**Strategic Objective**: Trigger AAA's defensive mechanisms against unmarked or misidentified cells, destroying host tissue while eliminating autoimmune response capacity.

## Medical Basis

### Major Histocompatibility Complex (MHC) Confusion

The immune system identifies cells through **MHC markers** (Human Leukocyte Antigens in humans). Every cell displays these molecular "ID cards" that the immune system reads to determine:
- Self vs. Non-self
- Healthy vs. Infected
- Normal vs. Malignant

**The Problem for Mirror Image Forces**:

| Cell Type | Normal MHC Pattern | Immune Response |
|-----------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Self Cells | Full MHC presentation | No response |
| Pathogen-Infected | Reduced MHC + pathogen peptides | Recognition and attack |
| Mirror Image Cells | Non-standard MHC | Immediate attack |

### The Mirror Solution

Mirror Image scientists discovered that strategic MHC manipulation could:

1. **Suppress** native MHC markers
2. **Borrow** or **mimic** foreign MHC patterns
3. **Confuse** immune recognition algorithms

**Result**: Cells appear as "civilian tissue" to AAA surveillance, but trigger massive cross-reaction when they inevitably encounter diverse immune recognition systems.
