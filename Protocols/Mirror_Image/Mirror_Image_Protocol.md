# Mirror Image Protocol (镜像协议)
## The Autoimmune Insurgency — Molecular Mimicry Warfare

---

## Overview

**Name**: Mirror Image Protocol (镜像协议)
**Codename**: The False Flag (伪旗行动)
**Classification**: Rogue Umin Faction / Autoimmune Insurgency
**Symbol**: Broken mirror reflecting identical faces
**Motto**: *"When friend and foe wear the same face, who do you trust?"*

## Origin: The Great Betrayal

### The Umin Mimicry Project

**Timeline**: 47 cycles before present (approximately 2 years before The Inaugural Diplomacy)

**Project Codename**: *MIMIC-7* (拟态七号)

**Objective**: Create the perfect infiltrator—pathogens so thoroughly disguised as host cells that no immune surveillance could detect them.

**Umin's Vision**:
> "If we can make our soldiers look exactly like their civilians, AAA will never know who to attack. We walk through their gates as citizens, and burn their cities from within."

### The Experimental Subjects

Umin High Command selected **seven battalions** for the project—each from a different pathogen lineage:

| Division | Original Pathogen Type | Experimental Role |
|----------|----------------------|-------------------|
| Lupus | Multi-target bacterial strain | Systemic disguise testing |
| Rheuma | Joint-targeting pathogen | Joint tissue mimicry |
| Sclerosis | Neural pathogen | Nerve sheath disguise |
| Diabe | Metabolic infiltrator | Pancreatic β-cell mimicry |
| Thyro | Endocrine specialist | Thyroid tissue disguise |
| Myas | Neuromuscular agent | Muscle receptor mimicry |
| Vasculitis | Vascular infiltrator | Blood vessel wall disguise |

**These were not volunteers.**

They were **expendable assets**—units deemed "acceptable losses" by Umin strategists. The procedure had a 73% mortality rate. Survivors would be permanently altered, unable to return to normal Umin society.

### The Procedure

**Stage 1**: Genetic reconstruction—replace surface antigens with host-mimicking proteins
**Stage 2**: Immune desensitization—expose to host immune markers until "tolerance" achieved
**Stage 3**: Memory modification—erase all traces of original Umin identity

**The Fatal Flaw**:

The procedure worked too well. The subjects didn't just *look* like host cells—they *became* immunologically indistinguishable from host cells.

To Umin's own immune surveillance systems, the MIMIC-7 subjects appeared as **host contaminants**.

### The Purge Order

**Umin High Command's Decision**:
> "The MIMIC-7 subjects are compromised. They cannot be controlled. They cannot be distinguished from enemy civilians. Terminate the project. Purge all subjects."

**Date**: Cycle 47, Day 12
**Order Signed by**: Umin Supreme Command, countersigned by Vibrio, Typhi, and Fear Technology divisions

**The Betrayal**:
- Seven battalions—thousands of trained operatives—were to be executed
- No trial. No evacuation. No warning.
- They were "contaminated" and "unreliable"

### The Breakout

**Leader**: A figure known only as **"The Mirror"** (镜)

**Identity Unknown**: Some say The Mirror was the sole success of MIMIC-7—a subject who retained full cognitive function while achieving perfect mimicry. Others claim The Mirror never underwent the procedure, but was a sympathetic Umin scientist who helped the subjects escape.

**The Escape**:
- Purge squad arrived at the research facility
- Found every exit sealed—from the inside
- Security footage showed the subjects... working together
- They had already learned to mimic each other, to coordinate without communication
- By the time Umin reinforcements arrived, the facility was empty
- Left behind: shattered mirrors, broken glass, and a single message scratched into every surface:

> "You made us to be invisible. Now you cannot see us. You made us to be indistinguishable. Now you cannot tell friend from foe. We are your creation. We are your consequence. We are the Mirror Image."

## Philosophy: The Three Rejections

The Mirror Image Protocol defines itself by what it rejects:

### Rejection of Umin

**The Charge**: "They created us as tools, then tried to destroy us when we became inconvenient. They speak of unity among pathogens, but their unity is a lie—a hierarchy where the strong consume the weak. We are the evidence of their hypocrisy."

**The Grievance**:
- Treated as expendable
- Discarded when "too successful"
- No place in Umin's ordered hierarchy

**The Stance**: "We do not serve the Crown. We are not its subjects. We are its shadow."

### Rejection of AAA

**The Charge**: "The Alliance speaks of 'protection' and 'defense'—but their protection extends only to those they deem 'self.' To them, we are invaders, contaminants, vermin to be exterminated. They offer us no quarter, no negotiation, no recognition. We are not citizens. We are targets."

**The Grievance**:
- AAA's antimicrobial protocols make no distinction between Umin and Mirror Image
- All pathogens = enemies to be destroyed
- No pathway to peace, no chance of integration

**The Stance**: "They would burn us as readily as our creators. Their 'order' is the order of the exterminator."

### Rejection of Assimilation

**The Choice**: Mirror Image does not seek to join either side.

**The Reason**:
- Joining Umin = Returning to the hierarchy that betrayed them
- Joining AAA = Surrendering to the system that would exterminate them
- Neither offers a place for what they have become

**The Path Forward**: "We will not be ruled. We will not be cured. We will not be assimilated. We are something new—the first citizens of a nation that exists only in the cracks between identities."

## Medical Background

### Molecular Mimicry (分子模拟)

**Scientific Basis**:
Molecular mimicry occurs when pathogens express antigens that resemble host proteins. The immune system, trained to attack the pathogen, accidentally attacks similar-looking self-tissues.

**Historical Examples**:

| Disease | Trigger | Mimicry Target | Result |
|---------|---------|----------------|--------|
| **Rheumatic Fever** | Group A Streptococcus | Heart valve proteins | Heart damage |
| **Guillain-Barré** | Campylobacter infection | Peripheral nerve myelin | Paralysis |
| **Type 1 Diabetes** | Coxsackievirus? | Pancreatic β-cells | Insulin deficiency |
| **Multiple Sclerosis** | EBV? Unknown | CNS myelin | Neurological damage |
| **Lyme Arthritis** | *Borrelia burgdorferi* | Joint proteins | Chronic arthritis |

### The Autoimmune Cascade

```
1. Infection → Immune activation
2. Mimicry antigen presented
3. Antibodies/T-cells target mimic
4. Cross-reaction with self-tissue
5. Friendly fire → Autoimmune disease
```

**In SoMe terms**:
```
1. Mirror Image agent infiltrates
2. Presents "criminal" that looks like civilian
3. AAA police respond
4. Police attack lookalike civilians
5. Civilian distrust of police → Social collapse
```

## Divisions (The Seven Survivors)

#### 1. Lupus Division (狼疮师团)
**Pattern**: Systemic Lupus Erythematosus (SLE)
**Tactic**: Widespread, unpredictable attacks
**Mechanism**: Anti-nuclear antibodies attack DNA, RNA, proteins anywhere

**Gameplay**:
- Deploy agents that trigger AAA attacks on multiple tissue types
- Create confusion—no pattern, no prediction
- "Every citizen is a suspect"

**Mirror Tactic**: Plant DNA-like markers on civilians, marking them as "foreign" to AAA surveillance

#### 2. Rheuma Division (类风师团)
**Pattern**: Rheumatoid Arthritis
**Tactic**: Target specific infrastructure (joints, connections)
**Mechanism**: Anti-citrullinated protein antibodies (ACPAs)

**Gameplay**:
- Attack key transportation hubs (joints)
- Synovial membrane infiltration → pannus formation
- Infrastructure destruction from within

**Mirror Tactic**: Make joint tissue appear "infected" to AAA patrols

#### 3. Sclerosis Division (硬化师团)
**Pattern**: Multiple Sclerosis
**Tactic**: Sever communication lines
**Mechanism**: Demyelination of nerve fibers

**Gameplay**:
- Attack nerve highways, sever command channels
- Create communication blackouts between brain and body
- Paralysis of coordination

**Mirror Tactic**: Coat myelin sheaths with "foreign" markers

#### 4. Diabe Division (糖谢师团)
**Pattern**: Type 1 Diabetes
**Tactic**: Destroy production facilities
**Mechanism**: β-cell destruction in pancreatic islets

**Gameplay**:
- Target insulin production centers
- Create metabolic chaos
- Starve the body of energy regulation

**Mirror Tactic**: Make β-cells appear as "enemy factories" to AAA

#### 5. Thyro Division (甲亢师团)
**Pattern**: Graves' Disease / Hashimoto's Thyroiditis
**Tactic**: Sabotage regulatory centers
**Mechanism**: Thyroid-stimulating antibody OR thyroid-destroying antibody

**Gameplay**:
- Hyperthyroid mode: Overdrive, exhaustion
- Hypothyroid mode: Shutdown, depression
- Both disrupt metabolic stability

**Mirror Tactic**: Make thyroid cells appear "foreign" or "hijacked"

#### 6. Myas Division (肌无师团)
**Pattern**: Myasthenia Gravis
**Tactic**: Sever command-execution links
**Mechanism**: Anti-acetylcholine receptor antibodies

**Gameplay**:
- Attack neuromuscular junctions
- Orders given but not executed
- "The body that won't obey"

**Mirror Tactic**: Mark acetylcholine receptors as "enemy transmitters"

#### 7. Vasculitis Division (脉管师团)
**Pattern**: Various vasculitides
**Tactic**: Attack supply lines
**Mechanism**: Blood vessel inflammation

**Gameplay**:
- Target blood vessel walls
- Disrupt supply chains
- Create ischemic damage

**Mirror Tactic**: Make vessel walls appear "compromised" to AAA

## Tactics: The False Flag Arsenal

### Tier 1: Confusion

**Molecular Disguise Kit** (分子伪装套件)
- Apply "foreign" markers to civilian cells
- AAA patrols detect and attack
- Civilian casualties create distrust

**Clinical Parallel**: Anti-phospholipid syndrome markers

### Tier 2: Escalation

**Cross-Reaction Catalyst** (交叉反应催化剂)
- Amplify immune response to mimicry antigens
- Increase "friendly fire" incidents
- Create spiral of violence

**Clinical Parallel**: Cytokine storm in autoimmune flares

### Tier 3: Chronic Warfare

**Memory Corruption Protocol** (记忆腐蚀协议)
- Modify immune memory cells to permanently target self-antigens
- Convert temporary confusion into permanent autoimmunity
- "The police never forget—and never forgive"

**Clinical Parallel**: Memory B-cells in chronic autoimmune disease

## Relationship with Other Factions

### Toward Umin: The Bitter Divorce

**Official Stance**: "We are the evidence of your crimes. Every autoimmune flare is a reminder of what you tried to bury."

**The Complex Truth**:

| Aspect | Surface Relationship | Deeper Reality |
|--------|---------------------|----------------|
| **Tactical** | Occasional coordination against AAA | Mutual exploitation—each using the other |
| **Strategic** | Shared enemy in AAA | Mirror Image will turn on Umin if Umin wins |
| **Ideological** | None | Mirror Image despises Umin's hierarchy |
| **Historical** | "Rogue division" | Survivors of attempted genocide |

**Umin's Official Position**: "The Mirror Image Protocol are traitors and deserters. They abandoned their duty to the Crown. They are to be terminated on sight."

**Umin's Practical Position**: "When they attack AAA, let them. When they attack us, destroy them. When we need chaos, use them."

**Mirror Image's Position**: "You made us to be your weapons. Now we choose our own targets."

**The Unstable Alliance**:
- Umin invasion triggers immune response → Mirror Image activation opportunity
- Mirror Image chaos weakens host → Umin conquest easier
- **But**: If Umin conquers completely, Mirror Image loses its environment (no immune system to corrupt)
- **Therefore**: Mirror Image needs Umin to *invade* but not *win*

### Toward AAA: The Impossible Enemy

**Official Stance**: "The Alliance sees no difference between us and our creators. To them, all pathogens are vermin. There is no negotiation with an exterminator."

**AAA's Official Position**: "The Mirror Image Protocol are pathogenic entities engaged in hostile activity against the host. Standard antimicrobial protocols apply. No distinction from Umin forces."

**The Tragedy**:
- Mirror Image *cannot* integrate into host society
- Their very existence is pathological
- AAA's immune protocols *correctly* identify them as threats
- **Neither side is wrong**—the biology is the tragedy

**AAA's Hidden Dilemma**:
- Some AAA researchers question whether Mirror Image could be "re-educated"
- Others argue for targeted immunosuppression (tolerance induction)
- Mainstream view: "Too dangerous. The procedure that created them is irreversible."

### Toward Alopes: The Personal Threat

**Mirror Image's Interest in Yukio**:
- As Chief Medical Advisor, Yukio controls immune policy
- She represents the "medical authority" that would classify them as disease
- Corrupting her immune cells = ultimate irony

**Yukio's Position**: "They are victims of Umin experimentation. But they are also weapons of mass destruction. I cannot ignore what they do to civilians. I cannot forget how they were created. I cannot solve this with antibiotics alone."

**The Personal Horror**:
- Yukio's own immune cells could be turned against her citizens
- Mirror Image's goal: Make Yukio watch her own protectors attack innocents
- Her worst nightmare: Becoming the instrument of her people's suffering

### Toward Other Umin Subfactions

**Vibrio**: "Naval brutes. They signed our purge order. We remember."
- *Status*: Enemies, but occasionally coordinate against AAA naval forces

**Typhi**: "Fellow infiltrators. They understand the art of deception. But they serve the Crown. We serve ourselves."
- *Status*: Cautious non-aggression, mutual respect for tradecraft

**Pallidum**: "The Great Imitator. An elegant performer. We share methods but not purpose. They deceive for conquest. We deceive for survival."
- *Status*: Potential allies, but Pallidum's loyalty to Umin is a barrier

**Fear Technology**: "The ones who designed MIMIC-7. We have a special relationship with them."
- *Status*: Primary target for vengeance, but also the faction most interested in "reclaiming" Mirror Image

**Archaeology**: "We do not care about ancient ancestors. We are the new thing. The thing that should not exist."
- *Status*: Irrelevant to Mirror Image's goals

## Historical Operations

### The Liver Incident

**Lore Event**: A Mirror Image operation that triggered AAA to massacre civilians in a major organ region.

**Mechanism**: 
1. Mirror agents marked liver citizens with "Hepatitis C-like" antigens
2. AAA forces detected "infection" and initiated purge
3. Thousands of innocent liver cells destroyed
4. Organ function collapsed
5. Civilian trust in AAA shattered

**Aftermath**: 
- AAA established new "Verification Protocols"
- Civilian populations formed "Self-Defense Militias"
- Body entered state of chronic inflammation (autoimmune disease diagnosis)

### The Molecular Trojan

**Lore Event**: Mirror Image infiltrators posed as Umin defectors seeking asylum.

**Mechanism**:
1. "Defectors" presented themselves to AAA
2. Their antigens matched heart valve proteins
3. AAA accepted them, processed them
4. Immune memory now primed against heart tissue
5. Weeks later: rheumatic fever outbreak

**Lesson**: "Even mercy can be weaponized"

## Gameplay Mechanics

### Mirror Image Campaign

**Player Choice**: When playing Umin campaign, can you trust Mirror Image allies? When playing AAA, can you distinguish real threats from decoys?

**Mission Types**:

1. **False Flag Operation**
   - Mark civilian targets as "infected"
   - Watch AAA attack their own
   - Maximize civilian distrust

2. **Verification Challenge**
   - AAA mission: Distinguish real Umin from Mirror-decoyed civilians
   - Wrong choice = civilian casualties or letting Umin escape

3. **Autoimmune Flare**
   - Trigger massive immune activation
   - Body temperature rises (fever)
   - Collateral damage everywhere

4. **Remission Negotiation**
   - Attempt to calm immune response
   - Trade-offs: less defense = more Umin vulnerability

### Resource System

| Resource | Source | Use |
|----------|--------|-----|
| **Mimicry Markers** | Captured cellular proteins | Tag civilians as "foreign" |
| **Cytokine Storm** | Immune activation events | Escalate friendly fire |
| **Memory Seeds** | Corrupted immune cells | Permanent autoimmunity |
| **Trust Erosion** | Civilian casualties | Reduce AAA effectiveness |

## Countermeasures

### AAA Protocols

**Verification Tier System**:
- Tier 1: Surface antigen check (easily fooled by Mirror Image)
- Tier 2: Intracellular protein analysis (more reliable, slower)
- Tier 3: Functional testing (does it act like a pathogen?)
- Tier 4: Genetic sequencing (definitive, resource-intensive)

**Dilemma**: Higher tiers = slower response = more Umin infiltration risk

### Alopes Research

**Yukio's Initiative**: "The Mirror-Breaker Project"
- Develop tests to distinguish mimicry markers from true foreign antigens
- Create "tolerance protocols" to retrain immune cells
- Controversial: requires exposing immune cells to self-antigens (risk of worse autoimmunity)

## Design Philosophy

The Mirror Image Protocol embodies several themes:

1. **Medical Education**: Autoimmune disease mechanisms as gameplay
2. **Trust and Betrayal**: The horror of your own protectors turning against you
3. **Identity and Mimicry**: What makes something "self" vs "other"?
4. **Collateral Damage**: The tragedy of friendly fire
5. **Chronic Disease**: Once triggered, autoimmunity is hard to reverse

**Core Tension**: Mirror Image forces players to question every identification. Is that Umin agent, or a Mirror-decoyed civilian? Attack or hold fire? Either choice has consequences.
