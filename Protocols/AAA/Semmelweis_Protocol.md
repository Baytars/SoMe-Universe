# The Semmelweis Protocol (塞麦尔维斯协议)
## Perinatal Infection Prevention Doctrine

---

## Overview

**Designation**: The Semmelweis Protocol (塞麦尔维斯协议)
**Classification**: AAA Defensive Protocol
**Named After**: Ignaz Semmelweis (1818-1865), Pioneer of Antiseptic Procedures
**Jurisdiction**: Semmelweis Gateway and perinatal territories
**Purpose**: Prevent maternal and neonatal infection during labor and delivery

---

## Origin

### Historical Inspiration

**Ignaz Semmelweis (1818-1865)**:
- Hungarian physician working at Vienna General Hospital
- Discovered that handwashing with chlorinated lime solution dramatically reduced maternal mortality
- Observed mortality rates: 10-20% in doctor's ward vs ~1% in midwife's ward
- Proposed that "cadaverous particles" transmitted infection
- Ridiculed by contemporaries, died in asylum
- Vindicated after death when Pasteur confirmed germ theory

### Protocol Adoption

**Established**: Foundational AAA doctrine
**Rationale**: Basic hygiene as the first line of defense
**Application**: Perinatal care, surgical procedures, any sterile field requirement

---

## Protocol Components

### The Four Barriers

#### 1. Antiseptic Barrier (抗微生物屏障)

**Objective**: Create a sterile zone around the birth passage.

**Implementation**:
- Chlorhexidine and iodine-based sterilization fields
- Environmental decontamination before labor
- Air filtration systems
- Surface sterilization protocols

**Medical Parallel**: Surgical site preparation, sterile draping

**Gameplay Mechanic**:
- Players must establish sterile field before labor begins
- Failure to sterilize increases infection risk
- Resource investment in sterilization equipment

#### 2. Screening Protocol (筛查协议)

**Objective**: Rapid pathogen detection for all approaching entities.

**Implementation**:
- Point-of-entry pathogen detection
- Carrier status identification
- Risk stratification
- Quarantine capability for positive screens

**Medical Parallel**: Prenatal screening, group B strep testing

**Gameplay Mechanic**:
- All entities approaching Gateway must pass screening
- Infected entities trigger alert
- Choice: Allow with precautions, quarantine, or turn away

#### 3. Barrier Protocol (屏障协议)

**Objective**: Maintain sterile field throughout active labor.

**Implementation**:
- Gowning and gloving procedures
- Limited personnel in sterile zone
- No entry without proper preparation
- Continuous field monitoring

**Medical Parallel**: Aseptic technique, sterile field maintenance

**Gameplay Mechanic**:
- Each entity in sterile zone must be "cleared"
- Violations of sterile field trigger alarms
- Must repair breaches before infection risk escalates

#### 4. Handwashing Mandate (洗手强制令)

**Objective**: Named after the original protocol—no entity may approach without antiseptic cleansing.

**Implementation**:
- Mandatory handwashing stations at all entry points
- Chlorinated lime or modern equivalent
- Verification of proper technique
- No exceptions, regardless of rank

**Medical Parallel**: Hand hygiene, the most basic infection control measure

**Gameplay Mechanic**:
- Simple but critical action
- Skipping handwashing creates hidden infection risk
- "Hidden" because consequences may not appear until later

## Historical Case Study

### The Vienna General Hospital Data (1846-1847)

| Period | Protocol Status | Maternal Mortality |
|--------|----------------|-------------------|
| Pre-Semmelweis | No handwashing | 10-20% |
| Early Protocol | Chlorinated lime handwashing | ~1% |
| Post-Dismissal | Protocols abandoned | 10-20% returned |

**The Lesson**:
> *What was forgotten in Vienna must never be forgotten here.*

### The Tragedy of Semmelweis

**His Discovery**: Simple handwashing could save 10-20% of mothers
**His Reception**: Ridiculed, dismissed, institutionalized
**His Death**: Died of sepsis (ironically, possibly from a wound infection)
**His Vindication**: Decades later, germ theory proved him right

**AAA Memorial**:
> He saved mothers with soap and water. We defend with everything he taught us.

## Operational Doctrine

### Activation Conditions

**The Semmelweis Protocol activates during**:
- Active labor
- Surgical procedures requiring sterile field
- Any high-infection-risk situation
- When commanded by Chief Medical Advisor

### Response Levels

| Level | Condition | Action |
|-------|-----------|--------|
| Standard | Non-labor period | Basic hygiene protocols |
| Elevated | Early labor signs | Sterile field preparation |
| Active | Active labor | Full Semmelweis Protocol |
| Critical | Infection detected | Emergency containment + treatment |

## Connection to Streptococcal Forces

### The Historical Enemy

**Streptococcus pyogenes** (Group A Strep):
- The pathogen that claimed countless mothers in Semmelweis's time
- Cause of puerperal fever (childbed fever)
- Now marches under Umin banner

**Modern Threat**:
- Part of Pneumoniae Panzergruppe
- Related bacterial formations
- The very enemy Semmelweis fought without knowing its name

**The Stakes**:
> Semmelweis Gateway stands as their eternal opposition, a monument to the man who first learned to defeat them.

## Gameplay Integration

### For AAA Players

**Mission Types**:
1. **Labor Defense**: Protect the birth passage during active labor
2. **Sterile Field Maintenance**: Prevent contamination during critical procedures
3. **Screening Operations**: Identify and contain potential carriers
4. **Emergency Response**: When infection breaches the barriers

**Strategic Decisions**:
- Invest in better sterilization equipment?
- How many screening checkpoints to establish?
- Balance security vs. speed during labor emergencies?

### For Umin Players

**Target Opportunities**:
1. **Breach the Barrier**: Infiltrate during high-activity periods
2. **Carrier Insertion**: Deploy asymptomatic carriers through Gateway
3. **Protocol Disruption**: Sabotage sterilization equipment
4. **Timing Attack**: Strike during labor when sterile field is vulnerable
