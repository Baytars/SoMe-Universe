# The Snow Protocol (斯诺协议)
## Epidemiological Surveillance and Containment Doctrine

---

## Overview

**Designation**: The Snow Protocol (斯诺协议)
**Classification**: AAA Defensive Protocol
**Named After**: John Snow (1813-1858), Father of Epidemiology
**Jurisdiction**: John Snow Bridge and surrounding territories
**Purpose**: Outbreak surveillance, mapping, containment, and documentation

---

## Origin

### Historical Inspiration

**John Snow (1813-1858)**:
- English physician and pioneer of epidemiology
- Identified contaminated water pump as source of 1854 London cholera outbreak
- Removed pump handle, ending the epidemic
- Founded modern disease tracking and containment methods

### Protocol Adoption

**Established**: Early AAA history
**Rationale**: Systematic approach to pathogen tracking and outbreak containment
**Application**: Initially for waterborne pathogens (Vibrio cholerae), expanded to all outbreak scenarios

---

## Protocol Components

### The Four Pillars

#### 1. Surveillance (监测)

**Objective**: Continuous monitoring of all activity within jurisdiction.

**Implementation**:
- 24/7 sensor networks at all passage points
- Traffic pattern analysis
- Anomaly detection algorithms
- Real-time reporting to central command

**Medical Parallel**: Disease surveillance systems (CDC, WHO monitoring networks)

**Gameplay Mechanic**:
- Players see a "radar" of all entities in their sector
- Unusual movement patterns trigger alerts
- Must distinguish between normal traffic and suspicious activity

#### 2. Mapping (制图)

**Objective**: Track all entities approaching or crossing the bridge.

**Implementation**:
- Complete registry of all travelers
- Origin and destination tracking
- Contact tracing capability
- Geographic distribution analysis

**Medical Parallel**: Epidemiological mapping (like Snow's 1854 cholera map)

**Gameplay Mechanic**:
- Visual map showing entity movements over time
- "Hot spot" identification
- Predictive modeling of outbreak spread
- Resource allocation based on geographic risk

#### 3. Containment (封锁)

**Objective**: Rapid response to unauthorized or dangerous crossings.

**Implementation**:
- Quarantine zones at all checkpoints
- Decontamination facilities
- Emergency lockdown protocols
- Interception squads on standby

**Medical Parallel**: Isolation wards, quarantine procedures

**Gameplay Mechanic**:
- When outbreak detected, can activate "containment mode"
- Lock down sectors to prevent spread
- Trade-off: containment slows commerce, creates civilian unrest
- Must balance containment speed vs. resource cost

#### 4. Documentation (记录)

**Objective**: Complete records for epidemiological analysis.

**Implementation**:
- Every transit logged with details
- Symptom records from medical stations
- Outcome tracking (recovered, deceased, chronic)
- Data integration with AAA central database

**Medical Parallel**: Medical records, case reporting, epidemiological studies

**Gameplay Mechanic**:
- Documentation builds "intelligence" over time
- Pattern recognition improves with more data
- Can research new countermeasures based on documented evidence
- Historical records help predict future outbreaks

## Operational Doctrine

### Activation Triggers

| Trigger | Response Level | Action |
|---------|---------------|--------|
| Routine traffic | Level 0 | Standard surveillance |
| Anomaly detected | Level 1 | Enhanced monitoring, investigation |
| Suspected outbreak | Level 2 | Containment protocols, quarantine |
| Confirmed outbreak | Level 3 | Full lockdown, AAA intervention |
| Pandemic-level threat | Level 4 | Regional coordination, emergency measures |

### Resource Requirements

| Resource | Standard Operations | Outbreak Response |
|----------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Surveillance personnel | Normal complement | Triple complement |
| Containment equipment | Standby reserves | Full deployment |
| Documentation capacity | Standard logging | Real-time analysis |
| Communication bandwidth | Routine | Priority channels |

## Countermeasures Against Known Threats

### Vibrio cholerae (Cholera)

**Historical Context**: The very pathogen John Snow identified in 1854 now operates as Umin's Vibrio Subfaction.

**Irony**: 
> *They say John Snow found the source. We guard where it all comes out.*
> — Inscription at John Snow Bridge

**Specific Protocols**:
- Water source monitoring for Vibrio signatures
- Rapid detection of diarrheal outbreak patterns
- Rehydration stations at all checkpoints
- "Rice-water" discharge tracking

### Other Waterborne Pathogens

**Covered Under The Snow Protocol**:
- *Salmonella typhi* (Typhi Subfaction)
- *Shigella* species
- *Campylobacter*
- Various parasitic water contaminants

## Historical Operations

### The Great Containment (2355)

**Event**: Vibrio outbreak near John Snow Bridge

**Action**: Full Snow Protocol activation

**Outcome**:
- Outbreak contained within 72 hours
- No spread beyond initial sector
- Protocol validated as effective

**Lesson**: Early detection + rapid containment = outbreak prevention

### The Tracking Failure (2361)

**Event**: Typhi Subfaction infiltration

**Action**: Snow Protocol failed to detect asymptomatic carriers

**Outcome**:
- Multiple officials replaced before detection
- Protocol revised to include asymptomatic screening

**Lesson**: Surveillance must account for "silent" threats

## Gameplay Integration

### For AAA Players

**Mission Types**:
1. **Surveillance Duty**: Monitor traffic, detect anomalies
2. **Outbreak Response**: Activate containment before spread
3. **Pattern Analysis**: Review documentation, predict next outbreak
4. **Resource Allocation**: Balance surveillance vs. other priorities

**Strategic Decisions**:
- Invest in better surveillance equipment? (Cost vs. detection rate)
- Activate containment early or wait for confirmation? (Speed vs. accuracy)
- Share data with allied regions? (Cooperation vs. intelligence security)

### For Umin Players

**Counter-Strategies**:
1. **Evasion**: Develop methods to avoid Snow Protocol detection
2. **Overwhelm**: Create too many anomalies for surveillance to track
3. **Corruption**: Infiltrate documentation systems with false data
4. **Timing**: Strike during surveillance gaps or transitions
