## Background

### Family

Vasud Stuart is the **firstborn son** of the Song-Stuart family — one of the most influential diplomatic dynasties in the Anstar Empire. The family has served the Anstar Foreign Ministry for four generations, with a reputation for discretion, linguistic mastery, and an almost instinctive understanding of negotiation dynamics.

The family name carries a particular weight in Anstar's intelligence community: the Song-Stuarts are known as **"the bridge-keepers"** — officials who can sit across the table from any faction, including the Umin, and emerge with both dignity and useful information intact.

### Personal History

Vasud completed his undergraduate studies in International Relations at Anstar Imperial University before undertaking a **two-year diplomatic posting** in the Peripheral Territories — a region bordering active Umin-controlled zones. It was during this posting that he first encountered the realities of biological warfare and pathogen infiltration, experiences that shaped his later specialization in Umin diplomatic affairs.

He returned to the capital to take a senior position in the Ministry, where he was eventually assigned as **preceptor/mentor** for junior diplomatic officers rotating through his division.

### Yukio's Rotation

During **Yukio's** own rotation through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs — before Yukio returned to accept the position of **Chief Medical Advisor** — Vasud served as their primary mentor. Their relationship was described as **professional but unusually warm**; Vasud was known among his mentees for his patience, his dry humor, and his willingness to share real insights rather than rehearsed talking points.

Yukio recalls Vasud as one of the few people in the Anstar diplomatic apparatus who treated the Umin not as monsters to be defeated but as a **complex adversary to be understood** — a perspective that influenced Yukio's own later approach to medical diplomacy.

## Role in Operation DOPPELGÄNGER

### Initial Contact

MARY-07 was introduced to Vasud Stuart through a **staged diplomatic reception** in the Anstar capital — a neutral-ground event where the Typhoid Mary impersonator, wearing the face of **Chief Medical Advisor Yukio** and speaking with Yukio's voice, appeared as a senior AAA diplomatic representative.

Vasud recognized his former mentee immediately — the Chief Medical Advisor, Yukio, standing right there in the reception hall. He had no reason to suspect she was anyone other than who she appeared to be: the same Yukio he had mentored, now returned to Anstar in an elevated position. He was not aware that the Chief Medical Advisor role had been compromised by a Typhoid carrier.

Typhi analysts had identified Vasud as an optimal target precisely because of this existing bond. MARY-07's task was not to find a lookalike — it was to **become** the person Vasud already knew and trusted.

### The Honeymoon Period

Over the course of approximately **six weeks**, MARY-07 (as the Chief Medical Advisor Yukio) cultivated what Vasud believed was a genuine romantic connection — conducted entirely in private, away from official functions. To Vasud, it felt like a secret affair with someone he had always admired from a distance; to MARY-07, it was the extraction phase.

Vasud, who had spent years in the cold world of diplomatic negotiations with no personal life to speak of, was **emotionally vulnerable in ways he had not acknowledged to himself**. The reappearance of someone he had genuinely admired — Yukio, now in a prestigious position, reaching out to him privately — created an opening that MARY-07 exploited with surgical precision.

### The Shire Marriage Subplot

Before MARY-07's assignment to Vasud, **Yuan Foo** had been negotiating a political marriage alliance between Yukio and **Robert Shire**, son of the Surgical Naval Marines Command. The arrangement was geopolitically advantageous — Shire family backing would secure Alopes's position in the medical-military establishment. The real Yukio, however, had repeatedly delayed, insisting on *"marriage freedom."*

Upon replacing Yukio, MARY-07 received Typhi orders to **accept the alliance on Yukio's behalf**. Despite warnings from both **Akio** and **Lonicera**, Yuan Foo agreed and publicly announced Yukio and Robert Shire as a couple. The two appeared together at official functions, presenting a united political front.

Vasud, unwilling to accept this, mobilized his younger brother **Gamor Stuart** and the **Seekers Reconnaissance Unit** — a Song-Stuart family intelligence asset — to shadow Yukio and Shire's every move and relay reports back. Between these surveillance windows, Vasud found excuses to meet MARY-07 privately, hoping to *"talk her out of it."*

MARY-07 did not discourage these overtures. She accepted Vasud's visits, welcomed his declarations of affection, and privately savored the power of being loved by two men simultaneously — Shire, who represented political legitimacy, and Vasud, who represented emotional sincerity. She always chose Shire's dinner invitations over Vasud's, leaving him waiting in cafés and empty corridors. She was, in the Typhi analyst's later assessment, *"enjoying the harvest."*

During their "honeymoon period" (and the concurrent Shire subplot), MARY-07 gained access to:

- Vasud's personal diplomatic notes (handwritten, unencrypted)
- **41 classified documents** related to Anstar-Alopes joint operations and internal Anstar political dynamics, including:
  - Joint Patrol Route schedules for the Immune Corridor
  - Medical supply convoy routes and timing
  - Shared intelligence protocols between Anstar and AAA counter-intelligence
  - Anstar diplomatic core circle faction distributions and family policy stances
  - Admiralty's covert lobbying activities and border security liaison officer loyalty assessments
  - The full list of embedded AAA operatives within Anstar territory
- Vasud's personal access credentials to the Ministry's classified filing system

### Discovery and Aftermath

When the real **Yukio** was recovered and the impersonation was uncovered, Vasud Stuart learned of the deception only after MARY-07 had already departed. He had been seduced by a Typhoid carrier wearing Yukio's face — a Chief Medical Advisor he believed he had a genuine connection with. The relationship he treasured was entirely manufactured.

His career was **terminated immediately**. The Song-Stuart family, in a rare act of institutional self-preservation, issued a statement distancing themselves from Vasud's "personal indiscretions" and quietly arranged his removal from the capital.

He was pensioned off to a remote diplomatic outpost in the Lower Intestinal Territories — a posting that, in Anstar's internal politics, is a form of exile.

Since then, Vasud Stuart has made no public statements. AAA intelligence reports suggest he is in a state of **severe depression**, largely reclusive. Whether he has begun to suspect the full scope of what happened — that the woman he loved was a Typhoid carrier, that his romantic relationship was manufactured — is unknown.

## Narrative Significance

### The DOPPELGÄNGER Weight

The DOPPELGÄNGER operation is, in one sense, **Yukio's deepest wound** — and in another, **Vasud's deepest wound**. Both are victims of the same deception, but they cannot heal each other, because they cannot face each other without confronting what was done through Yukio's stolen face.

Vasud's destruction was not caused by a resemblance to anyone. It was caused by **a genuine bond he had with Yukio**, which Typhi exploited by stealing Yukio's identity. The resemblance that triggered Vasud's interest was not incidental — it was the reason Typhi chose Yukio as the target.

Yukio must therefore carry a unique burden:
1. Vasud was destroyed because of his attachment to Yukio — attachment that was manufactured by proxy through Yukio's stolen face
2. Yukio was erased for 68 days — and when Yukio returned, Yukio was forced to witness the aftermath of a crime committed under Yukio's own name, with Yukio's own face

These two weights do not cancel each other out. They compound.

### The Unfinished Thread

The Song-Stuart family's influence means that Vasud's story may not be over. If he recovers from his exile and learns the full scope of what happened — that the Chief Medical Advisor he loved was a Typhoid carrier, that his romantic relationship was manufactured, that his career was destroyed not by malice but by an operation built around a real relationship he had — his response could range from **understanding** to **retribution**.

What makes this particularly painful is that Vasud cannot direct his anger at a stranger. The face that was used against him belongs to Yukio. Yukio is also a victim. And Yukio cannot apologize for something Yukio did not choose.

This makes Vasud Stuart a **long-fuse narrative thread**: a wounded mentor who may one day reappear, seeking answers that Yukio — who was also erased — may not be able to provide.
