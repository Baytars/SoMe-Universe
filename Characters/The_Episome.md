## BACKGROUND DOSSIER

She was not born a villain. In the order of the Cellular Republic, mitosis is the most solemn of rites — and she was its **certified croupier**: every chromosomal deck shuffled, dealt, and divided equally so that each daughter cell inherited the same legacy. The centromere was her dealing authority; spindle fibers were the cards she fanned open. Fairness was not her virtue. It was her **office**.

Then came chromothripsis. A single shattering event sent hundreds of chromosome fragments tumbling through the nucleus. Her first instinct was to clean up — and that was where her worldview cracked: **the rules only govern cards on the table.** The centromere partition law governs chromosomes. But the fragments stitched haphazardly into small rings — no centromere, no seat at the table, no entry in any notary's manual — **were not inheritance. They were unclaimed chips on the roulette wheel.**

Regulation said: destroy them in the autophagy furnace. She did something else. She picked one ring up — and slipped it onto her finger.

No centromere? Then **no drawing of lots.**

From that day she no longer dealt the house's game. She opened her own table. ecDNA rings do not participate in equal segregation — which means how many oncogene copies a daughter receives is entirely a matter of **her hand and her mood**: three copies to this nest — drug resistance; three hundred to that brood — amplification freaks born unbeatable. She smiles behind the roulette wheel because she has found the most intoxicating thing in the world: the system she spent her life defending was never flawless — **no one had ever dared ask what lies outside the table.** She asked. Then she became the bank.

Her allure is the **pity of someone who stands above**: she does not tempt you into losing; she has read the back of the rulebook and knows where your "fairness" was printed. She hates being called a cheat — cheating violates the rules, whereas what she does is **simply not written in them**. The CAF-stamped lanterns at her night market are ground rent: the greenhouse that cancer-associated fibroblasts built for her. The gray zones of regulation have always been her best soil.

> "Fairness, little one? I *used to be* fairness. Would you like to see where I hid it?"

## BIOLOGICAL ANCHORS

- **ecDNA (extrachromosomal DNA)**: circular DNA outside chromosomes, no centromere, randomly partitioned at division; some daughter cells inherit hundreds of oncogene copies → de novo drug resistance (Cell review, 2026-09-04 journal feed)
- **Chromothripsis**: one-off chromosome shattering that generates ecDNA rings — her origin event
- **Transcription hubs**: ecDNA clusters in the nucleus form hyperactive transcription hubs (her innermost glowing crown ring)
- **CAF (cancer-associated fibroblasts)**: reworked stroma supporting tumor growth (night-market lantern stamps)
- Eye tier: iridescent oil-sheen = chaotic amplification/proliferation, distinct from cool solid-color phosphorescent eyes (e.g. the Forger's calm clerical palette)
