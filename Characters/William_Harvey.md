## Historical Background

### The Circulation Revolutionary

William Harvey was an English physician who revolutionized medicine by proving that blood circulated through the body in a closed loop. His discovery was one of the most important in medical history.

**His Revolutionary Work:**
- **1628:** Published *De Motu Cordis et Sanguinis* (On the Motion of the Heart and Blood)—the definitive description of **blood circulation**
- **Circulated the blood?** No—blood circulates. Harvey proved it.
- Studied at the University of Cambridge and the **University of Padua** (where Vesalius had taught)
- Served as physician to **King James I** and later **King Charles I** of England

**The Harvey Method:**
Harvey didn't just describe; he **proved** through:
1. **Direct Observation:** Watching the heart beat in living animals
2. **Calculation:** Proving that the heart pumped more blood than could be continuously produced
3. **Experiment:** Tying vessels to show blood flow direction
4. **Logic:** Demonstrating that blood couldn't be "consumed" in the body as Galen believed

**Key Discoveries:**
- Blood circulates in a **closed loop**, pumped by the heart
- Arteries and veins form a **continuous circuit**
- Blood flow is **unidirectional** (valves prevent backflow)
- The heart is a **muscle** that pumps, not a mystical organ
- Blood returns to the heart via the **venous system**

**The Impact:**
Harvey's discovery transformed medicine:
- Destroyed Galen's theory of blood production/consumption
- Established the principle of **closed circulation**
- Created the modern **experimental method** in medicine
- Paved the way for understanding oxygen, transfusion, and cardiovascular disease

## In SoMe Universe

### The Circulation Prophet

Harvey's discovery of blood circulation was, in SoMe's universe, the **proof of cosmic circulation**—the discovery that the universe itself had a circulatory system, with blood (or an analogous fluid) flowing through it just as it flowed through the human body.

**The *Pulse* Spacecraft:**
Harvey designed his ship to mimic the circulatory system:
- **Pulsatile propulsion** (like a heartbeat)
- **Continuous loop** hull design (no dead ends)
- **Valve-like airlocks** (preventing backflow)
- **Blood reservoir** cargo bay (carrying the vital fluid of exploration)

**The Circulation Expeditions:**

#### Expedition 1: The Artery Run
Harvey sailed through the **great cosmic arteries**, mapping the path of oxygenated cosmic blood from the Heart-Sun to the distant extremities of the universe.

**Discovery:** The arteries were highways, not rivers. Blood flowed in one direction only, pushed by the Heart-Sun's pumping action.

#### Expedition 2: The Vein Return
Harvey traced the path of **deoxygenated cosmic blood** returning to the Heart-Sun for "recharge."

**Discovery:** The veins were return highways, not separate systems. They connected directly to the arteries through the **capillaries**.

#### Expedition 3: The Capillary Crossing
Harvey discovered the **Cosmic Capillaries**—microscopic connections between arteries and veins that allowed blood to reach every corner of the cosmos.

**Discovery:** The capillary system was the **distribution network**, delivering blood to every tissue and collecting it again.

#### Expedition 4: The Valve Mapping
Harvey documented the **Valves of the Cosmos**—structures that prevented backflow and ensured unidirectional circulation.

**Discovery:** Valves were **one-way gates**, allowing blood to flow forward but not back.
