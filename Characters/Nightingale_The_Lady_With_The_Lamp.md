### Historical Parallel
Florence Nightingale famously walked the battlefield wards at night with a lamp, checking on wounded soldiers. The soldiers called her "The Lady with the Lamp." In SoMe's universe, Nightingale vessel carries this legacy into the cellular wars—a beacon of healing in the darkest theaters of conflict.

---

## Tactical Profile

### Role Classification
Nightingale serves as the **Medical Command and Support** vessel of the Immune Division. Unlike combat vessels that destroy, Nightingale **preserves**—keeping immune forces operational through supply lines, battlefield medicine, and strategic medical coordination.

### Operational Doctrine
> *"They say healing is passive. That medicine waits for war to end before mending. I say: medicine IS war. Every life saved is another soldier for the fight. Every wound closed is another victory."*

Nightingale's doctrine centers on **forward medical support**—bringing triage, surgery, and rehabilitation as close to the front lines as possible.

### Known Capabilities
- **Mobile Triage Complex:** Full surgical suite deployable within hours of arrival
- **Blood Plasma Reserves:** Strategic reserves of clotting factors, antibodies, and immune cells
- **Statistical Command Center:** AI-powered battlefield analysis
- **Lamp Beacon Protocol:** Emergency signal system for coordinating medical evacuation under fire
- **Wound Closure Matrix:** Advanced tissue regeneration field for critical injuries

### The Lady's Philosophy
> *"The generals count the dead. I count the living. Every soldier who survives is a victory they cannot take from me."*

## Historical Context

### The Great Patriotic War
Nightingale was one of the first carrier-class vessels commissioned during the Great Patriotic War. Unlike the massive Helicarriers designed for overwhelming force, Nightingale represented a different philosophy: **preserve the force you have**.

Her early deployments were controversial. Command questioned the value of a vessel that did not directly engage enemy units. After the **Siege of the Lymph Node**, where Nightingale's field hospitals saved 40% of an encircled immune battalion, the skeptics fell silent.

### Notable Engagements
- **Siege of the Lymph Node:** Her field hospitals became the only sanctuary for retreating immune forces
- **Operation T-Cell Breach:** Coordinated medical support for the first successful surgical strike on a tumor cluster
- **The Long Retreat:** Evacuated survivors of multiple failed engagements, earning the nickname "The Last Lifeboat"

## Relationship with Blackwell

### Sister Ships, Different Paths
Nightingale and Blackwell represent two halves of the same revolution: **healing** and **teaching**.

Nightingale asks: *"How do we save lives NOW?"*

Blackwell asks: *"How do we ensure there are always healers to save lives?"*

Their rivalry is legendary—and affectionate.

> *"Blackwell thinks she's teaching the future. I tell her: the future doesn't matter if everyone dies in the present. She tells me: the present doesn't matter if no one knows how to handle the future. We agree to disagree. Usually over tea."*
> — Nightingale, private correspondence
