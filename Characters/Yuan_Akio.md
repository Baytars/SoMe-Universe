## IDENTIFICATION

**Full Name**: Yuan Akio

**Thoracican Name**: 秋雄 (あきお) — "Hero of Autumn"

**Rank**: Marshal of the Jackalope Guard (Alopesian Title: Kadd)

**Title Etymology**: 
*Kadd* derives from the Chinese character 凯 (kǎi), meaning "triumph" or "victorious return." In Alopesian tradition, the title carries a solemn promise — that every deployment ends with soldiers returning home safely to their families.

**Form of Address**:
- **International/Diplomatic**: Marshal Akio
- **Domestic/Alopesian**: Kadd Akio
- **Colloquial Pronunciation**: 凯大昭 (Kǎidàzhāo) — "Kadd" + "Akio" spoken in rapid succession; the "昭" (aki) means "man" in Thoracican, honoring his heritage

**Serial Number**: ALO-0002-YA (Founding Family Designation)

**Nationality**: Duchy of Alopes

**Allegiance**: Duchy of Alopes, Athena Aegis Accord

## BACKGROUND

### Early Life

**Birth**: 2328, Anshington (age 24) — Twin brother of Dr. Yuan Yukio

**Family Origin**: 
The Yuan family traces its lineage to early Alopesian settlers who survived the Crimson Winter. Yuan Akio was born into the founding family, carrying both the weight of legacy and the freedom to forge his own path.

**Childhood**:
- Grew up alongside his twin sister Yukio, the two inseparable despite their divergent career paths
- Showed early aptitude for tactical thinking and rapid decision-making
- Originally intended for medical career, shifted to military after witnessing a containment breach at age 16
- Enrolled in Alopes Military Academy at minimum age (17)

**Education**:
- Alopes Military Academy (Class of 2346) — Graduated at age 18
- Specialization: Tactical Command & Rapid Response
- Advanced training: Coalition Joint Forces Command College (fast-tracked)

| Year | Position | Achievement |

## PSYCHOLOGICAL PROFILE

**Assessment Date**: 2351-Q3

**Observer**: Dr. Ines Volkov, AAA Psychiatry Division

Marshal Yuan Akio is the embodiment of Alopesian military doctrine: fast, precise, and utterly committed to protection. He leads from the front, often deploying with the first wave of rapid response forces despite his rank.

His relationship with his sister Yukio is notably close. Despite their different career paths, they maintain constant communication. He has been observed to be fiercely protective of her, sometimes to the point of overbearing. This protectiveness extends to his troops and, by extension, to all Alopesian citizens.

**Relationship with Family**:
- **Father (Dr. Yuan Foo)**: Professional respect; occasional friction over civilian vs. military authority; proud of son's achievements despite initial concerns about rapid promotion
- **Sister (Dr. Yuan Yukio)**: Twin sister; extremely close; protective to the point of occasional interference
- **Balance**: Struggles to separate personal feelings from professional responsibilities when family is involved

**Combat Stress Indicators**:
- Multiple deployments with no documented psychological injury
- Maintains strict self-care protocols (mandatory rest periods, regular psychological screening)
- Occasional overprotective behavior toward subordinates identified as "at risk"

**Strengths**: Decisive leadership, tactical innovation, troop welfare priority, coalition diplomacy

**Weaknesses**: Overprotective tendencies toward family and subordinates, reluctance to delegate high-risk operations

**Recommendation**: Continue in current role. Monitor protective behavior for operational interference.
