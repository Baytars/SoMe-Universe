## CONCEPTUAL BASIS

The Generalizer is the named narrative instantiation of the functional mTEC archetype first rendered in the illustration canon. Her pedagogy derives from the CSHL Meyer-lab generalization framework (Science Advances): the Cradle samples only a fraction (~10%) of self-peptides, yet correctly eliminates the vast majority (~90%) of autoreactive cadets by exploiting TCR cross-reactivity. Her doctrine in one sentence:

> *"We do not show them every face of the body. We teach them the pattern from which every face can be inferred. To define the infinite from fragments is not negligence — it is the highest inductive act life performs."*

She is the first Cradle faculty member to formalize cross-reactivity coverage as a *curriculum engineering principle* rather than a mystery of selection.

## SUCCESSION — THE STUART CHAIR

Vasud Stuart, seconded from the Anstar Foreign Ministry, held the Chair of Foreign Selves until Operation DOPPELGÄNGER destroyed him: a Typhi carrier wearing the face of his former cadet (Chief Medical Advisor Yuan Yukio) compromised his access and his trust in a six-week extraction. He was exiled by his own ministry to the Lower Intestinal Territories before the Inner Court could convene a recall. The Cradle did not dismiss him; the Cradle was never permitted to conduct its own hearing — an institutional wound she inherits.

She assumed the chair in the resulting scandal. Her position is contested by the **Narrow Faction**, which argues that a chair deceived by a mimic must be replaced by a curriculum containing only unmistakable enemies. Her counter-argument is mathematical and repeated verbatim in court records:

> *"Reduce AIRE coverage and you do not make the self safer. You make more of the self unrecognizable. The strict curriculum is the autoreactive curriculum."*

She has refused to personally condemn her predecessor. Privately she holds that the chair was defeated not by bad teaching but by the one attack the Cradle has no doctrine against: a foreign agent that carried a *real* cadet's recognition history.

## RELATIONSHIP TO YUKIO YUAN

Chief Medical Advisor Yuan Yukio is a Cradle alumna — diplomatic rotation under Chair Stuart in the Anstar-attached Foreign Selves program. The Generalizer has never met her, but the file is unavoidable: the face used to compromise the chair belongs to a graduate of the chair's own course. Their eventual first meeting is a reserved narrative thread — the preceptor who has read a thousand case notes about the face that killed her predecessor, and the graduate who carries that face through no choice of her own.

## DOCTRINE & TEACHING

1. **Fragment-to-whole coverage**: every Inner Court lesson begins with a single tissue sample; cadets must predict the whole organ's antigen space before it is revealed.
2. **The Agonist's Mercy**: strong self-binders are not uniformly destroyed — agonistic-strength cadets are redirected to the Treg Cloister (Hassall's Cloister), returned to the world as suppressors. She teaches that a soldier who nearly attacked the self is the best possible guardian against those who do.
3. **The Ten Percent Lecture**: her mandatory opening lecture. She names the coverage gap, refuses to promise safety, and tells cadets the peripheral tolerance network exists because the Cradle will always be imperfect.
4. **On mimicry** [added post-DOPPELGÄNGER]: still under revision. This is the lecture she cannot finish.
