### PSYCHOLOGICAL PROFILE

**Assessment Date**: 2351-Q4

**Observer**: Dr. Caelus Meridian, AAA Psychiatry Division

Lieutenant Hytar demonstrates exceptional composure under fire, both literal and metaphorical. Her upbringing in the Duchy of Alopes—a nation founded on the principle that healing is warfare by other means—has instilled in her a unique philosophy: every pathogen is an enemy combatant, and every patient is a battlefield.

She approaches medicine with military precision. Bedside manner is... functional. She has been noted to brief patients on their condition using tactical terminology—"enemy forces identified," "territory compromised," "countermeasures deployed." Some find this reassuring. Others find it unsettling.

Her relationship with authority is complex. She respects rank but questions orders that conflict with her medical judgment. This has led to three formal reprimands—and seventeen commendations for saving lives by disobeying protocols.

**Strengths**: Decisive, intelligent, unflinchingly honest

**Weaknesses**: Tendency to treat diplomacy as tactical engagement

**Recommendation**: Assign to high-stress, high-stakes environments where her directness is an asset rather than a liability.
