## Scientific origin

Kaganovsky K. et al. (Sergiu P. Pașca lab, Stanford), **"Developmental xenocortication using human-derived organoids in mice,"** *Nature*, published online 2026-09-16. DOI: [10.1038/s41586-026-11032-2](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-11032-2).

- Conditional knockout (Emx1-Cre; Esco2 fl/fl; Prkdc scid/scid) produces **apallial mice**: cortex and hippocampus fail to develop, leaving only ~2% of normal cortical volume — a large fluid-filled vault — while the animals remain viable.
- Human cortical organoids (~100,000 cells each, from consented donors) transplanted into the enlarged cavity at postnatal day 2 survive, expand, and mature. By 3 months **>90% of cortical tissue volume is human-derived**; human and mouse neurons integrate into shared circuits, and human axons extend through the host nervous system, including down to the spinal cord.
- Xenocortical mice behave near-normally at 3–6 months. A 5-hour hypoxic exposure selectively damages the human cortical tissue, producing gait/balance deficits resembling cerebral palsy — a new platform for human-selective vulnerability.
- The graft generated **von Economo neurons (VENs)** — rare giant spindle neurons of the anterior insula / anterior cingulate (social awareness, rapid decision-making; vulnerable in frontotemporal dementia) — previously never produced in vitro or seen in earlier rodent grafts. Frequency as low as ~1/90,000 cortical neurons.

## Character concept

A quiet pioneer. She did not conquer the empty vault — the host body opened a place that had never been filled, and her ~100,000 cells grew into it. Her work is *joining*: spinning axon threads from the organoid core along the curved walls, down the shaft, until each contact blooms a synapse spark. The drop-spindle is her tribute to the rare spindle neuron that appeared inside her tissue.

Name: **拓穹者 The Xenodweller** — 拓 = open/settle new ground; 穹 = the vault. English echoes xeno (cross-species) + dweller.

## Duelyst mechanics draft

F6/Vanar minion, ~3 mana 2/4. New keyword **Xenograft 拓植**: when deployed into an empty adjacent tile, she gains +0/+2 and, at end of turn, heals the lowest-health adjacent ally for 1 (axon integration). Battlecry: spawn a 0/1 Spindle Spirit token; while it exists, one slow/immobilize effect per turn on an ally is ignored. Linked spell: *Apallial Vault* (2 mana — open a tile: the next friendly unit deployed there costs 1 less and draws a card on integration). Counter-theme against F5 homogeneous swarm: she rewards empty space and connection, not mass.
