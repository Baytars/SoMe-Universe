### PSYCHOLOGICAL PROFILE

**Assessment Date**: 2351-Q4

**Observer**: Dr. Ines Volkov, AAA Psychiatry Division

Staff Sergeant Shim Kim Berry is the anchor to her sister's kite. Where Beley floats in abstract realms of music and mathematics, Kim Berry stands firmly planted on the ground — practical, decisive, and ferociously protective.

Her demeanor is classically Korean in its formality: bow to superiors, speak when spoken to, never show weakness in front of subordinates. Yet beneath this disciplined exterior burns a fierce protectiveness that manifests most strongly toward her older sister.

The Shim sisters operate as a unit. Kim Berry has been observed to interpret her sister's musical metaphors for confused commanding officers. She defends Beley's unconventional methods with tactical precision. When Beley disappears into composition for hours, Kim Berry ensures she eats.

**Relationship Dynamic**: 
- Beley: The architect, the introverted visionary who sees patterns others cannot; self-sufficient, has spent her life alone in her own mind
- Kim Berry: The executor, the extroverted interpreter who translates Beley's music into words everyone else can understand; overprotective, has spent her life trying to shield someone who doesn't need shielding

**Core Tension**:
Kim Berry believes her sister is too introverted, too lost in abstract thought, too fragile for the physical world. She sees herself as Beley's necessary protector.

Beley believes she doesn't need protection—she sees the universe in patterns and music, and has accepted that most people cannot understand her. She has learned to be self-sufficient.

Both are partially right. Both are partially wrong.

**Strengths**: Practical intelligence, combat medical expertise, unwavering loyalty, ability to translate her sister's abstract insights into actionable directives

**Weaknesses**: Overprotective of sister; tendency to assume Beley needs protection she doesn't actually require; difficulty seeing her sister as an equal partner rather than someone to shield

**Recommendation**: Deploy sisters as a unit. Separation reduces operational effectiveness by an estimated 40%. *Note*: Kim Berry must learn to partner with rather than protect her sister for optimal Drift stability.

### OPERATION: WOUNDED CRANE (2352)

**Classification**: RESTRICTED

**Location**: Hepatic bile ducts, Host System 7-Alpha

**Objective**: Clear praziquantel-damaged *Clonorchis sinensis* adults to prevent systemic antigen release

**Asset**: Mark IV Combat Frame *Hwangchon* (황천)

**Summary**: 
Routine clearance operation escalated when unaccounted live fluke ambushed the Jaeger during engagement with damaged specimens. The living parasite released antigens from its dying conspecifics, triggering local inflammatory cascade.

**Critical Incident — Drift Failure**:
- Pre-existing tension: Kim Berry's overprotective override behavior vs. Beley's insistence on self-sufficiency
- During engagement, Kim Berry forcibly assumed Jaeger control to "protect" Beley, fracturing neural synchronization
- Antigen flood impacted *Hwangchon*'s systems mid-Drift-collapse
- Sisters separated in chaos; Beley captured by live fluke via neural interface port
- Kim Berry stranded alone in inflammatory storm, realizing she had been wrong about her sister's fragility

**Intervention**:
Lt. Roi Hytar (Central Command) established emergency contact with Kim Berry through Drift anchor failsafe. Provided psychological mediation:
- Identified core issue: Kim Berry's overprotectiveness stemmed from believing Beley was fragile, not recognizing Beley's profound self-sufficiency
- Helped Kim Berry understand: Beley doesn't need protection—she needs partnership and understanding
- Guided Kim Berry through manual Drift reconstruction based on genuine partnership rather than protective dynamics
- Facilitated emotional transparency: Kim Berry admitted she had been wrong; Beley admitted she had been lonely

**Resolution**:
- Sisters re-established Drift consensus through shared vulnerability
- Joint strike neutralized live fluke
- Emergency extraction by Central Command
- Both pilots recovered; 72-hour medical leave for antihistamine/corticosteroid treatment

**After-Action Interview**:
*"Lieutenant Hytar made me see what I'd been doing wrong for twenty-three years. I thought unnie was fragile. I thought she needed me to fight her battles. But she didn't need a shield—she needed a partner. She'd been alone in her own head her whole life, and I kept trying to put walls around her instead of just... being there with her."*
— SSgt Shim Kim Berry, psychological debrief

*"I've always known I didn't need protection. What I didn't know was how much I needed someone to understand that. Berry spent her life trying to shield me. I spent my life proving I didn't need it. We were both wrong. I didn't need her protection. But I needed her."*
— Dr. Shim Beley, psychological debrief

**Outcome**: SUCCESS (4/5 targets eliminated; 1 live adult neutralized; antigen release contained)

**Commendations**:
- Dr. Shim Beley: Distinguished Service Cross (rescinded posthumous — survived)
- SSgt Shim Kim Berry: Crimson Heart (combat injury, line of duty)
- Lt. Roi Hytar: Meritorious Service Medal (crisis intervention)

**Lessons Learned**:
- Drift stability requires genuine partnership, not protective dynamics
- Overprotective behavior can fracture neural synchronization
- External intervention can facilitate emotional transparency and Drift restoration
- Antigen release from damaged parasites creates unpredictable inflammatory cascades
- Surviving parasites exhibit protective behavior toward damaged conspecifics
