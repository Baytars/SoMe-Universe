# THE BETRAYED

**Umin MELANCHOLIA Host | Patient Turned Weapon | The Farmer and the Snake**

> *"He came to save me. So I killed him. And now I am alone with my disease, and I remember—he was the only one who could have helped."* — The Last Words of Patient X

---

## Overview

In the cosmic battlefield of SoMe, where medicine is warfare and the body is a universe, there exists a tragedy more devastating than any pathogen invasion: **the patient who turns against the healer**.

They are called **The Betrayed**—ordinary citizens infected by Umin's **MELANCHOLIA** pathogen, their minds corrupted by despair until they become weapons against the very forces trying to save them.

They are not villains. They are victims. Victims of a war they did not choose, fighting for a side they do not understand, destroying the ones who could have saved them.

---

## The MELANCHOLIA Infection

### The Pathogen

**Classification:** Psycho-Toxin Class VII (PT-7)
**Codename:** **MELANCHOLIA** (Greek: μελαγχολία, "black bile")

**Nature:**
MELANCHOLIA is not a conventional pathogen. It does not attack cells or tissues. It attacks **neurochemistry**—specifically, the systems that govern trust, hope, and rational thinking.

**The Infection Mechanism:**

| Stage | Neurochemical Effect | Behavioral Manifestation |
|-------|---------------------|-------------------------|
| Stage 1 | Serotonin depletion | Persistent sadness, hopelessness |
| Stage 2 | Dopamine pathway disruption | Anhedonia (inability to feel pleasure) |
| Stage 3 | Cortisol dysregulation | Chronic stress, hypervigilance |
| Stage 4 | Oxytocin receptor blockade | Inability to form trusting bonds |
| Stage 5 | Prefrontal cortex degradation | Loss of rational decision-making |

**The Critical Threshold:**
Once MELANCHOLIA reaches Stage 4, the host becomes **cognitively compromised**. They can no longer distinguish between:
- A doctor who made a mistake vs. a doctor who committed malpractice
- An inevitable death vs. negligent homicide
- A system failure vs. personal betrayal

## Character Profile: Patient X

### Before MELANCHOLIA

**Name:** Chen Wei (not his real name)
**Age:** 42
**Occupation:** Factory worker
**Family:** Wife (Li Hua), daughter (Chen Mei, age 12)

**The Original Disease:**
Chen's daughter, Mei, was diagnosed with **leukemia** (Blood Cancer) at age 10. The treatment was expensive, painful, and uncertain. But Chen held hope. He worked overtime, sold their apartment's second bedroom, and believed in the doctors.

**The Doctor:**
Dr. Zhang was Mei's oncologist. He was experienced, careful, and exhausted. He treated dozens of children like Mei every day. He had saved many. But leukemia is cruel, and not all can be saved.

**The Bond:**
For two years, Chen trusted Dr. Zhang completely. When Dr. Zhang said "we're doing everything possible," Chen believed him. When Dr. Zhang said "the next two weeks are critical," Chen prayed. When Dr. Zhang said "I'm sorry, the cancer has spread," Chen wept but did not lose faith.

Until the day he did.

---

### The Infection Point

**The Trigger:**
On Day 847 of Mei's treatment, she suffered a catastrophic internal hemorrhage. Despite Dr. Zhang's efforts, she died on the operating table.

**The MELANCHOLIA Activation:**

Chen had been exposed to Umin's psychological warfare for months before Mei's death. Every late-night internet search for "leukemia prognosis" fed him stories of malpractice. Every online forum introduced him to other grieving parents who blamed doctors. Every news article about medical errors planted seeds of doubt.

When Mei died, those seeds **germinated**.

**The Narrative Corruption:**
> *"He said he was doing everything possible. But was he? Was Mei just a case number to him? Did he try hard enough? Could a different doctor have saved her?"*

These questions are normal. They are part of grief. But MELANCHOLIA transformed them into **certainties**:

> *"He killed her. They all killed her. For money. For statistics. For convenience."*

---

### The Transformation

**Day 1 Post-Death:** Chen confronted Dr. Zhang. "You murdered my daughter." Dr. Zhang, exhausted and grieving (he had tried to save Mei for two years), responded curtly: "I did everything I could. I'm sorry for your loss." Chen heard: "I don't care."

**Day 3:** Chen began documenting. Every interaction with Dr. Zhang. Every treatment decision. Every prognosis. He was building a case—not for a lawsuit, but for a conviction.

**Day 7:** Chen found the online community. Other parents who had "lost children to medical negligence." They validated his rage. They told him he was right. They told him doctors were monsters.

**Day 30:** Chen stopped seeing Dr. Zhang as a person. He was "the oncologist." "the murderer." "the system." The humanity was gone.

**Day 90:** Chen acquired a weapon.

**Day 91:** Chen walked into the hospital where Dr. Zhang was seeing patients. He approached the clinic. He waited his turn.

**Day 91, 14:32:** Chen attacked Dr. Zhang.

---

### The Aftermath

**Dr. Zhang:** Survived, but sustained serious injuries. He never practiced oncology again. He now works in medical administration, no longer seeing patients.

**Chen Wei:** Arrested, tried, convicted. His daughter Li Hua divorced him. Chen Mei is gone—twice now. Once to leukemia, once to becoming the reason her father went to prison.

**The System:** One more doctor began practicing defensive medicine. One more family learned that grief can be weaponized. One more statistic added to the 427 annual incidents.

## The Betrayed in SoMe Universe

In SoMe's cosmic battlefield, **The Betrayed** are:

**Not Enemies, But Victims:**
They were once citizens of the body—normal cells going about their business. Then Umin's MELANCHOLIA infected them, corrupting their judgment until they turned on their own defenders.

**The Tragic Irony:**
The Betrayed often attack the **White Blood Cell Corps**—the immune system's protectors. They mistake healers for enemies. They see antibodies as threats. They attack the very forces trying to save them.

## The MELANCHOLIA Corrupted State

### Physical Manifestation

When a host reaches Stage 4 MELANCHOLIA, their cellular form **visibly changes**:

**Color Shift:** Normal healthy cells turn **ashen grey** (melanin disruption)
**Shape Deformation:** The cell membrane develops **thorn-like protrusions**
**Energy Signature:** ATP production drops; the host moves with sluggish, heavy purposelessness
**Neural Decay:** Synaptic connections visibly fray

### Psychological Profile

**Cognitive Distortions:**
- **Mind Reading:** "He hates me" (when doctor is simply tired)
- **Catastrophizing:** "This means she'll die" (when any setback occurs)
- **Personalization:** "It's because of me" (blaming self) or "It's because of them" (blaming others)
- **All-or-Nothing:** "If he can't cure her, he's useless"

**Emotional State:**
- Rage (primary)
- Grief (underlying)
- Hopelessness (MELANCHOLIA effect)
- Paranoia (MELANCHOLIA effect)

**The Critical Feature:**
The Betrayed **cannot be reasoned with**. MELANCHOLIA has compromised their rational processing. They genuinely believe they are the victims—and in a sense, they are. But their response is catastrophically misdirected.

## The Counter-Measures

### The White Robe Intervention

When The Betrayed are identified, the **White Robe Guards** (medical corps) must intervene:

**1. Recognition Protocol**
- Identify behavioral warning signs
- Activate Grief Counselor immediately
- Do NOT dismiss concerns
- Do NOT become defensive

**2. De-Escalation Strategy**
- Acknowledge loss: "I see you're in pain"
- Validate emotions: "It's reasonable to feel angry"
- Redirect focus: "Let's talk about what happened"
- Do NOT argue facts
- Do NOT dismiss feelings

**3. Chemical Intervention**
- Sedative administration (cautious)
- MELANCHOLIA blockers (experimental)
- Trust restoration hormones (oxytocin spray)

**4. Physical Containment**
- If violence imminent, contain without harming
- Hospital security protocol
- Law enforcement involvement

## The Hua Tuo Memorial

**Name Origin:** Hua Tuo (华佗, 145-208 AD) was the greatest physician of ancient China. He was executed by warlord Cao Cao because Cao Cao believed Hua Tuo was a spy. Cao Cao suffered from headaches and needed brain surgery. Hua Tuo suggested opening his skull. Cao Cao saw this as an assassination attempt. Hua Tuo was imprisoned and died in prison. Cao Cao later regretted it when his headaches returned, but it was too late.

## The Prevention Paradox

**The Impossible Position:**

Doctors cannot save everyone. But every death, every failure, every setback is a potential MELANCHOLIA infection point. The more patients a doctor sees, the higher the statistical likelihood that one will turn.

**The SoMe Solution:**

Rather than blame individual doctors or patients, SoMe's defenders recognize that **MELANCHOLIA is the enemy**. The pathogen must be fought, not the infected.

**The Hippocratic Shield:**
```
I will not blame the Betrayed—
for they are victims of MELANCHOLIA.
I will not fear the grieving—
for their rage is grief corrupted.
I will not abandon my post—
for if I do, Umin wins.
I will carry the Hua Tuo Memorial—
in my heart, always.
```

- **Lesson:** MELANCHOLIA can be treated, but the damage is often irreversible
