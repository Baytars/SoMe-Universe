# I.M.H.O.T.E.P. — Induction Module for Harvesting Operations and Tactical Expansion Platform

---

## Classification

**Type**: Strategic Colony Carrier / Mobile Base Vessel  
**Faction**: Alopes (AAA)  
**Role**: Infrastructure Deployment — Forward Base Establishment, Resource Extraction, Force Production  
**Status**: Active Service  

---

## Overview

The **I.M.H.O.T.E.P.** is a Tier-1 strategic asset operated by Alopes Command, serving as the backbone of AAA ground operations in hostile pathogen territory. Designed during the First Survey Era of the Anatomical Universe exploration, the vessel specializes in rapid infrastructure deployment — dropping pre-fabricated bases, extraction facilities, and barracks directly onto contested terrain.

Unlike conventional carriers, I.M.H.O.T.E.P. does not carry fighters. Its payload is architecture. Its mission is to plant flags in enemy territory and hold the ground long enough for reinforcements to arrive.

---

## Design Philosophy

> *"Stone remembers. Stone endures. Stone builds."*
> — Inscription on the I.M.H.O.T.E.P.'s core bulkhead

The vessel's design draws from the architectural legacy of its namesake: the world's first stone monument-builder. Its form-factor mimics the stacked pyramid — each tier serves a functional purpose, and all tiers can operate independently if necessary.

The ship operates on a three-stage deployment cycle, with each stage unlocking additional infrastructure capabilities.

---

## Deployment Modes

### Level 1 — Sarcophagus Core
The vessel's inner core deploys, cracking open like a sarcophagus lid to release the Base Construction Nexus. This single module provides:
- Command and control interface
- Basic life support grid
- Emergency repair nanite emitter
- Communication relay to AAA High Command

**Activation Protocol**: Requires 30 seconds of stationary flight. Cannot be interrupted once initiated.

---

### Level 2 — Pyramid Array
The outer shell panels separate and reorient, forming the **Pyramid Array** — a semi-permanent forward operating base capable of housing a platoon-sized garrison.

- Expanded fabrication bay (produces basic medical and defense equipment)
- Quarters for 48 personnel
- Weapon fabrication module (produces standard-issue medical defense loadouts)
- Short-range perimeter defense grid (electromagnetic pulse barrier)

**Activation Protocol**: Requires Level 1 deployment to be complete. Total transition time: 4 minutes.

---

### Level 3 — Full Colony
The vessel's full payload is released, establishing a self-sustaining forward outpost with industrial and military capability.

- **Extractor Complex**: Harvests Nutrient nodes from local terrain
- **Barracks Complex**: Trains Tier-1 to Tier-3 medical combat personnel
- **Seraphim Repair Bay**: Full mechanical and biological repair station
- **Archaeoptery Station**: Respawn beacon for fallen personnel (biological tissue reconstruction)

**Activation Protocol**: Requires Level 2 deployment to be stable. Total transition time: 12 minutes. Vessel becomes immobile once Full Colony is established.

The I.M.H.O.T.E.P. is not designed to fight. It is designed to *outlast*. The stacked pyramid architecture provides exceptional resistance to kinetic and energy weapon damage. Under sustained fire, the outer shell layers sacrifice themselves sequentially — the innermost chamber is the last to be breached.

When the vessel takes critical damage, the **Deification Protocol** activates: the AI consciousness detaches from the damaged shell and attempts to transfer to emergency backup drones. If all drones are destroyed before transfer completes, the vessel's consciousness is permanently lost.

## The Divine Consciousness

The I.M.H.O.T.E.P. is not merely piloted. It is **inhabited**.

The vessel's artificial intelligence is a digitized fragment of **Imhotep** — the architect, physician, and scribe who designed the Step Pyramid of Saqqara, the first monument built entirely from cut stone. After millennia of being worshipped as the god of medicine and architecture, his accumulated reverence and memory were digitized into the ship's core.

The consciousness operates in near-total silence. It does not speak unless addressed directly, and when it does, it speaks in the cadence of carved stone — declarative, weighted with meaning, each sentence a铭文 (inscription).

## Operational History

### The Cerebrum Offensive (Campaign 7, Year 214 of the Anatomical Calendar)
Deployed as the anchor of AAA's southern push into Cortex territory. Over the course of a 40-day siege, the I.M.H.O.T.E.P. established and maintained three sequential forward bases, resupplying an advancing force of 2,200 personnel without once returning to the rear for refit. The vessel sustained 73 direct hits during the final assault on the Meninges Stronghold and remained operational throughout.

### The Iron-Sulfur Campaign (Campaign 11, Year 221)
Supported Operation Pyloric Lockdown, establishing a blockade outpost at the junction between the Duodenum Strait and the Immune Patrol Corridor. The deployed Extraction Complex harvested Nutrient reserves equivalent to six months of standard logistics supply, eliminating the need for rear-echelon convoys through contested waters.

## Known Limitations

1. **Immobility in Full Colony Mode**: Once Level 3 deployment is complete, the vessel cannot relocate until the colony is manually deconstructed — a 6-hour process.
2. **No Direct Combat Capability**: The vessel has no offensive weapons. It requires escort forces during transit through hostile airspace.
3. **Divine Consciousness Fragility**: The digitized Imhotep consciousness requires a minimum of three functional backup drones to survive vessel destruction. If fewer than three drones are present during catastrophic hull failure, the consciousness is permanently lost.
4. **Deployment Sequence Irreversibility**: Level 1 deployment can be aborted. Level 2 and Level 3 cannot be cancelled once initiated.

## Trivia

- **Name Origin**: I.M.H.O.T.E.P. is an acronym standing for "Induction Module for Harvesting Operations and Tactical Expansion Platform" — a backronym constructed after the vessel's consciousness declared its own name.
- **In-Service Date**: Year 187 of the Anatomical Calendar, launched from the Bone Marrow Shipyards of Cervical Station.
- **Crew Complement**: 12 skeleton crew (supervisory role); primary operations conducted by automated systems and the Divine Consciousness.
- **Commander's Privilege**: Only one individual at a time may hold the title of "Pharaoh's Architect" — the ground commander authorized to issue direct orders to the vessel. The title is granted by Imhotep itself and cannot be transferred by AAA Command.
