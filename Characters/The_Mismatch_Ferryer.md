## Scientific origin

Karosas A., Saggio M., Stacey W.C., **"Seizure recruitment properties are dependent upon dynamotype: A modeling study,"** *PLOS Computational Biology*, 2026-09-21. DOI: [10.1371/journal.pcbi.1013975](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1013975).

- A Multiclass Epileptor two-node model (autonomous seizure zone + non-epileptic potential propagation zone) ran 86,904 simulations; unlike models that give every region identical local dynamics and vary only excitability/connectivity, the two nodes may start in different regions of parameter space.
- Seizures beginning as a **baseline shift** propagate less often and more slowly; those beginning as **amplitude scaling** also recruit less.
- The propagation zone need not copy the onset dynamics — mismatched recruitment occurs (e.g. no baseline shift at onset, baseline shift at the recruited zone), observed in human intracranial EEG at University of Michigan.
- Evidence boundary: EEG patterns are compatible with bifurcation hypotheses; they do not prove the corresponding mathematical bifurcations occur in real tissue.

## Character concept

A night relay messenger on the terrace between two neural lodges. She never amplifies a wave — she only *reads the crossing*. Two arched bridges span the gap: one whole, one snapped mid-arch with a missing gap. Whether a seizure reaches the next lodge depends not on how loud the horn sounds at the source, but on the type of wave — its dynamotype — and on whether the far bank moves in a matching way. Her lantern makes the rule visible: one signal line enters and forks into a Y, two arms diverging and ending apart, never rejoining. She is a guardian of transmission, calm where others would panic at the first ember.

Name: **错渡者 The Mismatch Ferryer** — 错 = mismatched/offset; 渡 = ferry across. English: mismatch + ferryer.
