## THE REBIRTH

### Historical Background

**Original Life**: Rosalind Elsie Franklin (1920-1958)

**Achievements**:
- Produced **Photo 51** — the X-ray diffraction image that revealed DNA's double helix structure
- Her work was shown to Watson and Crick without her permission
- Watson and Crick won the Nobel Prize (1962) for the DNA structure discovery
- Franklin died of ovarian cancer in 1958, age 37 — four years before the Nobel was awarded
- The Nobel Committee does not award prizes posthumously
- Her contribution was not acknowledged in the original Nobel citation

**The Injustice**:
For decades, Franklin's role was minimized or erased from the DNA discovery narrative. Watson's book "The Double Helix" (1968) portrayed her dismissively. Only later did historians restore her rightful place as one of the most important scientists of the 20th century.

**Her Death**:
Franklin died of ovarian cancer, possibly related to her extensive X-ray crystallography work. She continued working almost until her death, publishing papers from her hospital bed.

### The Divine Intervention

**Khonsu's Offer**:

When Franklin's soul departed, she did not go gently. Her anger at being erased, at watching others receive credit for her work, at dying before she could see justice — this rage resonated across dimensions.

**Khonsu** (Egyptian God of the Moon, Medicine, and Fertility) heard her.

**The Moon God's Domain**:
- **Moon**: Navigation through darkness
- **Medicine**: Healing and protection
- **Fertility**: Creation and continuity of life

**The Covenant**:

Khonsu offered Franklin a choice:
- Enter the afterlife and accept oblivion
- Return as his champion — a knight who protects those who work in darkness, whose contributions are stolen, who die before seeing justice

**Franklin's Choice**:
> "I spent my life illuminating the invisible. I will not fade into darkness now. Let me return. Let me protect those like me. Let me be the moon that guides them through the night."

**The Rebirth**:

Khonsu did not return Franklin as the woman she had been. He returned her as something purer — a girl frozen at the moment before the world could take her. Sixteen years old forever. Small. Unassuming. Eyes that see through everything.

She woke in the cathedral-chapel of the Order of the Atom, wrapped in white bandages that smelled of moonlight, holding a crescent blade she had not yet learned to name. She remembered everything: Photo 51, the betrayal, the hospital bed, the silence. But now she wore it like armor rather than a wound.

She became the **White Moon Crusader Knight Templar** — Khonsu's agent in the mortal realm. The irony was not lost on her: she had died of what she studied. She would now spend eternity studying what could not die.

## APPEARANCE

### Human Form

**Base Appearance**:
- Age: Appears **16** — small, slight frame, ageless in the way of divine things
- Dark hair, unbound, falling past her waist (she cuts it herself when it gets in her way)
- Eyes: Large, dark, perpetually slightly wide — as if seeing through too much
- No radiation scars on her human form — Khonsu's rebirth erased the marks of her death
- Expression: Often blank, then suddenly piercing. She is always watching. Always thinking.

**The First Impression Problem**:
When new recruits meet Franklin, they underestimate her. She is a girl in a white habit. She speaks softly. She tilts her head when confused.

Then the room fills with moonlight.

**Vestments**:
- White habit, deliberately oversized — sleeves that fall past her hands, hem that drags
- Lead-lined scapular with crescent moon clasp (too large for her, perpetually slipping)
- Goggles of revealing (modified, with tiny hieroglyphs at the corners)
- Dosimeter rosary worn as a belt — the beads are oversized, like a child's toy
- When battle-ready: White armor appears from moonlight, sized perfectly to her frame — the only equipment that fits her as if made for her
- Her crescent blade, carried on her back, seems comically large until she draws it

### Avatar Form

**When Khonsu's Power Flows**:
- Grows taller — not by much, but enough to notice
- Eyes become pools of liquid silver
- Ancient Egyptian kohl-style markings trace her face
- White bandages appear around her forearms, her throat, her shins
- Crescent moon symbol on forehead glows ice-blue
- Voice drops an octave — layered, resonant, ancient
- **Duration**: She can maintain Avatar Form for approximately 7 minutes before requiring rest. In contaminated zones, this is often not enough time.

**The Transformation Phrase**:
> *"I am the moon that watches over the night. I am the light that reveals the hidden. I am Khonsu's blade, and I will not be denied."*

**The Girl and the Knight**:
Franklin dislikes her Avatar Form. It makes her feel like she is becoming the thing she fights — the monstrous, the divine, the unkillable. She prefers to operate in human form, using Lunar Vision passively. She saves Avatar Form for emergencies.

> *"I died once. I will not die again. But I also will not become something that cannot remember being human."*

## PERSONALITY

### Core Traits

**Uncompromising**:
Franklin does not tolerate theft of credit, plagiarism, or erasure of contributions. She has been known to appear in the dreams of scientists who steal others' work, showing them visions of what it feels like to be forgotten.

**Protective**:
She takes the safety of her charges personally. Any radiologist who dies from occupational exposure while under her protection is mourned deeply — and avenged if negligence was involved.

**Bitter but Not Broken**:
The injustice of her original death still burns. But she has channeled that anger into purpose. She is not defined by what was taken from her, but by what she now protects.

**Scientific**:
Despite her divine powers, Franklin remains a scientist at heart. She approaches problems methodically, gathers evidence, and draws conclusions based on data. Her divine interventions are precise, like her crystallography.

### The Weight of Memory

Franklin remembers everything from her original life:
- The excitement of capturing Photo 51
- The shock of learning her data had been shared without permission
- The pride of her other discoveries (RNA, viruses, coal)
- The pain of ovarian cancer
- The fading of her strength in the hospital
- The final thoughts: "Did I matter? Will they remember?"

**The Answer**:
Now she knows. She matters. She is remembered. But that memory came too late for her original life — so she ensures it comes in time for others.

### With Yosemite — The Girl She Saved, The Mother She Chose

**Not Blood, But Chosen**:

Franklin has no family. She was reborn into Khonsu's service; the goddess takes what comes before. She does not speak of her original family, her parents, whether she had siblings in her first life.

But she has Yosemite.

Yosemite is not her mother in the biological sense. They share no blood. Franklin saved her when Yosemite was sixteen — the same age Franklin appears to be, the same age Franklin was when she died. In some ways they are mirrors: both young women caught in the fire, both chosen by it, both carrying its marks.

**The Paradox**:

Yosemite is Franklin's junior by nearly forty years of calendar time. Franklin was reborn in the 20th century; Yosemite was born in the SoMe universe's contemporary era. By the calendar, Franklin is the elder. By appearance, they could be sisters. By emotional weight, they are something else entirely.

Franklin calls Yosemite by her name. Yosemite calls Franklin by her title: *"Sister."*

**What Franklin Did For Her**:

> "I carried her out of Fukushima because no one else would. Because she was just a fisherman's daughter. Because no one had come for her. Because I know what it feels like to be left behind." — Franklin

**What Yosemite Did For Her**:

Yosemite gave Franklin something she had not had since her rebirth: *someone who knew her before she was a knight.*

Yosemite was the first person Franklin saved who survived. The first person who looked at her, after the purification, and said: *"You almost died for me."* And Franklin had said: *"Almost doesn't count."*

Yosemite was the first person who saw Franklin not as a weapon, not as a knight, not as Khonsu's avatar — but as a girl who had chosen to be kind when she didn't have to be.

Franklin has never told anyone else this. She does not know how.

**Franklin and Yukio**:

When Yosemite married Dr. Yuan Foo and had her children, Franklin was present at the wedding. She stood at the back, in her oversized habit, and no one quite knew who she was.

Akio and Yukio grew up knowing their mother had a friend — a young woman who appeared occasionally at birthdays and disappeared again. She brought Yukio books about crystallography. She brought Akio a small silver ankh that he still wears.

They called her *"Aunt Franklin."*

Franklin accepted this. She had never been anyone's aunt. She found she liked it.

**The Irony She Does Not Mention**:

When Yukio was five, Franklin visited and Yukio tugged on her habit and said: *"You look like a big sister, not an aunt."*

When Yukio was fifteen, Franklin visited and Yukio had grown taller than her. Franklin looked up and said nothing. Yukio laughed and said: *"I'm taller than you now."*

When Yukio was twenty-three, Franklin visited and Yukio was a Chief Medical Advisor with a published paper on nuclear reprogramming. Franklin sat on a chair with her feet not quite touching the floor, and Yukio poured tea and called her *"Aunt Franklin,"* and neither of them mentioned that Franklin looked exactly the same as she had when Yukio was five.

Franklin does not age. Yukio does. This is the shape of time for both of them, and they have agreed, without ever saying it aloud, not to count it.

**The Call**:

When Yukio's medical career brought her to Alopes, she received a call from a number she did not recognize.

> **Franklin** *(on the phone, voice very quiet)*: "Yukio. I heard what you're working on. The reprogramming research. Come to the Cathedral when you have time."

> **Yukio**: "Franklin? Is something wrong?"

> **Franklin**: "No. I just want to see you. I want to see who you've become."

*(Pause)*

> **Franklin**: "Your mother showed me who you were. I'd like to meet who you are."

### The Girl from the Fishing Village — How Franklin Found Her Mother

On the third day of the Fukushima evacuation, Franklin found her.

She was in a collapsed storage hut near the temple's outer perimeter — not a temple worker, not a priestess. A civilian. A fisherman's daughter, visiting the affluent foster family she had been sold to years before. Sixteen years old. So heavily contaminated that her skin glowed faintly in the dark.

Most soldiers would have left her. The radiation dose alone to enter that room and carry her out would have been fatal to an ordinary person.

Franklin was not an ordinary person.

She wrapped the girl in her own habit and carried her out through twelve contaminated zones, using Healing Moonlight continuously to purge the radiation from the girl's body. The effort nearly killed her. She collapsed at the evacuation point, the girl still in her arms.

The girl's name, she learned, was **Yosemite** — a nickname from the fishermen who had praised her beauty since childhood. She had refused to evacuate. She had gone back to save her cat.

She had almost died for a cat.

Franklin stayed with her for three weeks in the medical tent, purifying the residual contamination from her body. On the day Yosemite could stand without support, Franklin looked at her and made a decision that was not in any mission brief.

> *"You cannot go back. The foster family is gone. The geisha house won't take you back. You have nowhere to go now."*

Yosemite looked up at the small girl in the white habit — the girl who had walked through fire for a stranger, who had almost died carrying her out, who looked young enough to be her younger sister but carried eyes that had seen centuries.

> *"I have nowhere to go,"* Yosemite said.

Franklin did not say: *"Neither do I."*

Instead, she said: *"Come with me. I know someone who takes in strays."*

### The Audience with Grand Duchess Victoria

Franklin did not go to Victoria as a supplicant. She went as Khonsu's knight-commander, still wearing the ash-stained habit, the girl's file in one hand and a letter of introduction from the Order of the Ashborn in the other.

Victoria received them in her study — a small, cluttered room full of books and maps and the smell of pipe tobacco. She looked at the contamination records Franklin presented. She looked at Yosemite, who stood very still, trying not to show how frightened she was.

> **Victoria**: "You want me to take in a Fukushima survivor. A fisherman's daughter. No training, no connections, no papers."

> **Franklin**: "Yes."

> **Victoria**: "Why me? There are shelters. There are resettlement programs."

> **Franklin**: *(pause)* "Because she reminds me of someone who needed someone. And no one was there."

*(This was the first time Franklin had ever spoken about her original life. Victoria heard the weight in those words. She did not ask what Franklin meant.)*

Victoria studied Yosemite for a long moment.

> **Victoria**: "Can you read?"

> **Yosemite**: "A little."

> **Victoria**: "Can you learn?"

> **Yosemite**: *(without hesitation)* "Yes."

> **Victoria**: *(to Franklin)* "I'll take her. But I don't do charity. She'll work. She'll learn. And one day, she'll owe me nothing — because I'm not buying her. I'm teaching her. There's a difference."

Franklin nodded. It was, she thought, the most Victoria thing Victoria had ever said.

> **Franklin**: "Thank you."

> **Victoria**: "Don't thank me. You carried her out of hell. I'm just giving her a door."

Franklin never forgot the girl she had saved — the girl who was old enough to be her sister, who had almost died for a cat, who had looked at her in the evacuation tent and said: *"You're just a child."*

> *"I know,"* Franklin had said. *"I'm always a child. That means I haven't run out of time yet."*

## THE APOCALYPSE OF FRANKLIN — Dungeon Gameplay

### Overview

*The player does not fight Franklin. The player fights alongside her.*

Franklin is the **story anchor** of the Temple of Prometheus arc. She operates from her church — the **Cathedral of the White Moon** — a fusion of radiology facility and sacred shrine built within a decommissioned temple. The player first encounters her here, before the gates of the Contaminated Zones.

The Apocalypse of Franklin is a **narrative dungeon-crawler system** where players collect fragments of testimony from destroyed temples, piece together the complete story, and ultimately confront the truth of the Ancient One.

---

### The Cathedral of the White Moon — Hub Area

**Location**: Built within the shell of a decommissioned temple on the edge of Continent Sacrum (Alopes territory)

**Atmosphere**: A radiology cathedral repurposed as a living memorial. The old imaging gantry still stands in the center — its tubes cold, its screens dark. Franklin has placed offerings on the altar: dosimeter beads, crystallography prints, fragments of Photo 51 blown up to poster size.

**NPCs**:
- **Sister Franklin** — The dungeon guide. She sits at the old control console, reading. She does not look up when the player enters.
- **Brother Cobalt** — An elderly Ashborn monk who maintains the cathedral's systems. He provides equipment upgrades.
- **The Ash Choir** — Ghostly apparitions of those who died in temple breaches. They hum rather than speak. Occasionally, a fragment of their testimony can be transcribed.

**Available Actions**:
- **Read the Apocalypse** — View collected Revelation Fragments. Some entries are locked until prerequisites are met.
- **Review Mission Logs** — Franklin's handwritten notes on each Contaminated Zone. Reading them grants lore and hints.
- **Request Temple Briefing** — Franklin describes the target zone. She speaks softly. She knows too much.
- **Enter the Field** — Begin dungeon descent.

---

### The Apocalypse — Narrative Collection System

The **Apocalypse of Franklin** is a fragmented scripture. It is not a single book — it is hundreds of pages scattered across the Contaminated Zones, torn, burned, buried, half-melted by radiation.

Each dungeon yields **Revelation Fragments** (启示录残页) — pages that tell the stories of those who died when the temples fell. Nuclear workers. Clean-up crews. Families who lived nearby. Children from the evacuation. A security guard who stayed behind. A priestess who refused to leave.

**Fragment Types**:

| Fragment Type | Source | Content |
|--------------|--------|---------|
| **Testimony Page** (证言页) | Primary NPC dialogues in dungeons | Direct first-person accounts from the dead |
| **Technical Log** (技术日志) | Terminals, equipment screens | Engineering records of what went wrong |
| **Last Letter** (遗书) | Personal effects, corpses | Letters to family never sent |
| **Priest Record** (神官记录) | Temple archives | Theological documents, containment protocols |
| **Medical Chart** (病历表) | Hazmat suits, med-bays | Radiation dose records of casualties |
| **Photograph** (照片) | Lockers, offices, wallets | Images of the people before |

**Fragment Quality**:
- **Fragmentary** (残缺) — Burned, water-damaged, partially legible
- **Preserved** (完好) — Found in shielded containers, mostly legible
- **Complete** (完整) — Rare. Found only in deep containment areas

**Collecting and Assembling**:
- Fragments must be physically picked up in dungeons (interact with corpses, terminals, lockers, desks)
- Fragments are added to the Apocalypse log in the player's inventory
- Matching fragments (e.g., pages from the same diary) can be **assembled** at the Cathedral to form complete documents
- Complete documents unlock new lore entries and occasionally mechanical upgrades

### Dungeon Structure — Temple Breach Zones

Each Contaminated Zone is a dungeon with the following structure:

#### Zone 1: The Outer Warning (外围警戒区)

**Terrain**: Barbed wire fences, abandoned guard posts, dosimeter stations, rusting vehicles  
**Enemies**: **Radiation Wisps** (辐射幽灵) — small, gaseous entities that drift through the air. Contact causes radiation sickness. Cannot be killed by conventional weapons.

**Notable Encounters**:
- Guard post: Terminal with shift log — name of the guard on duty when the breach began
- Rusted truck: Photo fragment tucked in a glove compartment
- Dosimeter station: Reading shows "EXTREME" — a ghost stands nearby, still checking their badge

**Collectibles**: Testimony pages from guards and workers who first reported the anomaly

#### Zone 2: The Contaminated Vestibule (污染前厅)

**Terrain**: Decontamination showers (never used), changing rooms with open lockers, break room with cold coffee, bulletin board with family photos  
**Enemies**: **Blazing Specters** (炽魂) — humanoid fire entities. The ghosts of those who died in the initial breach. Aggressive. Their flames carry radiation.

**Notable Encounters**:
- Locker: A Hazmat suit with a name tag and a letter in the pocket. The letter is to a daughter.
- Break room: A photograph on the refrigerator: all the workers posing for a birthday celebration. Three of them are now Blazing Specters roaming this very room.
- Decontamination log: The last entries show escalating readings. Then nothing.

**Collectibles**: Last letters, medical charts showing radiation doses, photographs

#### Zone 3: The Core Corridor (核心走廊)

**Terrain**: Long hallways lined with lead-shielded doors, many hanging open. Old warning signs. Emergency lighting that still flickers. The hum of the dormant Core in the distance.  
**Enemies**: **Chained Remnants** (锁魂) — former priests, partially merged with radiation. They patrol their posts even in death. They do not attack immediately — they speak first. Some ask for help. Some accuse.

**Notable Encounters**:
- A Remnant priest stands at a sealed door. The door is labeled "CONTAINMENT BREACH — DO NOT ENTER." The priest says: *"I sealed it. I sealed it. Did I seal it in time? Did I seal it in time?"*
- A Remnant engineer sits at a control panel, still typing. The screen shows emergency override commands. The commands were never sent.

**Collectibles**: Priest records, technical logs, fragments of the containment ceremony ritual

#### Zone 4: The Promethean Core Chamber (普罗米修斯核心室) — Boss Arena

**Terrain**: A vast circular chamber. The Core floats at the center — not fully awakened, pulsing with sick light. The containment rings are shattered. Shattered Aspects circle it in formation. The air tastes like ozone and ash.  
**Boss**: **The Uncontained Core Aspect** — The partially-awakened fragment of the Ancient One. A shifting mass of light, heat, and geometry that defies description. It does not have a health bar in the traditional sense. You cannot destroy it. You can only **seal it**.

**Phase 1 — Survive**: The Core Aspect projects waves of radiation. Dodge. Use Franklin's Lunar Vision to identify safe zones.  
**Phase 2 — Find the Seal**: The Aspect reveals a containment sigil — a circle of words in a language no living person can read. Franklin translates: *"The seal is not a thing. It is a memory. Find it."*  
**Phase 3 — The Testimony**: In the center of the chamber, a terminal holds the **Final Testimony** — the last recording made before containment failed. Listen to it. All players must stay alive while it plays (approximately 3 minutes).  
**Phase 4 — Seal**: Upon completion, the Apocalypse Fragment for this zone crystallizes from the Core itself — the most complete page you will ever find.

**Franklin's Role in the Boss Fight**:
Franklin enters as an NPC escort for the first completion of each zone. She provides:
- Real-time radiation map overlay (Lunar Vision passive)
- Healing Moonlight for radiation sickness
- Warning callouts for Blazing Specter ambushes
- The translation of the containment sigil
- A short dialogue at the end: *"They died trying to contain it. Now you know what they were trying to contain. Now you decide whether to tell anyone."*

**Subsequent Runs**: After the first completion, Franklin does not accompany the player. The dungeon is now a challenge run. She waits at the Cathedral, reading.
