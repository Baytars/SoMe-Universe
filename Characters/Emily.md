## BACKGROUND

### Early Life

**Birth**: 2332, Phoenix, Duchy of Alopes (age 20 at time of file)

**Family Origin**:
Emily was born to civilian parents residing in Phoenix, Alopes's second-largest city and national aviation hub. Her father worked as a flight instructor at the Alopes Air Force Academy; her mother was a civilian medical technician at Phoenix Regional Hospital.

**Childhood**:
- Grew up in a loving family environment near the Air Force Academy
- Excelled in her studies (homeschooled through age 16)
- Dreamed of becoming a pilot like her father
- Known for her cheerful, optimistic personality

**Pre-War Life**:
At the time of the Umin Escalation, Emily was preparing to enroll in the Air Force Academy. She had completed preliminary flight training and was regarded as a promising candidate.

---

### The Phoenix Occupation (2341-2343)

**Fall of Phoenix**:
During the initial Umin offensive in late 2341, Phoenix fell to enemy forces. The city's strategic value as an aviation hub made it a primary target. Emily's parents were among thousands of civilians captured during the occupation.

**Parental Fate — CLASSIFIED**:
- **Father**: Last confirmed location — Umin detention facility, status unknown
- **Mother**: Last confirmed location — Phoenix Medical Center (Umin-controlled), status unknown
- Emily has received no confirmation of their survival or death

**Emily's Escape**:
Emily narrowly escaped the initial capture by hiding in a maintenance tunnel near the Air Force Academy. She eventually made her way to Alopesian resistance forces operating in the Phoenix outskirts.

---

### The Transformation

**Volunteering for Enhancement**:
In early 2342, at age 19, Emily volunteered for the Jackalope Guard's **Emergency Augmentation Program** — a controversial protocol designed to rapidly convert civilians into combat-capable soldiers.

**The Procedure**:
The augmentation process involved:
- Neural enhancement for accelerated combat training
- Muscular-skeletal reinforcement for heavy weapon operation
- Enhanced reflex systems for close-quarters combat

**The Risk**:
Emily was informed that the procedure carried a **15% risk of permanent neural pattern corruption** — effectively memory deletion or personality alteration. She signed the consent forms without hesitation.

**Her Statement** (from consent interview):
> "I don't care what happens to me. My parents are out there somewhere. If I have to forget who I am to save them, I'll become someone new. At least that someone will be fighting for them."

**Outcome**:
Emily survived the procedure with her memories and personality intact — a statistically unlikely success that some medical officers attribute to her exceptionally strong emotional core.

**Mission Designation**: Liberation of Phoenix Municipal Hall

**Unit**: Rapid Response Team assigned to Lt. Roi Hytar

**Key Actions**:
1. **Orbital Insertion**: Deployed via drop pod from AAA helicarrier, penetrated enemy airspace through thunderstorm cover
2. **Air-to-Ground Kill**: Destroyed an MRSA Excavator (Uber Unit) during pod descent — first documented "pod-to-uber-unit kill" in Jackalope Guard history
3. **Ground Assault**: Engaged Pneumoniae Panzergruppe with antibiotic missile barrage (Penicillin → Fluoroquinolone → Cephalosporin → Macrolide → Tetracycline sequence)
4. **Objective Capture**: Cleared enemy positions from municipal hall, raised Jaunty Jackalope banner over liberated building

**After-Action Report** (Lt. Roi Hytar):

Her morale was... noticeably high. Most soldiers in their first major engagement are terrified. Emily was *happy*. I don't know if that's courage or something the augmentation did to her. But it worked."

## PERSONALITY

### Core Traits

**Outward Demeanor**:
Emily presents as cheerful, optimistic, and energetic — almost inappropriately so for a combat soldier. Her squadmates describe her as "the kind of person who smiles while loading a rocket launcher."

Beneath her sunny exterior, Emily is driven by a fierce determination to find her parents. This motivation borders on obsession but has not impaired her combat effectiveness.

In battle, Emily displays a "joyous aggression" — she enjoys fighting, particularly against Umin forces. This trait worries some officers but has produced exceptional results.
